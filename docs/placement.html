Placement diagnostics

Take these over two days before Day 1, in the order given, under test conditions: no notes, no help, timed. Their purpose is to find the right starting point, not to grade. Skip a subject's later sections when the earlier ones go badly; stop any section where you are guessing. Mark everything with the placement answer key, which also says how to route each result.

Day A: Mathematics (90 min) · Writing (60 min) · Latin (20 min) · Physics (40 min) · Atelier (120 min) Day B: Science (30 min) · History & literature (45 min) · Modern language (20 min) · Music (20 min)

Mathematics (90 min)

Section A: algebra fundamentals 1. Solve 3(x − 2) = 2x + 5. 2. Solve the system 2x + y = 7, x − y = 2. 3. Simplify (2a³b)² / (4a²b). 4. Expand (2x − 3)².

Section B: algebra II 5. Solve x² + x − 12 = 0. 6. Solve 2ˣ⁺¹ = 32. 7. Evaluate log₂ 8 + log₃ (1/9). 8. Simplify √50 − √18.

Section C: geometry 9. A right triangle has legs 5 and 12. Find the hypotenuse. 10. Two angles of a triangle are 35° and 75°. Find the third. 11. Two similar triangles have sides in the ratio 2 : 3. The smaller has area 8. Find the area of the larger. 12. Prove that the diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other.

Section D: trigonometry and functions 13. Give exact values of sin 30°, cos 60°, tan 45°. 14. Solve sin x = √3/2 for 0° ≤ x < 360°. 15. f(x) = (x − 1)/(x + 2). State its domain and find f⁻¹(x). 16. Find the sum 1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + … (forever).

Section E: calculus 17. Find the limit of (x² − 4)/(x − 2) as x → 2. 18. Differentiate x³ − 5x. 19. Find the slope of the tangent to y = x² at x = 3. 20. Evaluate the integral of x² from 0 to 1.

Section F: modeling (bar model or algebra, your choice) 21. A gallery sold 40% of its prints on Saturday and 1/3 of the rest on Sunday. 120 prints remained. How many were there at first? 22. The ratio of red to blue tiles is 3 : 5. After 24 red tiles are added, the ratio is 1 : 1. How many blue tiles are there?

Writing (60 min)

  1. (10 min) Combine into one well-built sentence: The painting was damaged. It was in a flood. The flood was in 1966. The painting was restored over many years.
  2. (5 min) Identify and fix: (a) Because the light changed. (b) The cast was white the shadows were blue. (c) Running to the studio, the easel fell over.
  3. (45 min) Essay of about 400 words: Is it better to master the rules of an art before breaking them? Take a position, support it with specific examples, and address the other side.

Latin (20 min)

If you have never studied Latin, write "none" and skip to Physics: you begin at Capitulum I. 1. Translate: Rōma in Italiā est. Italia nōn est insula. Sicilia et Sardinia insulae sunt. 2. Translate: Dominus servum vocat, sed servus dominum nōn audit, quia dormit. 3. Translate: Puerī in hortō sunt. Puer rosam puellae dat. 4. Give the genitive plural and accusative plural of servus, rosa, oppidum. 5. Translate: Marcus librum legit quod magister eum iussit.

Physics (40 min)

Conceptual (choose one answer and give a one-line reason): 1. A ball rolls off a table. Neglecting air resistance, while it falls its horizontal velocity: (A) decreases (B) stays constant (C) increases. 2. A book rests on a table. The table's upward force on the book is the "reaction" to: (A) the book's weight (B) the book's downward push on the table (C) gravity pulling the table. 3. A coin is tossed straight up. While it is rising, the net force on it is: (A) up (B) down (C) zero. 4. Two identical balls: one falls from height h, the other from 2h. The second reaches the ground with: (A) twice the speed (B) √2 times the speed (C) four times the speed. 5. A car goes around a curve at constant speed. Its acceleration is: (A) zero (B) toward the center of the curve (C) outward.

Quantitative: 6. A cyclist accelerates from 2 m/s to 8 m/s in 3 s. Find the acceleration and the distance covered. 7. A 5 kg block is pulled with 30 N across a floor with 10 N of friction. Find the acceleration. 8. A 0.5 kg ball moving at 6 m/s is caught and stopped. What was its kinetic energy?

Atelier (120 min)

Set out drawing materials as described on Day 1. 1. (45 min) Copy any reproduction of a Bargue plate, or of a master drawing of a head, in sight-size if possible. Block-in and contour only. 2. (20 min) A nine-step value scale from white to the darkest dark you can make. 3. (40 min) A still life of an egg and a box under one lamp, in pencil, with full value. 4. (15 min) Write a paragraph: which artists do you most admire, what do you most want to be able to do in two years, and what media have you used (oil, acrylic, watercolour, charcoal, digital)?

Science (30 min)

  1. Balance: C₃H₈ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O.
  2. How many moles are in 36 g of water (H₂O)? (H = 1, O = 16)
  3. Which gas makes up most of Earth's atmosphere?
  4. A gas at 300 K is heated to 600 K at constant pressure. What happens to its volume?
  5. What is the role of chlorophyll in a leaf?
  6. Why does salt dissolve in water but not in oil? (One or two sentences.)

History & literature (45 min)

Read this passage (Abraham Lincoln, Gettysburg Address, 19 November 1863, public domain):

Four score and seven years ago our fathers brought forth on this continent, a new nation, conceived in Liberty, and dedicated to the proposition that all men are created equal. Now we are engaged in a great civil war, testing whether that nation, or any nation so conceived and so dedicated, can long endure.

  1. Sourcing: who, when, where, to whom, and why?
  2. "Four score and seven years ago" refers to which year and which document?
  3. What does "proposition" suggest: that equality is a settled fact, or a claim being tested? Use the second sentence.
  4. List up to ten substantial books (fiction, poetry, history, philosophy) you have read in full in the last two years, with one sentence each on what you remember.

Modern language (20 min)

If you have not studied Spanish (or your chosen language), write "none": you begin at Day 1. Otherwise: 1. Conjugate ser, estar, tener, ir in the present. 2. Translate: Ayer fuimos al museo y vimos cuadros de Velázquez. 3. Write five sentences about your plans for next summer.

Music (20 min)

  1. Name the notes on the lines of the treble staff and of the bass staff.
  2. Write the scale of D major.
  3. How many half steps are in a perfect fifth? In a major third?
  4. Spell the triads (three-note chords) of C major and A minor.
