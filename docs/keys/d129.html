Latin · Sequor and ēgredior, a second mixed practice

Check. 1. Sequeris, you (s.) follow; sequimur, we follow; ēgrederis, you (s.) go out; ēgrediuntur, they go out. 2. A fair pair: Puerī sequuntur. Vōs ēgrediminī. 3. Locūtus erat is the pluperfect of loquor, he had spoken. Sequor’s taught forms today are present only.

Exercises. 1. I follow, and you go out. They go out (Puerī ēgrediuntur). 2. Ēgredior, ēgrederis, ēgreditur, ēgredimur, ēgrediminī, ēgrediuntur. 3. A pluperfect, or any other tense, of sequor remains reserved.

Mathematics · Geometry LXXIV: signs of sine and cosine in the four quadrants, a first look

Check. 1. I: both +. II: sine +, cosine −. III: both −. IV: sine −, cosine +. 2. P is (−√3/2, 1/2). Sin 150° = 1/2, cos 150° = −√3/2. The second quadrant makes x, and therefore cosine, negative; y, and therefore sine, stays positive. 3. P is (√3/2, −1/2). Sin 330° = −1/2, cos 330° = √3/2. −1/2 is the sine, the y-coordinate, not the cosine. 4. Model: signs of sine and cosine in the four quadrants. Own words should match this idea: On the unit circle, cosine is the x-coordinate and sine is the y-coordinate, so their signs follow the signs of x and y. In the first quadrant both are positive. In the second, sine is positive and cosine is negative. In the third, both are negative. In the fourth, sine is negative and cosine is positive. A reference angle in the first quadrant gives the size of the coordinates; the quadrant gives the signs. Near-miss: Not a new set of absolute values for 30°, 45°, and 60°. Not a first look at radians. Not a claim that sine is positive in every quadrant. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. 150° has reference angle 30°. 210° is 180° + 30°, so the reference angle is 30°. In the third quadrant both signs are negative: sin 210° = −1/2, cos 210° = −√3/2. 2. The third quadrant. 3. Radians remain reserved.

Writing · A second short narrative, still without a moral

A stated moral at the end. 3. A narrative may be invented, but it must be labeled as invented if it is not a retelling of a set source. An official happening is not being certified here.

Exercises. 1. Complete action in order: jar, draft, sheet, jar closed. Time and place: noon, a desk. No moral. It passes. 2. A précis restates a source already written. Today’s job is a short narrative, usually invented, not a summary of the bull. 3. Chreia and maxim remain reserved.

Science · Ultramarine, the blue from lapis lazuli, a first look

Check. 1. The blue pigment prepared from lapis lazuli by grinding the rock and separating the blue lazurite from the other minerals. 2. The rock is lapis lazuli, which contains lazurite and other minerals. Lazurite is the blue mineral. Ultramarine is the prepared pigment, not the unprocessed rock. 3. A detailed historical recipe, a synthetic process, and a fifth palette tube. Invent no official yield, price, or mine output. The atelier stays with the four-paint limited palette this week. 4. Model: ultramarine (from lapis lazuli). Own words should match this idea: Ultramarine, in this first look, is the blue pigment prepared from lapis lazuli by separating the blue lazurite from the other minerals in the rock, after the rock has been ground. It is not the rock itself, and it is not a new official catalog color added to this week’s atelier palette. Invent no official historical price, and no official extraction yield. Near-miss: Not lapis lazuli the rock, Tuesday’s term. Not hematite, and not a fifth tube in the limited palette, which remains four paints. Not a detailed official workshop recipe invented from memory, and not a synthetic modern process, which remains reserved. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. No set primary recipe is being used today, so a detailed official workshop method would be invented. The outline — grind, then separate the blue — is enough for this first look. 2. Ochre was an earthy iron-oxide pigment from a mineral earth. Ultramarine is a blue prepared from a rock that contains lazurite. Both move from a mineral source to a paint, by different paths. 3. Yes, that would be a miss. The rock is lapis lazuli; ultramarine is the pigment prepared from it.

Modern language · Spanish: regular and irregular futures together

Check. 1. A regular future keeps the whole infinitive; an irregular one keeps the same endings on a changed stem. Hablaré, I will speak; haré, I will do or make. 2. A fair trio: Mañana hablaré con ella, invented. Mañana lo haré. Mañana tendremos tiempo, invented, not an official schedule. 3. They would treat hacer and tener as regular in this tense. Conditional and subjunctive remain reserved.

Exercises. 1. Tomorrow we will go. She will go out, or she will leave. We will want to see it, invented. You (tú) will know it. 2. They will say or tell; I will put or place; we will come. 3. Hablaré; haré; tendré.

Seminar · Economics: an income tax on invented figures, distinguished from a sales tax

Check. 1. A named percent taken from an invented amount of income, not added to a purchase price. Last week’s sales tax was a percent added to an invented price. 2. The tax is $50; $450 remains. $550 adds the tax to the income, as if it were a sales tax. 3. The figures are invented classroom numbers. No official federal, state, or local rate is being taught, and no living person’s tax is being claimed. 4. Model: income tax (invented figures). Own words should match this idea: An income tax, in this first look, is a named percent taken from an invented amount of income, not added to a purchase price. The figures are invented classroom numbers, not an official living tax rate. If the invented income is $500 and the invented rate is 10 percent, the tax is $50, and $450 remains. Near-miss: Not last week’s sales tax, which was a percent added to an invented purchase price (5 percent of $80 was $4, total $84). Not an official federal, state, or local rate. Not a claim about a living person’s tax. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. Income tax $20, remaining $180. A 10 percent sales tax on a $200 price would be $20, total $220. 2. The $4 was added to the $80 to make a total of $84, which is a tax on a purchase, not an amount taken from income. 3. Withholding, deductions, and official published rates.
