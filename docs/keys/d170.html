Modern language · Spanish: irregular participles, a short weekly mix

Check. 1. He, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han puesto. I have put; they have put, or they have placed. 2. *Ponido is not the participle of poner; visto is the participle of ver, not of poner. 3. Ha is the helping form. Hay means there is, or there are. Haya is “that there be.”
