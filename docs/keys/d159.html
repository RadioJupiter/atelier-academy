Science · Official Example 20.7, hot and cold power of a headlight, from OpenStax 20.4

Check. 1. P = 2.50 × 12.0 = 30.0 W, official. 2. P = 144 / 0.350 = 411 W, official. I = 12.0 / 0.350 = 34.3 A, official. 3. Official household 120 V remains reserved as a first look of its own. Today is official Example 20.7, with the official 12.0 V of the headlight. 4. Model: official Example 20.7, hot and cold power of a headlight. Own words should match this idea: OpenStax, College Physics 2e, official Example 20.7, uses the already-taught official headlight figures. For the hot headlight, I = 2.50 A and V = 12.0 V, and the book finds P = I V = 30.0 W. For the cold headlight, R = 0.350 Ω and V = 12.0 V, and the book finds P = V² / R = 411 W. The cold current, from I = V / R, is 12.0 / 0.350 = 34.3 A, which is the book’s official current. Those figures are official, not invented. Near-miss: Not an invented headlight, and not official household 120 V, which remains reserved as a first look of its own. Not official utility rates, which remain reserved. Not a replacement of the book’s official 30.0 W, 411 W, or 34.3 A. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. 2.50 × 12.0 = 30.0; 144 / 0.350 = 411; 12.0 / 0.350 = 34.3. 2. From P = I² R, I = √(P / R) = √(411 / 0.350) = 34.3 A, the same official current as I = V / R. 3. Official household 120 V as a first look of its own, and official utility rates, remain reserved.
