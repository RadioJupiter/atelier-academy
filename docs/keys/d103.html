Physics · OpenStax Example 16.7: friction on a mass-spring, and a total distance 1.59 m

Check. 1. 0.157 N, using g = 9.80 m/s² as in the example. 2. 1.59 m. Energy is not conserved, because friction is non-conservative; Wnc = −fd removes energy. 3. Underdamped. It travels a total distance much larger than X, back and forth across equilibrium, as the amplitude falls. 4. 0.0484 m toward equilibrium from the 0.100 m start. It is sourced from the same example’s discussion. 5. Model: OpenStax Example 16.7. Own words should match this idea: OpenStax Example 16.7, in section 16.7, places a 0.200 kg object on a spring of k = 50.0 N/m, released from rest at X = 0.100 m from equilibrium, with simple friction, μk = 0.0800, on a horizontal surface. The frictional force is f = μk mg = 0.157 N, using OpenStax’s 9.80 m/s² in that calculation, not a different official g blended in from another page. The total distance traveled, back and forth, until the motion stops, is d = 1.59 m.
