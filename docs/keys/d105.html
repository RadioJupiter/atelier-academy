Physics · Friday mix: damped motion, and Example 16.7

Check. 1. It removes mechanical energy; Wnc = Δ(KE + PE), and for damping Wnc is negative. 2. Underdamped: overshoots and crosses one or more times. Critically damped: returns as quickly as possible, overshooting at most once. Overdamped: slower than critical, without a train of oscillations. 3. f = 0.157 N, d = 1.59 m, and yes, underdamped. 4. 0.0484 m toward equilibrium from the 0.100 m start. It is sourced from the example’s discussion.

  1. The period is nearly the same as in undamped SHM. The amplitude gradually decreases.
  2. That 9.80 m/s² is the value used to obtain the sourced 0.157 N, and is not mixed with a different official g from another page.
  3. Mechanical energy is no longer constant; Wnc is negative.
  4. Forced oscillations and resonance are the next section, not this week’s first look.

Modern language · Spanish: Friday mix of the imperfect, with the preterite as a contrast

Check. 1. Hablaba, hablabas, hablaba, hablábamos, hablabais, hablaban. Comía, comías, comía, comíamos, comíais, comían. Vivía, vivías, vivía, vivíamos, vivíais, vivían. 2. The -ar imperfect uses -aba. The -er and -ir imperfect uses -ía, and those two conjugations share that set. 3. Sample: Cada mañana yo caminaba. Ayer yo caminé. Or: Cada mañana yo comía fruta. Ayer yo comí fruta. 4. The irregular imperfects of ir, ser, and ver have not been taught. The first-person and third-person singular share one imperfect form; the subject, or the rest of the sentence, tells them apart.

  1. Every morning we used to practice, or we were practicing: imperfect. Yesterday we practiced: preterite.
  2. Leía / leí (I was reading, or I used to read / I read, a completed event). Vivíamos / vivimos (we used to live, or we were living / we lived, in the already-taught preterite).
  3. The imperfect, in this first look, is for ongoing or habitual past action. The preterite is for a completed event.
  4. Those preterites were already taught. This week’s first look is the imperfect.

Vocabulary review

Check. 1. Volume: (1/3)πh(R² + Rr + r²), here 84π cubic units. Total surface: π(R + r)l + πR² + πr², here 90π square units. If l and h are both given, they must satisfy l² = h² + (R − r)²; a pair that does not is not one solid. 2. Loquor, loqueris, loquitur, loquimur, loquiminī, loquuntur. The perfect locūtus sum has not been taught. A perfect-participle absolute is already complete when the main verb happens; a present-participle absolute is contemporaneous with the main verb. 3. Hablaba, hablabas, hablaba, hablábamos, hablabais, hablaban. Vivía, vivías, vivía, vivíamos, vivíais, vivían. In this first look, the imperfect is for ongoing or habitual past action; the preterite is for a completed event. The irregular imperfects of ir, ser, and ver have not been taught. 4. Wnc = Δ(KE + PE), and it is negative for a damping force that removes mechanical energy. Underdamped motion oscillates with a falling amplitude; critically damped motion returns to equilibrium as quickly as possible, overshooting at most once; overdamped motion returns more slowly without oscillating. Example 16.7: f = 0.157 N, total distance 1.59 m, underdamped; if the force is twenty times greater, the mass moves only 0.0484 m toward equilibrium, a sourced contrast, using g = 9.80 m/s² as in that example. 5.
