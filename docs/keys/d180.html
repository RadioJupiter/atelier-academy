Latin · Term 3 examination (purpose and result)

Check. 1. She comes in order that she follow him (purpose). She comes so quickly that she follows him (result). 2. She stays in order that she not follow him (negative purpose). She comes so slowly that she does not follow him (negative result). 3. A negative result uses ut nōn, not nē. Nē marks a negative purpose. 4. Sequātur, sequētur; ēgrediātur, ēgrediētur. Sequētur and ēgrediētur are futures, not the present subjunctive these clauses take. 5. He rises in order that he go out (purpose). He rises so quickly that he goes out (result). He sits in order that he not go out (negative purpose).
