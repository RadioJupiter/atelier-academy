Vocabulary review

Check. 1. x, the identity. The function inverse undoes f; the logic inverse is a statement of the form “if not q, then not p.” 2. It is a later label, not Tacitus’s title. Tacitus says his career began under Vespasian, so he is not a simple eyewitness roll-call. 3. A vertical line can meet the circle at two points, so one x can have two y-values. A function graph assigns one output to each allowed input. 4. Arms and a man (forced by fate and by Juno’s hate, exiled from the Trojan shore). The English is Dryden’s translation, 1697, not Virgil’s Latin. 5. tan… como is “as … as” (equality). más… que is “more … than” (the comparative). 6. The core shadow is on the form, where it turns away from the light. The cast shadow is on the table or the wall. 7. If P then Q; Q; therefore P. A non-square rectangle is a rectangle and not a square. 8. It turns light into an electrical signal in the retina (NEI). We still refuse an invented rod-and-cone count. 9. The third: a minor third (the fifth is still perfect). 10. For example Debo estudiar. Other patterns: ir a, acabar de, or tener que.
