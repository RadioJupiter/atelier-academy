Latin · The pluperfect of ēgredior, a first look

Check. 1. The participle ēgressus plus the imperfect of sum: ēgressus eram, ēgressus erās, ēgressus erat, ēgressī erāmus, ēgressī erātis, ēgressī erant. 2. You (s.) had gone out. She had gone out. We had gone out. 3. Ēgressus eram, I had gone out, is already completed before another past moment. Ēgredior is the present, I go out. Secūtus eram is I had followed.

Exercises. 1. You (pl.) had gone out; they had gone out. 2. Ēgressus eram; ēgressī erant. 3. Locūtus eram is I had spoken, from loquor. The participle of ēgredior is ēgressus.

Mathematics · Geometry LXXVI: the area of a triangle as (1/2)ab sin C, a first look

Check. 1. Area = (1/2)ab sin C, with C the included angle between a and b. When C is 90°, sin 90° = 1, so the area is the already-taught (1/2)ab. 2. 9√3. 3. They are classroom figures, not official measurements of a real object. The law of sines, the law of cosines, and radians remain reserved.

Exercises. 1. Area = (1/2) × 4 × 8 × sin 30° = 16 × (1/2) = 8. 2. Any vertex may be the included angle, with the two sides that meet there. The area is the same number. 3. That would treat the triangle as if it were right-angled, and the height would be wrong.

Writing · A close retelling of the set chreia, then a short comment

Check. 1. Alexander finds Diogenes lying in the sun and asks whether he wanted anything. Diogenes says, “I would have you stand from between me and the sun.” A fair paraphrase and reason: he asks only that the sun not be blocked, which fits a person already content with lying in the sun and asking for no gift. 2. A narrative tells who, what, when, and where, in order, without a required famous saying. A fable has a stated moral, often with animals. Today’s piece stays a short attributed saying on an occasion. 3. Do not invent official facts about Diogenes, or about a living person, beyond the set passage. A full later elaboration, and a maxim, remain reserved.

Exercises. 1. Alexander “told his followers… that if he were not Alexander, he would choose to be Diogenes.” That later remark is a comment in the same passage, not the chreia itself; the chreia is Diogenes’ saying on the occasion. 2. A stated moral would turn it into a fable, which is a different exercise already taught. 3. It is the 1859 wording. Teaching prose may say anything.

Science · Azurite, a blue copper carbonate, a first look

Check. 1. A blue copper carbonate mineral. The USGS record gives Cu3(CO3)2(OH)2. 2. Lapis lazuli is a rock whose blue is lazurite. Ultramarine is the pigment prepared from that rock. Azurite is a different mineral, a copper carbonate. 3. Invent no official copper percentage, and no official price. Malachite, the related green copper carbonate, waits until Thursday. A detailed recipe, and a fifth atelier tube, remain reserved. 4. Model: azurite. Own words should match this idea: Azurite is a blue copper carbonate mineral. The USGS Spectral Library, Clark et al., 2003, Open-File Report 03-395, sample WS316, records azurite with the formula Cu3(CO3)2(OH)2. It is not last week’s lapis lazuli, whose blue comes from lazurite, a sulfur-bearing mineral, not from a copper carbonate. Invent no official copper percentage of a classroom stone, and no official modern price. Near-miss: Not lapis lazuli, and not ultramarine. Not malachite, the related green copper carbonate, which waits until Thursday. Not a fifth tube in the atelier palette, which remains four paints. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. Cu3(CO3)2(OH)2. USGS Spectral Library, Clark et al., 2003, Open-File Report 03-395, sample WS316. 2. The lesson uses the USGS formula, not an assay of a stone in the room. 3. Today the mineral is named and distinguished. A workshop method would be a later, sourced look, not an invented recipe.

Literature · Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 3: the assembly, a first look

Check. 1. Mr. Bingley, his two sisters, the husband of the eldest, and another young man. The report is “of his having ten thousand a year.” 2. Bingley dances every dance and is “lively and unreserved.” Darcy dances only twice, with his own party, and is “discovered to be proud.” “Ten thousand a year” is the novel’s figure, not an official salary. 3. Forms such as “neighbour,” “honour,” and “mein” stay inside quotations. The overheard “tolerable” remark, and Chapter 4, remain reserved until Thursday or later. 4. Model: the assembly (Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 3). Own words should match this idea: In the 1813 wording of Pride and Prejudice, Volume I, Chapter 3, as posted on Wikisource, the Netherfield party enters the assembly: “Mr. Bingley, his two sisters, the husband of the eldest, and another young man.” Mr. Darcy “soon drew the attention of the room by his fine, tall person, handsome features, noble mein; and the report which was in general circulation within five minutes after his entrance, of his having ten thousand a year.” “Ten thousand a year” is the novel’s figure, not an official salary. 1813 forms such as “mein,” “neighbour,” and “honour” stay inside quotations. Near-miss: Not last week’s undisclosed visit in Chapter 2, already taught, used as if it were this assembly. Not an official salary, and not an official identification of a character with a living person. The overheard “tolerable” remark waits until Thursday. Chapter 4 remains reserved. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. That Bingley “wore a blue coat and rode a black horse.” He is “obliged to be in town the following day,” and so cannot accept the invitation. 2. Her dislike is sharpened “by his having slighted one of her daughters.” 3. They are the novel’s words for the household, not a description of a living person, and they stay inside quotation or clearly in the book.

Modern language · Spanish: regular conditionals contrasted with regular futures

Check. 1. Both keep the whole infinitive. The future adds -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -éis, -án; the conditional adds -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían. 2. A fair trio: Mañana hablaré con ella, invented. En tu lugar, hablaría con ella, invented. Nosotros comeríamos más tarde, invented. 3. Hablaré means I will speak. Irregular conditionals such as haría, tendría, and podría wait until tomorrow.

Exercises. 1. Tomorrow I will live here, invented place. They would eat. We would speak. 2. You (tú) would live, conditional; they will eat, future; we would speak, conditional. 3. Viviré or viviría; hablaremos or hablaríamos, according to will or would.

Seminar · Music: rondo form, A–B–A–C–A, a first look

Check. 1. Rondo form is A–B–A–C–A: a first strain, a contrast, a return, a second contrast, and a further return of A. Ternary form is A–B–A only. 2. Four bars of A, 1–3–5–3; four of B, 2–4–6–4; A; four of C, 5–4–3–1; A again. The figure is invented and written only in scale degrees, so no copyrighted tune is being copied. 3. Stopping after A–B–A, or replacing a return with a new strain. A longer rondo, or a varied return of A, remains reserved. 4. Model: rondo form. Own words should match this idea: Rondo form, in this first look, is a musical shape in which a first strain A returns after each contrasting strain: A–B–A–C–A. The returns of A are the same A, or a close written-out repeat of it, not a new strain. The idea is taught on a short invented figure in scale degrees, not on a copyrighted score. Near-miss: Not last week’s ternary form, which is A–B–A only, without a second contrasting strain C and a further return of A. Not binary form, A then B. Not a copyrighted tune quoted as the example. A longer rondo, or a varied return, remains reserved. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. The returning A is a coming-back of the first strain, not a new idea that would make the shape A–B–C–D–E. 2. A period already taught may sit inside a strain, but today’s first look does not require that. 3. The lesson teaches the shape on an invented figure. A copyrighted melody is not the example, and is not to be copied into the exercise.
