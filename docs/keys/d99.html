Mathematics · Geometry LIV: the surface area of a composite solid

Check. 1. Each complete surface still includes the hidden joined face, which is no longer outside. 2. 240 square units. 3. 33π square units. 4. 336 square units. 5. Model: surface area of a composite solid. Own words should match this idea: The surface area of a composite solid is the sum of the areas that can still be seen or touched. A face where two pieces join is hidden and is not counted. That is why you cannot simply add the two solids’ complete surface areas: each complete surface still includes the hidden face. Near-miss: Not yesterday’s volume, which does add the pieces in full. Not a first look at a cube’s surface, or at Monday’s pyramid surface, except as already-taught pieces. Classroom lengths are invented. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

  1. Cylinder wall 2πrh = 60π, plus the exposed base 9π, plus the cone’s lateral 15π, total 84π square units.
  2. Volume counts space, and the joined face is not extra space. Surface counts only the outside, so the joined face is left out of both pieces.
  3. 216 square units. It is a single already-taught prism, not two solids joined.
  4. As in the earlier circle and solid work: exact answers stay in terms of π.

Modern language · Spanish: pedir and dormir, a two-verb preterite contrast

Check. 1. Pedí, pediste, pidió, pedimos, pedisteis, pidieron. Dormí, dormiste, durmió, dormimos, dormisteis, durmieron. 2. Only the third persons. Pedir: e to i. Dormir: o to u. 3. Sample: Ella pidió ayuda. El niño durmió temprano. 4. Those would swap the verbs. Pidió goes with pedir; durmió goes with dormir. 5. Model: pedir and dormir in the preterite, contrasted. Own words should match this idea: Pedir and dormir, in the preterite, share one shape: the stem vowel changes only in the third persons. Pedir goes e to i (pidió, pidieron). Dormir goes o to u (durmió, durmieron). The other forms keep the original vowel: pedí, dormí; pedimos, dormimos. The two vowels must not be swapped. Near-miss: Not a first look at either preterite set, already taught this week. Not a present-tense contrast, already done for pedir on Tuesday. Instruction remains in English. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

  1. Yesterday we asked for (pedimos, e). Last night we slept (dormimos, o). Both keep the original vowel in nosotros.
  2. Pide (present), pidió (preterite). Duerme (present), durmió (preterite).
  3. Pedir’s preterite was Monday; dormir’s was yesterday. Today is the contrast.
  4. Other verbs in the same families, if they have not been taught, remain reserved, rather than being invented from these two models without a lesson.
