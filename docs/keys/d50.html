Vocabulary review

Check. 1. The incircle is tangent to all three sides. The circumcircle passes through the three vertices. They are different circles. 2. Josephus writes that the house was burnt without Caesar's approbation (War VI.4), but in the Life he says Vespasian later housed, honored, and pensioned him, so the sparing-story is a client's claim, not a camera. The two works are the War and the Life (Whiston). 3. Attentive hearers; a man begins from a couch; a queen's command; a sad remembrance of a fate. The English is Dryden's, 1697, not Virgil's Latin. 4. Saber is for a fact, or knowing how (sé). Conocer is for a person or a place (conozco; personal a with a person). 5. Modus ponens: If P then Q; P; therefore Q — valid. Affirming the consequent: If P then Q; Q; therefore P — invalid. A non-square rectangle is the already-taught counterexample to the invalid form. 6. About 1.6 m/s², about one-sixth of 9.8, from the NASA Planetary Fact Sheet and NASA Science's “one-sixth,” with two significant figures respected. 7. Myopia: far-away objects look blurry. Hyperopia: nearby objects look blurry. Neither is a classroom diagnosis. 8. On a cube, value changes at the edges between planes. On a sphere, the turn is curved. 9. It was not finished and dedicated until after Titus's death (CIL VI.945). In 1822 it was taken down and rebuilt by Valadier, so a modern view is not simply the Flavian fabric. 10. The volta is at “Then felt I like some watcher of the skies,” after Chapman is heard. The Cortez comparison is a figure in the poem; this lesson does not treat it as a history class.
