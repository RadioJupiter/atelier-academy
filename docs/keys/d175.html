Latin · Purpose and result, a short weekly mix

Check. 1. Model: purpose ut, negative purpose nē, result ut with tam, negative result ut nōn, each translated and labeled. 2. A negative result uses ut nōn, not nē. 3. Sequātur, sequētur; ēgrediātur, ēgrediētur.

Exercises. 1. Model: the four jobs in original classroom Latin, with translations. 2. A miss: Puella tam celeriter venit ut eum sequētur, because sequētur is the future.

Mathematics · The derivative of invented f(x) = x², a short weekly mix

Check. 1. f'(a) = lim h→0 [f(a + h) − f(a)] / h, when the limit exists. For invented f(x) = x², f'(x) = 2x. 2. 6, 0, and 12. 3. If h were 0, the denominator would be 0. Infinity is not a number to substitute.

Exercises. 1. 18, and 1/2. 2. f(3) = 9 equals the limit of x² as x approaches 3, so f is continuous at 3. f'(3) = 6 is the limit of the difference quotient, a different job.
