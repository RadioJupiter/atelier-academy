Latin · The imperfect of a deponent, a first look

Check. Model: imperfect of a deponent. Own words should match this idea: The imperfect of a deponent is formed on the present stem with the already-taught imperfect endings, and it still looks passive and is translated as active: loquēbar, loquēbāris, loquēbātur, loquēbāmur, loquēbāminī, loquēbantur. The meaning is I was speaking, or I used to speak. Cōnābar, cōnābāris, cōnābātur, and the rest, follow the same pattern: I was trying, or I used to try. Near-miss: Not last week’s perfect locūtus sum, I spoke or I have spoken, which uses the participle plus sum. Not the present loquor, I speak. Not the already-taught absolute puerō locūtō, which has no sum and is not a tense of the verb in a main clause. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. The six forms are loquēbar, loquēbāris, loquēbātur, loquēbāmur, loquēbāminī, loquēbantur. Loquēbar: I was speaking, or I used to speak. Loquēbantur: they were speaking, or used to speak. Ego cōnābar: I was trying, or I used to try. Puer loquēbātur: the boy was speaking, or used to speak. Locūtus est is the perfect, “spoke” or “has spoken,” not the ongoing past.

Exercises. Model: Puer loquēbātur. The boy was speaking. Agricolae cōnābantur. The farmers were trying. Locūtus sum becomes loquēbar. Loquēbantur becomes loquuntur.

Mathematics · Geometry LXIII: tangent of an acute angle, a first look

Check. Model: tangent of an acute angle. Own words should match this idea: In a right triangle, the tangent of an acute angle A, written tan A, is the opposite side over the adjacent side. The ratio is the same for a given angle because two right triangles that share an acute angle also share the right angle, so they are similar by AA, already taught, and corresponding sides are in the same ratio, already taught as a scale factor. Near-miss: Not sine, which is opposite over hypotenuse, and not cosine, which is adjacent over hypotenuse, both already taught last week. Not a length by itself. The radian, the unit circle, and the reciprocal ratios remain reserved. Degrees only. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. Tan A = 3/4. The length 3 is the opposite side, not the ratio. Tan B = 5/12. Using the hypotenuse would give sine, 5/13, not tangent.

Exercises. Tan A = 9/12 = 3/4, the same as in the 3-4-5 triangle. Tan D = 12/5. 12/13 would be sin D, opposite over hypotenuse, not tangent.

Writing · Franklin’s method: hints from Inferno VI.1–57

Check. Model: Franklin hints from Inferno VI.1–57. Own words should match this idea: Franklin’s method, already taught, is used here to make hints from Longfellow’s Inferno VI, lines 1–57, and not to invent a scene the poem does not give. The passage covers the return of consciousness, the third circle of rain, Cerberus, the earth thrown into his gullets, and Ciacco, who names himself and the “pernicious sin of gluttony.” Near-miss: Not a first look at Franklin’s method. Not a reconstruction yet. Not an official historical identification of Ciacco beyond what the poem states. Canto VII remains reserved. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. Rain: “Eternal, maledict, and cold, and heavy,” with hail, sombre-hued water, and snow. Cerberus: three gullets, red eyes, unctuous black beard, claws; he rends the spirits. The Conductor throws earth into the gullets. Ciacco: “You citizens were wont to call me Ciacco”; “the pernicious sin of gluttony.” The poem does not, in this passage, give an official historical identification beyond that name, so inventing one would add a scene or a claim the set text does not give.

Exercises. The Conductor takes earth in his fists and throws it into those rapacious gullets, and the muzzles grow quiet. Lines 58–115, including the divided city and the two who are just, wait until Thursday and must not be smuggled into today’s hints.

Physics · Waves, wavelength, and wave velocity, a first look

Check. Model: wave, wavelength, and wave velocity. Own words should match this idea: In OpenStax College Physics 2e, section 16.9, a wave is a disturbance that propagates, or moves from the place it was created. Wavelength, λ, is the distance between adjacent identical parts of the wave. Wave velocity vw is the speed at which the disturbance moves, which is one wavelength in one period: vw = λ / T, or vw = fλ, since f = 1/T, already taught. This relation holds for all types of waves named in the section. The medium itself need not travel with the disturbance. Near-miss: Not last week’s resonance of a system that stays in place while it is driven. Not a claim that a water wave far from shore pushes the water itself from one place to another; OpenStax warns that the energy moves forward and the water, far from shore, stays in one location aside from a local motion. Superposition and interference remain reserved for a later section. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. A wave is a disturbance that propagates from the place it was created. vw = λ / T = fλ. Example 16.8: vw = 2.00 m/s. Do not invent an official measured speed of sound or of light today; OpenStax names those as kinds of wave, without making those official figures today’s job.

Exercises. vw = 8.0 / 4.0 = 2.0 m/s, and f = 1/4.0 = 0.25 Hz. vw = 12.0 × 0.50 = 6.0 m/s.

History · Dewing, Buildings I.2.17: two further hospices

Check. Model: two further hospices, Dewing I.2.17. Own words should match this idea: Dewing, Buildings I.2.17, in the 1940 Loeb translation posted at LacusCurtius, records that Justinian, “by no means feeling either a surfeit or any sort of weariness in shewing honour to God,” established two other hospices opposite Samson’s, “in the buildings called respectively the House of Isidorus and the House of Arcadius, the Empress Theodora labouring with him in this most holy undertaking.” The passage completes last week’s reserved close and does not give an official income or a room count for these two houses. Near-miss: Not a first look at Samson’s hospice, already taught from I.2.14–16. Not I.2.18–19, which wait until Wednesday. I.3 remains reserved. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. He established two other hospices opposite Samson’s, in the House of Isidorus and the House of Arcadius. “Labouring” is Dewing’s spelling, as “shewing” is, and should not be silently Americanized outside a quotation. Invent no official income, size, or room count. I.2.18–19 are Wednesday’s job, not today’s.

Exercises. “This one” is the hospice of Samson, already rebuilt and endowed in I.2.16. Today stops before I.2.18 because that section begins Dewing’s reason for not cataloguing the other shrines, a new claim to be taught as its own look.

Atelier · The neck as a volume, still monochrome

Check. Model: the neck as a volume. Own words should match this idea: On the monochrome head already placed, the neck is a volume under the jaw, not a stick or a flat strap drawn after the fact. It has a front plane, two side planes, and a back that turns out of sight, and it must be wide enough and set far enough under the already-placed jaw to carry the head. The work remains monochrome. Full color remains reserved for Term 3. Near-miss: Not a first look at the underplane of the jaw, already taught, or at the ears, already paused last week. Not a fully finished throat with every tendon named. Not a reason to begin full color. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. Full color remains reserved for Term 3, so a first pass in color would jump the method. Modern language · Spanish: preterite versus imperfect, a first look

Check. Model: preterite versus imperfect, a first look. Own words should match this idea: The preterite and the imperfect, already taught as forms, are now chosen as two past tenses with different jobs. The preterite presents a past action as completed: hablé, comí, fui, vi. The imperfect presents a past action as ongoing, habitual, or as background: hablaba, comía, iba, era, veía. No new verb forms are taught today. Instruction remains in English. Classroom sentences are invented and do not identify a living person. Near-miss: Not a first look at the preterite forms, or at the regular or irregular imperfect forms, already taught. Not a new irregular paradigm. Vía remains a miss for veía. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. Comer: comí, I ate; comía, I was eating or used to eat. Ir: fui, I went; iba, I was going or used to go. Ver: vi, I saw; veía, I was seeing or used to see. Ayer fui al mercado (completed). Cada mañana iba al mercado (habitual).

Exercises. Model: Ayer vi el cielo. Yesterday I saw the sky. Cada mañana veía el cielo. Each morning I used to see the sky. Ir and ser share the already-taught preterite fui, but their imperfects are different: iba and era.

Seminar · Logic: Datisi, a first look at a third-figure AII

Check. Model: Datisi. Own words should match this idea: Datisi is a valid third-figure mood, AII-3: All M are P; some M are S; therefore some S are P. The middle term is the subject of both premises, as in the already-taught third figure. It is particular in the conclusion because the second premise, and so the conclusion, is particular. It is distinguished from Darapti (AAI-3), already taught, which has two universal premises, from Felapton (EAO-3), and from Ferison (EIO-3). Near-miss: Not a first look at the third figure, or at Darapti, Felapton, or Ferison, already taught. Not first-figure Darii, which has the same vowels but a different figure. Not a claim about an official fact outside the invented terms. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card. All M are P; some M are S; therefore some S are P, third figure. Conclusion: some spades are metal. That is a conclusion from invented classroom terms, not an official fact about tools. “All square things are inked” would be universal, which Datisi does not give; the second premise was only particular.

Exercises. Ferison is EIO-3 and concludes some S are not P. Darii has the vowels AII but is first figure, with a different placement of M, and is not today’s mood. The stamp conclusion is particular, some S are P, not all S are P.
