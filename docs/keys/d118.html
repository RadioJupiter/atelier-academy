Latin · Deponents, the absolute, and AcI, mixed

Check. 1. Ego cōnābar: I was trying (imperfect). Nōs locūtī sumus: we spoke or have spoken (perfect). Puer loquitur: the boy speaks (present). 2. It is an absolute, a loosely attached phrase with no infinitive and no sum. The imperfect “was speaking” is loquēbātur. 3. The accusative is the subject of the reported clause; the infinitive is that clause’s verb.

Exercises. 1. Model: Ego loquēbar. Puer locūtus est. Magister puerum adesse dīcit. 2. Loquuntur, they speak; locūtī sunt, they spoke or have spoken.

Mathematics · Tangent, reviewed, with sine and cosine

Check. 1. Sin A = 3/5, cos A = 4/5, tan A = 3/4, tan(90° − A) = 4/3. 2. 3/5 is sine, opposite over hypotenuse, not tangent. 3. The 6-8-10 triangle is similar, so tan A = 6/8 = 3/4, not a new ratio.

Exercises. 1. Tan D = 12/5, and (12/13)/(5/13) = 12/5. 2. Tan E = 10/24 = 5/12, tan(90° − E) = 24/10 = 12/5.

Writing · Franklin and a précis, mixed

Check. 1. A reconstruction must include the maledict rain, Cerberus quieted with earth, and Ciacco naming himself and the sin of gluttony. It must not add an official historical identification, or the civic speech from line 58. 2. Name the two hospices and the two houses, keep “shewing honour to God” and Theodora “labouring with him,” and record that a catalogue is impossible. 3. Exercises. 1. “You citizens were wont to call me Ciacco.” “Shewing honour to God.” 2. Do not invent an official shrine count, income, or shade count, or an official identification of Ciacco.

Physics · Waves, reviewed

Check. 1. A wave is a disturbance that propagates from the place it was created. vw = λ / T = fλ. 2. vw = 2.00 m/s, the speed of the disturbance, not the gull’s changing vertical speed. 3. Transverse: a guitar string, or visible light. Longitudinal: sound in air or water.

Exercises. 1. vw = 6.0 / 2.0 = 3.0 m/s, f = 0.50 Hz. 2. OpenStax says P-waves and S-waves propagate at different speeds but does not give those official numbers in this section for use here, so a number would be invented.

Atelier · A monochrome head, mixed review

Modern language · Spanish: preterite versus imperfect, reviewed

Check. 1. The preterite presents a past action as completed: ayer comí, ayer fui, ayer vi. The imperfect presents it as ongoing, habitual, or background: cada mañana comía, cada mañana iba, era, veía. 2. Ayer, mientras comía, vi el cielo: while I was eating, I saw the sky. 3. They share the preterite fui, but their imperfects are different: iba and era.

Exercises. 1. Model: Cada mañana iba al mercado. Ayer, mientras iba, vi el cielo. 2. The miss swapped the jobs: mientras already marks a background, so comía, and the seeing, if completed, is vi, not veía.

Seminar · Economics: discounts and a markup, reviewed

Check. 1. The final price is $81. $80 would be a single 20 percent discount off the original, not two successive 10 percent discounts. 2. The final price is $99. $100 would treat the percents as cancelling. $81 was the successive-discount result, with no markup. 3. $200 becomes $220; 25 percent of 220 is 55; 220 − 55 = $165.

Exercises. 1. Successive discounts take 10 percent off $100 and then 10 percent off $90, leaving $81. The markup path adds 10 percent first, then takes 10 percent off $110, leaving $99. 2. The discount is a percent of the current price, so 10 percent of 110 is larger than 10 percent of 100, and the two steps do not cancel.
