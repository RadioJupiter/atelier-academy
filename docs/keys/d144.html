Latin · Sequor and ēgredior, a second mixed practice of perfect, future, present, and pluperfect

Check. 1. You (pl.) followed, or have followed; she had gone out. 2. I shall not go out. The friends have followed me, or followed me. 3. Ēgrediuntur is the present, they go out, not the perfect ēgressī sunt.

Exercises. 1. Sequeris, you follow, present; sequēris, you will follow, future; secūtus es, you followed, or have followed, perfect; secūtus erās, you had followed, pluperfect. Each sentence should keep that time. 2. A fair sentence keeps ēgressus sum, I have gone out, or I went out, completed, and sequētur, he or she will follow, still ahead. 3. Any later deponent tense not yet taught, including a future perfect, remains reserved.

Mathematics · Geometry LXXXVI: radians, arc length, and exact values, a second mixed practice

Check. 1. π, because 90° = π/2 and s = 2 × (π/2) = π. 2. 10π/3; cos(2π/3) = −1/2. 3. 120° is not yet in radians. The formula s = rθ requires θ in radians.

Exercises. 1. 135° = 135 × π/180 = 3π/4. Sin 135° = sin(180° − 45°) = sin 45° = √2/2, so sin(3π/4) = √2/2. 2. θ = (3π)/3 = π radians, which is 180°. 3. The ambiguous case remains reserved. Official measured arcs are not invented.

Science · A simple circuit, a first look, from OpenStax 20.1

Check. 1. A closed path for current, supplied by conducting wires connecting a load to the terminals of a voltage source, such as a battery. Model: a simple circuit. Near-miss: Not Ohm’s law, which remains reserved. Not an official plan of a house. Not a current without a closed path. Not last Tuesday’s official 180 A treated as if it were a new kind of circuit. Exercises. 1. A battery as two parallel lines, conducting wires as straight lines, and the load as a zigzag. 2. Official household voltages remain reserved, and are not in today’s extract as a first look. 3. Ohm’s law, drift velocity, and official household voltages and wiring remain reserved.
