Mathematics · Geometry XXIV: a short mix — a tangent-secant, and an inequality

Check. 1. PA = 3 (81 = PA × 27). 3 < 27, so A is nearer. 2. All real x except 3. 3. Model: a short mix of this week’s algebra and geometry. Own words should match this idea: A sitting that uses Monday’s and Wednesday’s quadratic inequalities and Tuesday’s tangent-secant theorem, each in its own picture, without mixing the theorems into one invented hybrid. Near-miss: Not a new theorem, and not a licence to use PT² = PA × PB on a quadratic’s roots. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

  1. x ≤ 3 or x ≥ 12 (opens upward; include the roots).
  2. III.36 is a relation among lengths in a circle figure. 4 < x < 10 is where a particular quadratic is negative. The jobs are different, even when the numbers look alike.
  3. PB = 12 (36 = 3 × PB); AB = 12 − 3 = 9.
  4. Only x = 5.

Economics · Unit price, in an invented shop

Check. 1. A unit price is the price of one unit, total ÷ count, when the units are the same kind. Profit, already taught, is a leftover after costs — a different job. 2. A comparison of unit prices is a comparison of like with like. Different goods, or a mixed pack treated as if it were uniform, would not be the same job. 3. Model: unit price. Own words should match this idea: The price of one unit, in an invented shop example: total price divided by the number of units (when the units are the same kind). It is not a profit, and not a budget. Invented numbers in this lesson are labeled as invented. Near-miss: Not a profit (already taught as a leftover after costs in an invented shop), and not a claim about a real shop’s ticket. Not a licence to invent a NASA or a historical price. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

  1. $10 ÷ 8 = $1.25 per unit, invented.
  2. Those would pretend a sourced price this lesson does not have. The shop numbers are labeled invented.
  3. A sound sentence: the totals are the same, but the counts differ, so the unit prices differ.
  4. A note that the seminar is not a reward.
