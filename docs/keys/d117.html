Latin · The accusative and infinitive, reviewed

Check. 1. AcI reports a clause with an accusative subject and an infinitive verb. AcI with esse uses esse (or a compound) as that infinitive, already taught. 2. Puer locūtus est is a main-clause perfect deponent, with a nominative subject and a finite verb. 3. Puerō locūtō is an already-taught absolute, a loosely attached phrase, not a reported clause with an infinitive.

Exercises. 1. Model: Magister puerum adesse dīcit. The schoolmaster says that the boy is present. 2. Puerum adesse dīcit: AcI. Puer loquēbātur: finite imperfect deponent. Puerō locūtō: absolute.

Mathematics · Sine and cosine, reviewed

Check. 1. Sin A = 3/5, cos A = 4/5, sin(90° − A) = 4/5. 2. The 6-8-10 triangle is similar, so sin A is still 3/5. 3. 3 is a length, not a ratio. Sine is opposite over hypotenuse.

Exercises. 1. Sin D = 12/13, cos D = 5/13. 2. No official degree measure was given; inventing one is not a calculation from the sides. Degrees only; the unit circle remains reserved.

Writing · A précis of Dewing I.2.17–19, reviewed

Check. 1. Keep the two houses and the two quotations, and end with “It will suffice us to have said thus much.” 2. “Shewing honour to God” and “labouring” are Dewing’s wording and must stay inside quotations. 3. I.3 begins the churches of Mary, a new chapter, and remains reserved.

Exercises. 1. It would drop the two houses and Theodora’s part in the undertaking. 2. An official number of those shrines, or a catalogue Dewing says he will not make.

Science · Heat transfer and conduction, reviewed

Check. 1. Conduction, convection, and radiation. Radiation carries most of the heat into the room. 2. Q/t = kA(T2 − T1)/d. The factors are k, A, (T2 − T1), and d. When T2 = T1, the net rate is zero. 3. Q = 1.15 × 10⁶ J, m = 3.44 kg. Example 14.6 remains reserved.

Exercises. 1. Copper 390 J/s·m·°C; Styrofoam 0.010 J/s·m·°C. 2. Half of 13.3, which is 6.65 J/s.

Literature · Inferno V and VI, reviewed

Check. 1. Rain “Eternal, maledict, and cold, and heavy.” The Conductor throws earth into the rapacious gullets. 2. “You citizens were wont to call me Ciacco.” Do not add an official historical identification. “The just are two, and are not understood there.” 3. They find “Plutus the great enemy.” Canto VII would take him up and remains reserved.

Exercises. 1. “There is no greater sorrow than to be mindful of the happy time in misery,” or “who reason subjugate to appetite.” 2. The poem does not name those two here. An official identification would add a claim the set text does not give.

Atelier · Likeness checks and written pauses, reviewed

Check. 1. A two-side comparison, already taught, applied to the eyes, the ears, and the neck and shoulder turns. 2. What sits, what still fails, and what remains reserved. It should not claim a finished portrait. 3. Full color remains reserved for Term 3.

Exercises. 1. A wrong width or a harsher turn is harder to hide under later accents. 2. The Gray lesson is a separate elective, not a reason to relabel the atelier pause after the fact.

Modern language · Spanish: the imperfect, reviewed

Check. 1. Hablaba, I was speaking or used to speak; comía, I was eating or used to eat; iba, I was going or used to go; era, I was or used to be; veía, I was seeing or used to see. 2. They share the preterite fui, but their imperfects are different: iba and era. 3. Cada mañana veía el cielo. Vía is a miss; veía keeps the stem e.

Exercises. 1. Model: Cada mañana iba al mercado. El mercado era grande. 2. Fui is a preterite, I went or I was (completed), not an imperfect.

Seminar · Music: invented figures, reviewed

Check. 1. A sequence repeats an invented figure from a new starting degree. Do not use a copyrighted tune. 2. F–G–A–C, keeping step, step, skip of a third. 3. Retrograde inversion turns the figure backwards and upside down; a sequence repeats it from a new degree, still facing the same way.

Exercises. 1. G–E–D–C. Invertible counterpoint remains reserved. 2. F–G–B♭, keeping a step and a skip of a third.
