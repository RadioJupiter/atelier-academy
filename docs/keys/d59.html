Latin · A short original reading

Check. 1. It contrasts: the girl laughs, but the boy does not. 2. Puer eam videt.

  1. Puer is nominative (the one who laughs). puellam is accusative (the one seen).
  2. Two copied sentences.

Mathematics · A short Term 1 mix: keep the pictures separate

Check. 1. PB = 27; then 3 < x < 27. 2. No real solution. A square is never negative.

  1. (g ∘ f)(4) = g(7) = 14, which is not 11. Composition is not automatically commutative.
  2. PA = 3 (36 = PA × 12), and 3 < 12, so A is nearer.
  3. That would merge a quadratic inequality with III.36. The numbers may match and the pictures may still be different.

Writing & Rhetoric · A précis of the set extract

  1. Must not drop, for example, the pine likeness or Vesuvius as a later discovery. Must not add Pompeii or a death toll.

Economics · Unit price beside profit

Check. 1. A unit price is the price of one unit, total ÷ count. Profit is a leftover after costs — a different job. 2. $10 ÷ 8 = $1.25 per unit, invented.

  1. A comparison of unit prices is a comparison of like with like. Different goods would not be the same job.
  2. Those would pretend a sourced price this lesson does not have. The shop numbers are labeled invented.
  3. A sound sentence: the totals are the same, but the counts differ, so the unit prices differ.
