Latin · Purpose and result, a short mix

Check. 1. Puella manet nē eum sequātur: she stays in order that she not follow him (negative purpose). Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur: he rises so quickly that he goes out (positive result). 2. It is a negative result, so the negative is ut nōn, not nē. 3. Both clauses take the present subjunctive, sequātur or ēgrediātur, not the future.

Exercises. 1. Model: Puella venit ut eum sequātur; Puella manet nē eum sequātur; Puella tam celeriter venit ut eum sequātur; Puella tam lentē venit ut eum nōn sequātur. 2. Model: Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur, he rises so quickly that he goes out.

Mathematics · Continuity, a tighter mix

Check. 1. Defined at the point, and the limit equal to the value. Invented 2x is continuous at 3; invented (x² − 4)/(x − 2) is not continuous at 2. 2. The function is not defined at 2, and continuity requires a defined value there. 3. Yes. r(0) = 1, and lim x→0 (x + 1) = 1.

Exercises. 1. Yes. (f + g)(3) = 10, and the limit is 10. 2. Not at 4, because k is not defined there, though the limit is 8. Yes at 5: k(5) = 9, and for x ≠ 4, k(x) = x + 4, so the limit at 5 is 9.

Literature · Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 10: “My good opinion once lost is lost for ever”

Check. 1. He has said his temper would perhaps be called resentful, and then: “My good opinion once lost is lost for ever.” 2. “And your defect is a propensity to hate every body.” “And yours, is wilfully to misunderstand them.” 3. They are 1813 forms; unquoted prose in this course uses US spelling (everybody, pianoforte). 4. Model: Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 10: “My good opinion once lost is lost for ever”. Own words should match this idea: Later in Volume I, Chapter 10, in the 1813 wording, Darcy says, “I have faults enough, but they are not, I hope, of understanding.” He continues, “My temper I dare not vouch for.” “My temper would perhaps be called resentful.—My good opinion once lost is lost for ever.” Elizabeth answers, “Implacable resentment is a shade in a character.” She then says, “And your defect is a propensity to hate every body.” He replies, “And yours, is wilfully to misunderstand them.” “Every body” stays inside the quotation. After this, “the piano-forte was opened.” “Piano-forte” stays inside the quotation. Chapter 11 remains reserved. Near-miss: Not Tuesday’s opening of Chapter 10 as if it were new. Not a modernization of “every body” or of “piano-forte.” Not Chapter 11, which remains reserved. The fresh case should use the term correctly and not be copied from the card.

Exercises. 1. Model: after the letter scene, the later exchange on a resentful temper, a lost good opinion, and a defect, with “every body” quoted. 2. Chapter 11 remains reserved.
