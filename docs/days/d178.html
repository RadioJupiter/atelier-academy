Open a card if you need a reminder; then close it and use the term in your own words.

This sitting mixes Monday’s purpose clauses with Tuesday’s result clauses. No new construction. Keep the four jobs distinct, and keep the present subjunctive distinct from the future. Original classroom sentences only.

Remember

Purpose: ut or nē. Result: ut or ut nōn, often with tam, ita, or sic. The verb is the present subjunctive, not the future.

Mathematics · Limits, reviewed

40 min · Term 3 review of already-taught limits and the algebra of limits · invented functions · a formal epsilon argument reserved

Learn

Key terms (from earlier lessons)

  • a limit, a first look — Day 156
  • a limit where the function is not defined at the point — Day 157
  • the limit of a sum, a first look — Day 161
  • the limit of a quotient, a first look — Day 163

Open a card if you need a reminder; then close it and use the term in your own words.

Today reviews already-taught limits. A limit, in the first look already taught, is a value that a quantity approaches as an input approaches a chosen number. On invented f(x) = 2x, lim x→3 2x = 6. A limit may exist even when the function is not defined at the point: invented (x² − 4)/(x − 2) is not defined at 2, but the limit is 4, by factorization. If lim f = L and lim g = M, then lim (f + g) = L + M, lim (f g) = L M, and, if M ≠ 0, lim (f / g) = L / M. On invented f(x) = 2x and g(x) = x + 1, as x approaches 3, those limits are 10, 24, and 3/2. The quotient rule does not apply when M = 0. Continuity and the derivative wait until tomorrow’s review. A formal epsilon argument remains reserved. The functions are invented.

Remember

Lim x→3 2x = 6, on invented f. Lim x→2 (x² − 4)/(x − 2) = 4, though the function is not defined at 2. On invented f(x) = 2x and g(x) = x + 1, as x approaches 3, the sum, product, and quotient limits are 10, 24, and 3/2.

Watch me

State the already-taught limit of invented 2x as x approaches 3, and the already-taught limit of (x² − 4)/(x − 2) as x approaches 2.

  1. Lim x→3 2x = 6.
  2. Lim x→2 (x² − 4)/(x − 2) = 4, by factorization, even though the function is not defined at 2.
  3. The functions are invented. A formal epsilon argument remains reserved.

Together

On invented f(x) = 2x and g(x) = x + 1, as x approaches 3, what are lim (f + g), lim (f g), and lim (f / g)?

Hint 1: The pieces

Lim f = 6, and lim g = 4 ≠ 0.

Hint 2: The rules

The limits are 10, 24, and 3/2.

Check

Write in your own words, then look at the key. The functions are invented.

  1. What is a limit, in the already-taught first look? Give lim x→3 2x.
  2. Why may a limit exist at 2 for invented (x² − 4)/(x − 2), even though the function is not defined there? What is that limit?
  3. On invented f(x) = 2x and g(x) = x + 1, as x approaches 3, give lim (f + g), lim (f g), and lim (f / g).

Exercises

  1. Why does the quotient rule not, by itself, give lim x→2 (x² − 4)/(x − 2)?
  2. On invented p(x) = 3x and q(x) = 1, as x approaches 2, give lim (p + q) and lim (p / q).

Physics · Total internal reflection and official angles, reviewed

40 min · Term 3 review of already-taught total internal reflection, official Example 25.4, official discussion angles, and qualitative fiber optics · no new official figure

Learn

Key terms (from earlier lessons)

  • total internal reflection, and the critical angle, a first look — Day 166
  • official Example 25.4, the critical angle for polystyrene to air — Day 168
  • official discussion angles: water to air, and diamond to air — Day 171

Open a card if you need a reminder; then close it and use the term in your own words.

Today reviews already-taught total internal reflection. The critical angle θc is the incident angle that produces θ₂ = 90°, and θc = sin⁻¹(n₂/n₁) when n₁ > n₂. Official Example 25.4: polystyrene n₁ = 1.49, air n₂ = 1.00, 1.00/1.49 = 0.671, θc = 42.2°, official. Official discussion results: water to air 48.6°, diamond to air 24.4°, flint to crown 66.3°, without invented official flint or crown indices. Light can be guided along a fiber by already-taught total internal reflection, qualitatively, without invented official fiber figures. Official fiber specifications remain reserved.

Remember

Official θc = 42.2° for polystyrene to air. Official discussion results include 48.6° and 24.4°. A fiber guides light by total internal reflection, qualitatively.

Watch me

Give official 42.2°, official 48.6°, and official 24.4°, each with its pair of materials.

  1. 42.2°, polystyrene to air, official Example 25.4.
  2. 48.6°, water to air, official discussion result.
  3. 24.4°, diamond to air, official discussion result.

Together

Hint 1: The method

Hint 2: The condition

Check

  1. Define the critical angle, and write the official arithmetic of Example 25.4.
  2. Give official 48.6° and official 24.4°, each with its pair of materials.
