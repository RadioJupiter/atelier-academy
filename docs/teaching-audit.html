Teaching audit

Audit of every written lesson in weeks 1–2 (ten days, 80 lessons), made before the explicit-instruction rewrite. A lesson teaches if it explains the idea, states the fact, rule or method, and gives a fully worked example. Partial means it explains or states a rule but has no fully worked example, or (for Latin) mainly sends the student to Ørberg’s book. Assignment only means it mainly sets exercises, a check, or a studio task.

American standardised style in the mathematics

Weeks 1–2 mathematics is already in the Singapore / Kiselev / Gelfand spirit (bar models to equations, proof, identities), not elementary American “word form / expanded form / value of the digit” drills. The only “write in words” exercise in these two weeks is in the Spanish lesson on numbers (Day 4), where writing siete, once, quince is vocabulary, not a mathematics test item. No change of method is needed on that point; mathematics lessons still need explicit worked examples and a check before independent practice.

After the rewrite (intended standard)

Every lesson in weeks 1–2 (all 80, including history, literature, science, and seminar) now follows Learn → Remember → Watch me → Together → Check → practice. Latin grammar and vocabulary are taught here with original examples (the book is extra reading, not the only teacher). Physics keeps Peer Instruction but places a short explanation and a worked example before the ConcepTests. Atelier lessons include a numbered demonstration. Listen-aloud is in English; only Spanish or Latin vocabulary clips use those languages. Each lesson has a Key terms card (Frayer model, plus a verified origin when one can be checked; origins are omitted rather than guessed). Together and Check require the student to explain or apply those terms, or to distinguish them from a near-miss. A glossary lists every term by subject, with a link back to the lesson; Friday of each week has a short spaced vocabulary review. Where a short review sheet is added, it is one page per lesson and only practices what that lesson taught.

Weeks 3–4

Weeks 3–4 (Days 11–20) were written to the same explicit-instruction standard as the weeks 1–2 rewrite: Learn, Remember, Watch me, Together (with hints), a Check that mirrors what was taught, then practice. Key terms use the Frayer card, with an origin only when it could be checked on Wiktionary (or, for a primary-text phrase, on the text itself). Origins that could not be verified were left out (including hatching, foreshortening, quotation, treaty, and the Egyptian etymology sometimes proposed for pyramid). Friday vocabulary reviews on Days 15 and 20 mix earlier weeks. Printable reviews remain one page per lesson and only practice what that lesson taught. Listen-aloud is in English; Spanish and Latin words use Hear clips (Latin with an Italian voice, labeled as an approximation). Mathematics continues in the Singapore / Kiselev spirit (constructions, bar models for systems, no American “write the number in words” filler). Latin examples are original. Physics numbers in the keys were chosen to come out cleanly (for example g = 9.8 m/s² with 19.6 m and 4.9 m for projectile times of 2.0 s and 1.0 s) and should be recomputed if a figure is changed.

Week 5

Week 5 (Days 21–25) was written to the same explicit-instruction standard: Learn, Remember, Watch me, Together (with hints), a Check that mirrors what was taught, then practice. The Friday vocabulary review on Day 25 mixes earlier weeks (1–4). Key terms use the Frayer card, with an origin only when it could be checked on Wiktionary; unverified origins were left out. Printable reviews remain one page per lesson and only practice what that lesson taught. Listen-aloud is in English; Spanish and Latin words use Hear clips (Latin with an Italian voice, labeled as an approximation). Mathematics continues from Euclid Book III and from pairing / subtraction arguments for progression sums, with no American “write the number in words” filler. Latin examples are original (Capitula VII–VIII are companions, not a text to copy). Physics introduces work and energy as an honest first look beyond the Term 1 list, with ( g = 9.8\,\mathrm{m/s}^2 ) and keys that should be recomputed if a figure is changed. Science starts the Living color module without invented absorption percentages, yields, or ( R_f ) values. History continues the source-dating habit (Livy late; traditional early dates; Polybius as a second-century analysis; Caesar as participant and advocacy). Literature quotes the Odyssey only in short, attributed public-domain form.
