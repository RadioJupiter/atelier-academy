Remember

Add every force’s work (signs included). W_net = ΔKE. Friction opposite the displacement does negative work. Perpendicular forces still do zero work.

Key terms: net work.

1. A 4 kg block is pulled 2 m by a 20 N force along the path. Friction is 9.8 N, opposite the path. Find W_net.

2. You hold a 30 N box still, then carry it at constant speed (same height) for 4 m. How much work does the upward supporting force do? Explain.

3. For each of today's key terms (net work), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A 2 kg object at 5 m/s is brought to rest. Find the net work. If a single constant force does that work over 5 m, what is the force (including direction relative to the motion)?
