Remember

A = (1/2)r²θ, with θ in radians. When θ = 2π, A = πr², the area of the disk. Do not put a radian measure into the degree formula as if it were in degrees.

1. What area does A = (1/2)r²θ give when θ = 2π, and why does that matter?

2. On an invented figure, r = 3 and θ = 2π/3, what is the sector area?

3. Why is a circular segment still a miss today?

4. On an invented figure, r = 5 and θ = π/5, find the sector area.

5. On an invented figure, r = 4 and θ = π, find the sector area, and say what fraction of the disk that is.

6. Why is (θ/360) × πr² a miss if θ is already π/2 and is left unchanged?
