In Chapter 9, Mrs. Bennet visits Jane at Netherfield. She says Jane “is a great deal too ill to be moved.” Bingley answers, “It must not be thought of.” Later she tells Elizabeth, “Lizzy, remember where you are.” Chapter 10 remains reserved.
