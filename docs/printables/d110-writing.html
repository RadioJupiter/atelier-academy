Remember

Franklin’s method: an ordered hint-list from Inferno V.1–72, then a reconstruction from those hints, keeping “who reason subjugate to appetite” and Longfellow’s typography. A précis of Dewing I.2.1–12 keeps the Augusteum, the column, the brass, and the statue, then stops. A précis of I.2.13–16 keeps Eirenê and the hospice of Samson, then stops before I.2.17. Invent no official counts, heights, or incomes.

1. What is a Franklin hint-list, and what is a reconstruction, as used this week?

2. Give the set wording from Inferno V.1–72 about reason and appetite, and name two typographical forms you must keep.

3. What must a précis of I.2.1–12 keep about the column and the brass?

4. What must a précis of I.2.13–16 keep, and why does I.2.17 remain reserved?

5. Where must each of this week’s writing jobs stop?

6. Why must you not invent an official height, shade-count, or income?
