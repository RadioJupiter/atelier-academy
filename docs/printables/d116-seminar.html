Remember

Third figure: M is the subject of both premises. Datisi: All M are P; some M are S; therefore some S are P. Ferison concludes some S are not P. Darapti also concludes some S are P, but from two universal premises.

1. In the third figure, where does M stand in the premises?

2. Give Datisi in letters, and apply it to this invented pair: All garden tools in this box are metal. Some garden tools in this box are spades.

3. How does Darapti differ from Datisi?

4. Give Ferison in letters, and say how it differs from Felapton.

5. Why is “all square things are inked” a miss from the stamp example for Datisi?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
