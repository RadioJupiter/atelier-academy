Remember

Haya is haber, sea is ser, vaya is ir, esté is estar; hable, coma, and viva remain the regular models. “Espero que” and “quiere que” in these invented sentences take the subjunctive.

1. Choose: “Espero que ___ (haya / hay / sea) silencio.”

2. Give the yo present subjunctive of haber, ser, ir, estar, and hablar, with short glosses.

3. Why is “coma” a miss for “that there be time,” and “hay” a miss after “espero que”?

4. Write four short invented subjunctive sentences, one each with haya, sea, vaya, and viva.

5. Give a near-miss for each of haya and vaya from another already-taught form.

6. What remains reserved?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
