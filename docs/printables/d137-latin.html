The future of ēgredior is ēgrediar, I shall go out. It is not ēgredior, and not sequar.

1. What are the six future forms of ēgredior?

2. Translate: Puer ēgrediētur. Nōs nōn ēgrediēmur.

3. Why is ēgressus eram a miss for I shall go out?

4. For each of today's key terms (future of ēgredior), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
