Remember

The neutrals lived “withouten infamy or praise.” The guide says, “look, and pass.” The set wording names “him / Who made through cowardice the great refusal,” and this course does not identify that shade. Lines 70 onward, including Charon, are reserved.

Key terms: the neutrals (Inferno III), the great refusal (as worded).

1. Who are the melancholy souls, in the guide’s wording, and with whom are they commingled?

2. Write out the guide’s instruction that ends “but look, and pass.”

3. What does the speaker behold at lines 58–60, and what identification does this course refuse to add?

4. For each of today's key terms (the neutrals (Inferno III), the great refusal (as worded)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
