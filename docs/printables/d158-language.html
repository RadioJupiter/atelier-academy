Remember

Regular participles such as hablado, and irregular participles such as dicho, both take he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han. They are still not hay, and not haya. Visto remains reserved.

1. Give one original sentence with a regular participle, and one with dicho, hecho, or escrito, each with a gloss.

2. How do those two sentences share a helping form, and how do the participles differ?

3. Why is hay a miss in either sentence if the job is the present perfect?

4. Choose the right participle: Yo he ___ (escrito / escribido) una carta; Tú has ___ (hecho / hacido) la tarea.

5. Choose the helping form: Ella ___ (ha / hay / haya) vivido aquí.

6. What remains reserved?
