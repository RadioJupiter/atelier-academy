Remember

Compare, list the misses, rewrite once more. Do not inflate the list with taste ('I just prefer this word') unless the original's word does a job yours does not.

1. What two things do you do after the first rewrite?

2. Is a different word order automatically a miss?

3. Copy the quatrain exactly. Mark the stressed syllables in the first line.

4. Turn it into plain, exact prose of one or two sentences, leaving out nothing the poem says.

5. Identify the metaphor in line 4: what are the "choirs", and why are they "ruin'd"?

6. Turn this prose into four lines of rhymed verse (any meter, rhyme ABAB): In the morning, before work, I draw for an hour; the light is clear, the house is quiet, and the hand is steady.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
