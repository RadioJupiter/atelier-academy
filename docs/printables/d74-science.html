Remember

Dilute: relatively little solute in a given amount of solution (the Gold Book’s wording is a small mole-fraction of solute compared with unity). Concentrated, in this week’s language: relatively much solute in that same sense, compared with a more dilute solution. No official percentage is invented. Saturated remains reserved.

Key terms: dilute solution, concentrated solution.
