Remember

Learn el libro, not just libro. Un / una mean 'a' and follow the same gender. Plural: los / las, and the noun usually adds -s or -es.

Key terms: gender (Spanish), article.

1. Which article goes with ciudad?

2. What does el lápiz mean?

3. For each of today's key terms (gender (Spanish), article), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give el or la: libro, mesa, día, canción, mapa, ciudad, mano, problema, ventana, cuaderno.
