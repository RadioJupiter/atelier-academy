Remember

Learn el libro, not just libro. Un / una mean 'a' and follow the same gender. Plural: los / las, and the noun usually adds -s or -es.

1. Which article goes with ciudad?

2. What does el lápiz mean?

3. Give el or la: libro, mesa, día, canción, mapa, ciudad, mano, problema, ventana, cuaderno.

4. Make plural with the article: el lápiz, la ciudad, el papel, la canción, el día.

5. Translate: a pencil; some windows; the maps; a song; the hand.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
