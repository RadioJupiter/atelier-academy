History · Friday mix: 410, 476, and late antiquity

Week 13 · Day 65 · Friday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

410: sack of Rome by the Goths under Alaric, from Augustine’s later account and the Dods footnote. 476: a conventional marker for the deposition of Romulus Augustulus by Odoacer; the East continued. Late antiquity is a period, not a single day. No invented death toll.

1. Quote or closely paraphrase the set words that name the Goths and Alaric.

2. Why must 476 not be described as a day on which the empire vanished?

3. What is late antiquity, as this week used the name?

4. A student uses 410 and 476 as two names for one event. What has gone wrong?

5. How does Augustine’s extract differ from a battlefield report?

6. Write one careful sentence about 476 that keeps the East in view.
