Remember

The irregular conditional uses last week’s changed stems with -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían. Haría, I would do or make, is not haré and not *hacería.

1. How is an irregular conditional formed, and give haría, diría, and podría with meanings?

2. Write original sentences with iría, tendríamos, and sabría.

3. Why is *hacería a miss, and what remains reserved?

4. Translate, invented only: Mañana lo haría. Ella no podría ir. Nosotros saldríamos. Ellos querrían verlo.

5. Give pondría, vendríamos, and dirías, with meanings.

6. Change hago, voy, and quiero into simple-conditional first-person sentences.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
