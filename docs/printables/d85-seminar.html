Remember

Gray 1918: seven tarsal bones — calcaneus, talus, cuboid, navicular, and the first, second, and third cuneiforms. The calcaneus is the heel, the largest; the talus is the ankle bone, the second largest. Last week’s grouping still holds. Carpal names remain reserved.

Key terms: the seven tarsal bones, calcaneus and talus.

2. Where is the calcaneus, and what two jobs does Gray give it in the wording used today?

3. Where is the talus, and which bones does it meet in the wording used today?

4. For each of today's key terms (the seven tarsal bones, calcaneus and talus), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
