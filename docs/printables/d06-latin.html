Remember

Who does the action: nominative. Who or what is acted on: accusative (often -m in these words). Puella fluvium videt — the girl sees the river.

1. In puella fluvium videt, who is doing the seeing?

2. What ending often marks the singular object in these masculine and feminine nouns?

3. Underline the subject and circle the object: (a) Dominus servum vocat. (b) Servum dominus vocat. (c) Ancilla puerum videt. (d) Puerum ancilla nōn videt.

4. Complete with the accusative: (a) Puella serv__ vocat. (b) Dominus ancill__ videt. (c) Servus domin__ nōn audit.

5. Write four Latin sentences of your own in which a person does something to another, with the verb last.
