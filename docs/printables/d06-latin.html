Remember

Who does the action: nominative. Who or what is acted on: accusative (often -m in these words). Puella fluvium videt — the girl sees the river.

Key terms: accusative, direct object.

1. In puella fluvium videt, who is doing the seeing?

2. What ending often marks the singular object in these masculine and feminine nouns?

3. For each of today's key terms (accusative, direct object), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Underline the subject and circle the object: (a) Dominus servum vocat. (b) Servum dominus vocat. (c) Ancilla puerum videt. (d) Puerum ancilla nōn videt.
