Remember

An encomium praises a general type, or an already-taught work, with already-taught reasons. It is not a commonplace, not a confirmation, and not a praise of a living person. This week’s invented subject is “The Crow and the Pitcher” as a fable well made, not as history.

1. What is an encomium in this course, and how does it differ from a commonplace and from a confirmation?

2. What invented subject is used this week, and what already-taught reason can open the praise?

3. Why is naming a living person a miss?

4. For each of today's key terms (encomium (progymnasmata)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
