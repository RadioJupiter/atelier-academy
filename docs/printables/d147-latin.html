Remember

The future perfect of ēgredior is ēgressus erō, eris, erit, erimus, eritis, erunt. It is not ēgrediar, not ēgressus sum, and not secūtus erō.

1. What are the six future-perfect forms of ēgredior, and what time relation does the tense mark?

2. Translate the original sentence “Puer ēgressus erit,” and say why “ēgrediētur” would be a miss if the job is the future perfect.

3. Why is secūtus erō a miss for “I shall have gone out”?

4. For each of today's key terms (future perfect of ēgredior), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
