Remember

A drying oil forms a film by autoxidation (reaction with oxygen) followed by polymerization (the molecules link into a solid network). That is not evaporation. A thick film dries more slowly inside because oxygen has farther to go.

Key terms: autoxidation, polymerization.

1. In your own words, how is autoxidation different from evaporation? Give one observation that fits each.

2. Why might a painter spread oil paint in thinner layers if the goal is a film that hardens through, not only on the surface?

3. For each of today's key terms (autoxidation, polymerization), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
