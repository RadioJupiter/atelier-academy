Remember

N is perpendicular to the surface; on the level, with no vertical acceleration, N = mg. Kinetic friction f_k = μ_k N, opposite the slide. Static friction ≤ μ_s N. Draw forces, then F_net = ma.

Key terms: normal force, friction.

1. A 4 kg block slides on the level with μk = 0.25. Take g = 9.8 m/s². Find N and fk. If it is pulled horizontally by 20 N, find a.

2. A 5 kg block has μs = 0.3. What is the largest horizontal pull that need not start it moving? (g = 9.8 m/s².)

3. For each of today's key terms (normal force, friction), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A block of mass m is at rest on the level. You push it downward with a force 10 N, and then try to slide it. What happens to N, and what happens to the maximum static friction?
