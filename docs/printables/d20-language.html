Retrieve first, then write. Use only taught forms. The composition must include, on purpose: a stem change, ser and estar, a pronoun, a reflexive, a personal a, an irregular yo, and a comparative.

Key terms: two-week Spanish check.

1. Write the yo and nosotros forms of querer, poder, despertarse, and hacer.

2. Translate: She is clever, and she is ready. I see her. I know her. I know the answer.

3. For each of today's key terms (two-week Spanish check), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Six short translations: (1) We return on Monday. (2) The book is on the table. (3) I am going to read it (the letter). (4) We leave (irse). (5) I bring more than ten books. (6) This lesson is as important as the first.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
