Remember

Choose the set first (regular -ar / -er / -ir, or ser/ir, or hacer), then the person. Fui is not hice; habló is not fue; hizo has z.

1. Give the él/ella form of hablar, of ir, and of hacer.

2. Write three short sentences: one regular -er, one ser or ir, and one hacer, each with a time word already taught.

3. What is the vosotros preterite of vivir, of ser, and of hacer?

4. Hear comí, fui, and hizo, and use each in a short preterite sentence, labeling the verb.
