Remember

Present: loquor, I speak. Imperfect: loquēbar, I was speaking or used to speak. Perfect: locūtus sum, I spoke or have spoken. The absolute puerō locūtō is not a main-clause tense.

1. Give the present, imperfect, and perfect, first person singular, of loquor and of cōnor, with a short English meaning for each.

2. Translate and label the tense: Nōs loquēbāmur. Vōs locūtī estis. Agricolae loquuntur.

3. Why is puerō locūtō not an answer to “the boy was speaking”?

4. Write one original sentence in each of the three deponent tenses of loquor. Translate them.

5. Change cōnābantur to the present and to the perfect, and translate all three.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
