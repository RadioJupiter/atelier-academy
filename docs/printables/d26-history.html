Remember

Three wars: 264–241, 218–201 (Cannae 216, Zama 202), 149–146 (Carthage destroyed in 146). Dates here are much firmer than 753 or 509. Polybius is central and still an argued, Greek, second-century source. Carthaginian voices are scarce in what we have.

Key terms: Punic Wars, Polybius (on the wars).

1. How are these dates better fixed than the traditional 753 BCE founding year, in the sense this course has used?

2. What should you add to a sentence that begins “Hannibal crossed the Alps” if you want the source habit visible?

3. For each of today's key terms (Punic Wars, Polybius (on the wars)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the dates of Cannae and of Zama, and say which war they belong to.
