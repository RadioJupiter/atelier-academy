Remember

Write two paragraphs from the outline. Then run the seven-point list and revise. The key is a checklist, not a model to imitate.

Key terms: two-paragraph essay, revision pass.

1. On your draft, mark the thesis, the two topic sentences, and the concluding sentence. If one is missing, write it.

2. Check every quotation for introduction, quotation marks, attribution, and explanation. Repair any that fail.

3. Name the concession or the limit in your draft, or add one.

4. For each of today's key terms (two-paragraph essay, revision pass), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
