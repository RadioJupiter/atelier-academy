Remember

The letter names Turuqueira, Ceyre, and Ayay. Last week’s extract gave twenty-five leagues, a port late in the evening, and a resolve to land at break of day. Footnotes are not the body.

1. Name the three islands, and say which was the first that they saw.

2. What apparent length, and what later port, did last week’s extract record, without converting the leagues?

3. Why is a later official island name a miss in the body?

4. In your own words, place the three names after last week’s port and resolve, without adding reserved later sentences.

5. Quote the letter’s “they do no injury to each other.”

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
