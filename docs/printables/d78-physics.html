Remember

For a simple pendulum and a small swing (less than about 15°), T = 2π√(L/g). T depends on L and g, not on mass. g ≈ 9.8 m/s² is the already-taught Earth figure. OpenStax, College Physics 2e, 16.4.

Key terms: simple pendulum.

2. If L = 1.00 m gives T about 2.0 s, what T does L = 4.00 m give, in this model?

3. For each of today's key terms (simple pendulum), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
