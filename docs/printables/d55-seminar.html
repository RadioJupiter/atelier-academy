Remember

Wordsworth, “I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud,” first published 1807. Familiar four-stanza public-domain text (Poetry Foundation), with “golden daffodils” and the “inward eye.” Not a sonnet. “Ten thousand” is a figure, not a census. The inward eye is a figure of memory, not a biology term. Attribute the lines.

Key terms: inward eye.

1. Quote the first four lines, and attribute the poem (title, author, first-publication year).

2. How is this poem different from last week’s Keats sonnet, without changing that earlier lesson?

3. For each of today's key terms (inward eye), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. In your own words, what does the last stanza add, and why is the inward eye not a science term?
