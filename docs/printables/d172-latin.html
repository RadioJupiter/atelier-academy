Remember

The four jobs do not change when the verb is ēgredior. The present subjunctive is ēgrediātur, not the future ēgrediētur.

1. Translate and label: Puer surgit ut ēgrediātur. Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur. Puer sedet nē ēgrediātur. Puer tam lentē surgit ut nōn ēgrediātur.

2. Why is ēgrediētur a miss in all four?

3. How can you tell the first sentence from the second?

4. Write four original sentences with ēgredior, one for each job, and translate them.

5. Write one original result with sequātur, and translate it.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
