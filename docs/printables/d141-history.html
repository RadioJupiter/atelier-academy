Remember

The letter follows the north coast of Española “one hundred and seventy-eight full leagues due east.” That figure is the letter’s, not Major’s footnote “188,” and not an official modern conversion. “Cetefrey” is not given an official modern identification.

1. In Major’s wording, how far does he follow the northern coast of Española, and with what comparison to Juana?

2. What does the letter say of the seaports and of the mountains, including “Cetefrey”?

3. Why is Major’s footnote “188 leagues” not taught as official, and what conversion must you not add?

4. Quote the “one hundred and seventy-eight full leagues” clause.

5. Why must “Cetefrey” stay inside the quotation as this edition prints it?

6. What part of the letter waits until Thursday?
