When C is 120°, cos C = −1/2, so −2ab cos C is positive, and the opposite side is longer than it would be for an acute included angle on the same two sides.

1. Why is cos 120° equal to −1/2?

2. On an invented triangle with sides 4 and 4 and included angle 120°, what is the third side?

3. How does that third side compare with last week’s law-of-sines result on the same invented triangle?

4. Find the third side of an invented triangle with sides 2 and 2 and included angle 120°.

5. Why is the opposite side longer than 2, even though the other two sides are 2?

6. Why must the 4, 4, and 120° stay labeled invented?
