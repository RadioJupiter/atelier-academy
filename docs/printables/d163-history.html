Remember

The letter says they searched more than a league and found “no harbour” on the first island. The other island was “at four or five leagues distance.” The admiral landed “with the royal banner in his hands, and took formal possession on behalf of their Majesties.” “Harbour” and “any thing” stay inside quotations. A footnote about “harbours” is Major’s note, not the body.

1. What does the letter say about a “harbour” on the first island, and what does the admiral then decide?

2. How is formal possession taken, in the letter’s wording?

3. Why do “harbour” and “any thing” stay inside quotations?

4. For each of today's key terms (“no harbour,” and formal possession with the royal banner), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
