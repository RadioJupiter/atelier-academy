Remember

Nōs and vōs are the plural pronouns; nōbīs and vōbīs are the dative and ablative forms. Licet, it is allowed, takes a dative and an infinitive, and has no personal subject. It is not posse.

Key terms: nōs and vōs, licet.

1. Translate: Vōs discipulī estis.

2. Write Latin for “We are allowed to hear.”

3. What is the difference between Recitāre possumus and Nōbīs recitāre licet?

4. For each of today's key terms (nōs and vōs, licet), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
