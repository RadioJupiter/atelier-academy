Remember

The present first-person endings are -ō and -mus; the second-person endings are -s and -tis. The ending names the person. A pronoun is added only for contrast, not because the verb is incomplete.

Key terms: present first person, present second person.

1. What person and number is recitātis, and how do you know?

2. Write Latin for “we recite” and for “I am silent,” using taceō, I am silent.

3. Why is tū unnecessary in Recitās, but useful in Ego recitō, tū tacēs?

4. For each of today's key terms (present first person, present second person), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
