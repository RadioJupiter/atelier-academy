Remember

Vi is ver (saw); di is dar (gave). Di is not dije; dio is not dijo. Last week’s dije remains a contrast only.

1. Give the six forms of ver and the six of dar in the preterite.

2. Translate the invented pair: Ella vio la puerta. Ella dio el libro.

3. Why is dije a miss for “I gave”?

4. Write invented sentences that use vi, di, vieron, and dieron, one each.

5. Place this week’s two first-person forms beside last week’s dije and traje, with a short sense for each.

6. Correct the misses vié and dió if they appear, in this lesson’s spelling, and *dije used for dar.
