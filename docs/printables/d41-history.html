Remember

69 CE is a civil-war year after Nero’s death, not a fall of Rome. Galba, Otho, Vitellius, and Vespasian are the four men later readers often list. “Year of the Four Emperors” is a later name. Tacitus’s opening names them and also names his career under Vespasian, Titus, and Domitian.

Key terms: Year of the Four Emperors (later name), Tacitus as a later source.

1. In what year does this lesson place the civil war it describes, and what year already ended the Julio-Claudian list?

2. Name one later title we will not treat as Tacitus’s own heading.

3. For each of today's key terms (Year of the Four Emperors (later name), Tacitus as a later source), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is 69 CE not taught as “the fall of Rome”?
