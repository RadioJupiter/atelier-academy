Remember

In any triangle, a/sin A = b/sin B = c/sin C. It follows from (1/2)bc sin A = (1/2)ac sin B. On an invented 30°-60°-90° triangle with hypotenuse 2, each ratio equals 2.

1. State the law of sines, and show, from the area formula, that a/sin A = b/sin B.

2. On an invented 30°-60°-90° triangle with hypotenuse 2, compute 1/sin 30°, √3/sin 60°, and 2/sin 90°.

3. Why are the sides not themselves equal, and what remains reserved?

4. If an invented right triangle has a side 5 opposite 30°, and a right angle, what is the hypotenuse, by the law of sines?

5. Why can last week’s sin 150° = 1/2 be needed later, even though today’s check used 30°, 60°, and 90°?

6. Why must the hypotenuse 2 be called invented?
