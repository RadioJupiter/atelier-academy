Remember

Regular present subjunctives: hable, coma, viva, and their persons. Irregular subjunctives remain reserved.

1. Give hablen, comáis, and viva, with meanings.

2. Correct the invented miss “Quiere que nosotros hablamos,” and say why it is a miss.

3. Choose: “Mañana ellos ___ (vivirán / vivan),” if there is no “quiere que.”

4. Write three short invented “quiere que” sentences, one each with hable, coma, and vivamos.

5. Give a near-miss for each of hable, coma, and viva from another already-taught tense.

6. What remains reserved?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
