Remember

Me gusta el libro / Me gustan los libros. The thing is the subject; the person is an indirect object. Not Yo gusto el libro. An infinitive counts as a singular subject: Me gusta leer.

Key terms: gustar (pattern).

1. Write: “Ana likes the class” (use le and a Ana).

2. Write: “We like to read.”

3. For each of today's key terms (gustar (pattern)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the forms: Me gusta / Me gustan with la frase and las frases.
