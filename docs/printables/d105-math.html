Remember

A frustum is a large cone minus a similar small cone. V = (1/3)πh(R² + Rr + r²). Lateral area π(R + r)l; total surface adds both bases. Similar solids: lengths k, areas k², volumes k³.

1. What is a frustum of a right cone, and what is its volume formula?

2. A classroom frustum has R = 6, r = 3, h = 4, l = 5. Give the volume and the total surface area.

3. If corresponding lengths of similar solids scale by k, how do areas and volumes scale?

4. Two similar cubes have edges 3 and 6. The smaller has volume 27. What is the volume of the larger?

5. A classroom large cone has radius 8 and height 12. A similar small cone of height 6 is cut away. Find the volume of the frustum, in terms of π.

6. A classroom frustum has R = 10, r = 5, and l = 13. Find the total surface area, in terms of π.
