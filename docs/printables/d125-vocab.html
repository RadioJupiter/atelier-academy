A definition you can only recognize on the card is not yet yours. Give the meaning, a fresh example, and a near-miss.

1. On the invented classroom box, what three relations can a line have to a plane, and what three conditions make two lines skew?

2. Give the six pluperfect forms of loquor, and say how locūtus eram differs from locūtus sum and from loquēbar.

3. Give the six simple-future forms of hablar, and choose: Mañana yo ___ (hablo / hablaré).

4. What is superposition, in OpenStax 16.10, and what is f_B if a 256 Hz fork gives two beats per second? For the invented string, L = 2.00 m and vw = 80.0 m/s, what is f1?

5. What is ochre, in the USGS sources, and what is the formula of hematite? What official figure must you not invent for a classroom lump?

6. Quote the first sentence of Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 1, in the 1813 wording, and say, from the second sentence, where the “truth” is already fixed.
