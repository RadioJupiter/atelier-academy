Remember

Ponens: If P then Q; P; therefore Q. Tollens: If P then Q; not Q; therefore not P. Disjunctive syllogism: P or Q; not P; therefore Q. Default “or” is inclusive. Not affirming the consequent. Day 16 is not reteached as new.

Key terms: valid “or” and conditional forms, a Term 1 close.

1. State modus ponens, and give last week’s square-and-rectangle instance.

2. State modus tollens, and say how it differs from denying the antecedent.

3. For each of today's key terms (valid “or” and conditional forms, a Term 1 close), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “it is a rectangle; therefore it is a square” not modus ponens?
