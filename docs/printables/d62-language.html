Remember

hablo / hablas / habla is present; hablé / hablaste / habló is preterite. Regular -ar verbs share those past endings. hablamos can be either tense. cantó and habló keep their written accents. Instruction remains in English.

1. Give the present and the preterite of hablar for yo and for ella.

2. Write “you (familiar singular) bought” and “you (familiar singular) buy.”

3. Why can Nosotros hablamos be either tense, and how would you make the past clear today?

4. Correct: Ustedes compró and Yo cantaste.

5. Write a two-sentence pair: one present and one preterite, with cantar.

6. Give the preterite forms of comprar for tú, nosotros, and ellos.
