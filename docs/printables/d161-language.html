Remember

The present perfect may use the irregular participle visto, from ver, with the same helping forms he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han. He visto is I have seen. It is still not hay, and not haya. Other irregular participles remain reserved.

1. What irregular participle is taught today, and from which infinitive does it come?

2. Translate the original sentence “Nosotros hemos visto la carta,” and say why “hay visto” would be a miss.

3. Why is a new irregular participle beyond visto a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (the irregular participle visto, a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
