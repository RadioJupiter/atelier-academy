Remember

Festino is EIO in the second figure: No P are M; Some S are M; therefore Some S are not P. Camestres, already taught, is AEE in the same figure. The conclusion of Festino is particular, not universal. Baroco is reserved.

Key terms: Festino.

1. State Festino in letters (P, M, S), and name the figure.

2. Give a short classroom example, and why “No S are P” would be a miss as its conclusion.

3. How does Festino differ from last week’s Camestres?

4. For each of today's key terms (Festino), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
