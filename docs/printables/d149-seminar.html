Remember

On invented figures, saving is setting aside some or all of a $10 remainder. Three invented months at $10 give $30. The figures are not official, and official interest is not taught today.

1. What is saving in this invented arrangement, and, if the whole invented $10 remainder is set aside each month, what is set aside after three months?

2. If only $5 of each invented remainder is set aside, what is set aside after three months, and what has happened to the other $5?

3. Why is the $30 not a tax, not a premium, and not a deductible, and why is official interest not added?

4. For each of today's key terms (saving (invented figures)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
