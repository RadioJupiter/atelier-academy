Remember

A précis of I.1.54–57 keeps the gold ceiling, the prevailing light from the stones, the two side stoas, their assignment, and their equality. It is not a quotation, and not a first look at the précis. Yesterday’s I.1.50–53 is not to be re-précised as if it were new.

1. In Dewing I.1.54, what is overlaid with gold, and what prevails?

2. What two colonnades does I.1.55–56 describe, and to whom are they assigned?

3. What does I.1.57 say distinguishes the two stoas?

4. Write a précis of I.1.54–57 in your own words, shorter than the extract.

5. Why is “the gold outshines everything” a miss?

6. How should yesterday’s lead joints appear today, if at all?
