Remember

A cervical vertebra is distinguished, in Gray, by a foramen in each transverse process, the foramen transversarium. In the upper six it gives passage to the vertebral artery and vein and a plexus of sympathetic nerves. Atlas, axis, and the seventh remain reserved. Official lengths remain reserved.

1. In Gray’s wording, how is a cervical vertebra distinguished from a thoracic or lumbar vertebra?

2. What is the foramen transversarium, and what does it give passage to in the upper six vertebrae?

3. What remains reserved, and what official figures must you not invent?
