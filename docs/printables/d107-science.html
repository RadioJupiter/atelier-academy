Remember

Whenever there is a temperature difference, heat transfer occurs by only three methods, from OpenStax 14.4. Conduction: through stationary matter by contact (burner to pan). Convection: by the macroscopic movement of a fluid (a forced-air furnace). Radiation: by electromagnetic radiation (the Earth warmed by the Sun). Quantitative rates remain reserved.

1. When does heat transfer occur, according to OpenStax 14.4, and by how many methods?

2. What is conduction, and what example does OpenStax give?

3. What is convection, and what example does OpenStax give?

4. What is radiation, in this lesson’s school sense, and why do official rates remain reserved?
