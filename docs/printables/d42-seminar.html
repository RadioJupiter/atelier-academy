Remember

A minor triad is a root, a minor third, and a perfect fifth. In C major, A–C–E is a minor triad; C–E–G remains major. The difference is the third. No invented harmonic-minor course today.

Key terms: minor triad.

1. Name the intervals from the root in a minor triad.

2. In C major, which of C–E–G and A–C–E is minor?

3. For each of today's key terms (minor triad), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Build a minor triad on D (D–F–A), and say why it is minor.
