Remember

After the Great Canary, the letter turns to Gomera for stores of meat, wood, and water, “nineteen or twenty days before we arrived at the Island of Ferro.” “Leaving Ferro on the thirteenth of October, within twenty days we came in sight of land.” Those claims are the letter’s. The landfall on the third of November waits until Friday.

1. What stores does the letter mention at Gomera, and how does it date the arrival at Ferro?

2. How does the letter date the departure from Ferro, and what does it say happened “within twenty days”?

3. Why is Friday’s landfall wording a miss for today?

4. For each of today's key terms (Gomera, Ferro, and the thirteenth of October, in the second-voyage letter), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
