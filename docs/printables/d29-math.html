Remember

Three non-collinear points determine a circle; the center is where perpendicular bisectors meet. In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is a diameter, so the center is the midpoint of that side. Four points need an extra condition.

Key terms: circle through three points (review).

1. Describe, in order, how to construct the circumcenter of an acute triangle ABC with compass and straightedge (no new steps, just the right order).

2. ABCD is a rectangle that is not a square, with sides 6 and 8. What is the circumradius, and why is there a circumcircle at all?

3. For each of today's key terms (circle through three points (review)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Three points are collinear. How many circles through all three? Explain.
