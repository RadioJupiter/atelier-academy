Remember

Lose an edge where values nearly match or the turn is slow; find an edge where the cut is clear. Name a few decisions. A lost edge is not an erased plane, and not a uniform outline.

Key terms: lost-and-found on the head.

1. Name two edges on your head you will lose or nearly lose, and one you will find, after looking at the subject.

2. Why is “portraits should always have lost edges on the left” a bad rule here?

3. For each of today's key terms (lost-and-found on the head), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Half-close the eyes and list the edges that actually disappear on this subject (not on a remembered recipe).
