Remember

Domī means at home, with no preposition. It is a locative, like the already-taught Rōmae, but domī itself is a first look. In plus the ablative is a different construction.

Key terms: domī.

1. What does domī mean, and why is a preposition a miss with it in this use?

2. Translate: Puerī domī sunt.

3. How is domī like Rōmae, and how is it a first look today?
