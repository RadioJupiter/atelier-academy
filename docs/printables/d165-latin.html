Remember

Ut marks a positive purpose; nē marks a negative purpose. Both take the present subjunctive. Sequētur and ēgrediētur remain futures. A result clause remains reserved.

1. What is a purpose clause with nē, and how does it differ from a purpose clause with ut?

2. Give ēgrediātur in an ut-clause and in a nē-clause, each with a gloss.

3. Why is a result clause still reserved?

4. Write a short mixed set: one ut-clause with sequor, one nē-clause with ēgredior, and one future, each with a gloss.

5. Correct the miss “Puer surgit nē ēgrediātur” if the job is a positive purpose.

6. What remains reserved?
