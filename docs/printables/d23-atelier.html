Remember

Simplify the head into a few large planes. Place one terminator for the whole head before local feature shadows. Reflected light stays quieter than the main light. The subject can soften a plane; it should not erase the turn.

Key terms: planes of the head, terminator of the head.

1. Name four planes you can actually point to on your head drawing.

2. When may you soften a constructed “corner” between planes?

3. For each of today's key terms (planes of the head, terminator of the head), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Where should the shadow of the nose sit relative to the large terminator, if the nose is still in the light?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
