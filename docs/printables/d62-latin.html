Remember

Pectus, ōs (long ō, mouth or face), crūs, and cor are third-declension neuters. Nominative equals accusative in the singular; those cases end in -a in the plural. ōs, ōris is not os, ossis (“bone”).

Key terms: third-declension neuter (more nouns).

1. What is the stem of crūs, and what is its nominative plural?

2. Write Latin for “The girl has a face” (use ōs).

3. How is ōs, ōris different from os, ossis?

4. Correct: *Pectorem hominis videō.
