Remember

A lever turns about a fulcrum. In rotational equilibrium, load × load arm = effort × effort arm (opposite senses). If the lever is at rest, the fulcrum also supplies the upward force that makes ΣF = 0. Numbers in this lesson are invented classroom figures.

Key terms: fulcrum, the lever rule.

1. In your own words, what is a fulcrum, and what torque does a force through the fulcrum give about it?

2. A 3.00 m light rod has its fulcrum 0.50 m from the left end. 30 N acts downward at the left end. Find the downward force at the right end that gives Στ = 0.

3. For that same rod, what upward force must the fulcrum supply if the rod is at rest?

4. Why is the lever rule not a new law, separate from Monday’s Στ = 0?
