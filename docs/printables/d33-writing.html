Remember

An interruption is a comment, set off, that the sentence can survive without. A series must stay parallel around it.

Key terms: parenthetical interruption.

1. Write one clean three-part series, then the same series with one legal interruption.

2. Repair a sentence of your own that broke a series with a mid-thought dash.

3. For each of today's key terms (parenthetical interruption), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. The removal test, in one sentence of your own.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
