Remember

Secūtus sum is I followed, or I have followed; sequor is I follow; sequar is I shall follow; secūtus eram is I had followed. The same four-way contrast holds for ēgredior.

1. Give the first-person singular present, future, perfect, and pluperfect of sequor, with meanings.

2. Translate: Puella ēgressa est. Puer eam sequētur.

3. Why is sequuntur a miss for they have followed?

4. Form the perfect of “we go out” and the future of “he follows.”

5. Write two original sentences, one with secūtī sumus and one with ēgrediar.

6. Why must Puer or Puella, not an unquoted English identity word, mark the person in a Latin sentence of this kind?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
