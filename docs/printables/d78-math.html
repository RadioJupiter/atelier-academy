Remember

A trapezoid has exactly one pair of parallel sides (the bases). A = ½(a + b)h, with h perpendicular to the bases. A slanted side is not the height.

Key terms: trapezoid, area of a trapezoid.

1. A trapezoid has bases 7 and 13 and height 6. Find the area.

2. Why is a slanted side of 8 not used as h, if the perpendicular height is 6?

3. For each of today's key terms (trapezoid, area of a trapezoid), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A trapezoid has bases 9 and 9. What has gone wrong with calling it a trapezoid in this course’s usage, and what would ½(9 + 9)h still equal?
