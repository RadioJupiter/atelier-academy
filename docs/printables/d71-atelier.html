Remember

A value pass assigns light, mid-tone, and dark to features already placed, according to the planes and the light. It does not move the features, and it is not a new medium.

Key terms: a value pass across the features.

1. Why is the well of a socket often darker than the forehead above it, if the light is from above?

2. What is the terminator, in the sense already used on the head, doing in this pass?

3. For each of today's key terms (a value pass across the features), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Name three values you will use on the already-placed features, and give one plane for each.
