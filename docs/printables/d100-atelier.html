Remember

If the light is from above, the underplane of the jaw starts in the shadow family; a seen bounce stays darker than the light-family halftones. The mouth is a slot in the form, not a line. A likeness check of mouth and chin together changes only what it shows. A written pause names only what was seen or changed.
