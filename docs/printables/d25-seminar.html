Remember

The torso is a rib-cage volume and a pelvic volume, with a soft abdomen between them. The two blocks can tilt in opposite ways in contrapposto. Landmarks are tools; the observed form wins. No identifying live-model records.

Key terms: rib cage (artistic), pelvis (artistic).

1. Name two landmarks of the torso this lesson actually gave you, and one thing it refused to turn into a memorized drill.

2. How does today’s pelvic tilt connect to the Doryphoros’s stance, without repeating an invented measurement?

3. For each of today's key terms (rib cage (artistic), pelvis (artistic)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Sketch (or describe in labeled words) the two blocks and the soft middle, in a front view, with one slight contrapposto tilt.
