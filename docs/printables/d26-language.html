Remember

tener que + infinitive: someone has to. hay que + infinitive: it is necessary to (impersonal). hay: there is / there are (same form for plural). Still the present.

Key terms: tener que, hay / hay que.

1. Write the tú and nosotros forms of tener que leer.

2. Write one sentence with hay and one with hay que.

3. For each of today's key terms (tener que, hay / hay que), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Contrast Tiene que repetir with Hay que repetir: who is obliged in each?
