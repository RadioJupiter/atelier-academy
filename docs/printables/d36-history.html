Remember

Augustus died in 14 CE; Tiberius succeeded as princeps. 14 CE is a succession date, not a new founding day and not a “fall” or a return of the Republic. Later writers are still later.

Key terms: succession of Tiberius.

1. About how long after 14 CE did Tacitus and Suetonius write, as this course has dated them?

2. What kind of source is the Res Gestae for the years after Augustus’s death?

3. For each of today's key terms (succession of Tiberius), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Place 31 BCE, 27 BCE, and 14 CE in order, with a short label for each.
