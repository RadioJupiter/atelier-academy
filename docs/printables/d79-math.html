Remember

A regular n-gon splits into n congruent isosceles triangles from the center. The apothem is the perpendicular from the center to a side. A = ½ × perimeter × apothem. A regular hexagon is six equilateral triangles. Circle area is reserved.

Key terms: apothem, area of a regular polygon.

1. A regular octagon has perimeter 40 and apothem 6 (invented). Find the area.

2. Why is the apothem not the same thing as a side?

3. For each of today's key terms (apothem, area of a regular polygon), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A regular hexagon has side 4. Find the area in the form that uses √3, by the same method as side 6.
