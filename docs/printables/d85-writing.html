Remember

The second body paragraph takes the next heading. The other-side paragraph is fair, then answers, and does not become a straw man. The opening states the thesis. The close returns to it and stops.

1. What does a second body paragraph do that the first one has already done for an earlier heading?

2. Name the three jobs of an other-side paragraph, in order.

3. What must the opening state, and what tour should it refuse?

4. What must the close refuse to add?

5. If a quotation has drifted, what are the two honest repairs?
