Remember

A topic sentence names the paragraph’s job. The rest of the paragraph keeps the promise. In a three-paragraph essay, write the three topic sentences first.

Key terms: topic sentence.

1. Write three topic sentences for an essay that claims a function has exactly one output for each allowed input.

2. Take one of those sentences and add two supporting sentences that keep the promise.

3. For each of today's key terms (topic sentence), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Mark the topic sentence in a short paragraph you write today.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
