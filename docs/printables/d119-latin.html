Remember

Present loquor, imperfect loquēbar, perfect locūtus sum. The absolute is not a main-clause tense. AcI has an accusative subject and an infinitive verb.

1. Translate and label: Ego cōnābar. Nōs locūtī sumus. Puer loquitur.

2. Why is puerō locūtō not an answer to “the boy was speaking”?

3. In already-taught AcI, what jobs do the accusative and the infinitive have?

4. Write one original sentence in each of the three deponent tenses, and one AcI sentence with esse. Translate them.

5. Change cōnābāminī to the present and to the perfect, and translate all three.
