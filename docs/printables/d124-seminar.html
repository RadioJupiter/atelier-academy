Remember

A sales tax is added to the price. Invented: 5 percent of $80 is $4, total $84. A discount leaving $76 is a miss. This is not an official rate.

1. For the invented $80 and 5 percent sales tax, what is the tax, and what is the total? Show two ways to get the total.

2. Why is $76 a miss, and why is last term’s markup-then-discount not this lesson?

3. Why must you not treat 5 percent as an official living tax rate?

4. For each of today's key terms (sales tax (invented figures)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
