Remember

For constant speed on a circle, a = v²/r toward the center, and F_net inward = m v²/r. That net force is supplied by a real inward force, not by a new ‘centrifugal’ force on the body.

Key terms: centripetal acceleration, centripetal force.

1. A car takes a level curve of radius 50 m at 20 m/s. Find a. If the car’s mass is 800 kg, what net inward force is required?

2. A stone on a 2 m string makes one lap every 1 s. Using v = 2πr/T, find v and a. (You may leave π in the answer, or take π² ≈ 10.)

3. For each of today's key terms (centripetal acceleration, centripetal force), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is it wrong to draw an outward force on a free-body diagram of a car on a curve, in this course’s inertial-frame treatment?
