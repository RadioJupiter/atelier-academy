Remember

The Republic has two annual consuls, a Senate, and assemblies; it is not a kingship and not a modern democracy. Polybius (2nd c. BCE) is a major source for the mid-Republic and is also making an argument. 509 BCE, as a beginning, is traditional.

Key terms: Roman Republic, consul, Senate.

1. What is the Senate, in the working picture of this lesson, and what is it not?

2. Why does this course treat 509 BCE as a traditional beginning rather than as a measured year you can simply quote?

3. For each of today's key terms (Roman Republic, consul, Senate), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Place Polybius in the second century BCE and name one aim of his history mentioned today.
