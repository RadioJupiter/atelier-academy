Remember

In Dewing I.1.33–34, a quarter-sphere and a crescent-shaped structure rise above the east end. The crescent seems to float and to put those inside in peril, yet it is, Dewing says, firmly braced. “Marvellous” stays in the quotation. No measurement is invented.

Key terms: seeming to float (Dewing I.1.34).

1. In your own words, what is the difference between how the crescent-shaped structure seems and how it is, in Dewing?

2. What is “the fourth part of a sphere,” as used today?

3. For each of today's key terms (seeming to float (Dewing I.1.34)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. How does today’s extract sit on Monday’s half-cylinder, rather than starting the church over again?
