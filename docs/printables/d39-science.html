Remember

Two eyes give slightly different views of a near scene, a first cue for judging distance. No invented measurements. Two eyes are not the only cue, and they also make a single blind spot easier to ignore.

Key terms: two views (binocular, first look).

1. Why this lesson refuses a measured number for the space between the eyes.

2. How can two eyes make a single blind spot easier to ignore, as named today?

3. For each of today's key terms (two views (binocular, first look)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a three-sentence paragraph whose topic sentence is “Two views are a cue, not a complete theory.”
