Remember

Resonance still occurs when the driving frequency equals the natural frequency. Less damping gives a higher and narrower peak; heavy damping lowers the amplitude and spreads the response. OpenStax 16.8. Invent no official peak height or wind speed. Piano strings and a car’s suspension are kinds of design, not official measured periods.
