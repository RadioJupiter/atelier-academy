Remember

The first sentence offers a “truth universally acknowledged.” The second already places that “truth” in the minds of surrounding families, as a claim of “rightful property.” That turn is the irony of the opening.

1. Quote the first sentence of the 1813 Chapter 1, and the phrase from the second sentence that names where the “truth” is fixed.

2. In your own words, what is ironic in the turn from the first sentence to the second?

3. What must you not treat the opening as, and what remains reserved until Thursday?

4. For each of today's key terms (ironic opening (Pride and Prejudice)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
