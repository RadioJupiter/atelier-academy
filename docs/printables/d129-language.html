Remember

Regular futures keep the infinitive. Irregular ones keep the same endings on a changed stem. Hablaré is not haré; *haceré is a miss.

1. How does a regular future differ from this week’s irregular ones, and give one yo form of each kind?

2. Write one original sentence with hablaré and one with haré, and one with tendremos.

3. Why are haceré and teneré misses, and what remains reserved?

4. Translate, invented times or places only: Mañana iremos. Ella saldrá. Nosotros querremos verlo. Tú lo sabrás.

5. Give dirán, pondré, and vendremos, with meanings.

6. Change hablo, hago, and tengo into simple-future first-person sentences about tomorrow.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
