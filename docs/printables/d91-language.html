Remember

Ver in the preterite: vi, viste, vio, vimos, visteis, vieron. The school sense is a completed seeing. Last week’s dij- and traj- are not first looks. Vio has no accent in the spelling taught here.

Key terms: vi.

1. Give the six preterite forms of ver.

2. Translate the invented sentence: Ayer vi la puerta.

3. Why is vió a miss in this lesson’s spelling, and how is vi not last week’s dije?

4. For each of today's key terms (vi), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
