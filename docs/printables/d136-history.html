Remember

The first-voyage letter, in Major’s 1870 translation, is a voyage report to Luis de Santangel. It is not the Treaty of Tordesillas, and later identifications in the footnotes are not taught as official.

1. In Major’s 1870 wording, to whom is the letter sent, and what does the opening say about thirty-three days?

2. What names does the letter give to the first five islands, including the name “the Indians call” the first, as this edition prints it?

3. Why is the Treaty of Tordesillas not this letter, and what official claim must you not add?

4. For each of today's key terms (the first-voyage letter (to Luis de Santangel)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
