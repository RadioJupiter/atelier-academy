Remember

A right pyramid has V = (1/3)Bh, with B the base area and h perpendicular to the base. A cube splits into three square pyramids and shows the one-third. A prism of the same base and height has three times the volume. Classroom lengths are invented.

Key terms: volume of a right pyramid.
