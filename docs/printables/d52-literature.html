Four set Dryden 1697 lines: a pretence; soldiers hidden in the hollow side; inward arms; a dark abode. Still Dryden, not Virgil’s Latin. Tenedos, the gates, and later scenes remain reserved.

3. For each of today's key terms (a short attributed hollow-side passage), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
