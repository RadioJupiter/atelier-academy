Remember

Sī, if; nisi, unless / if not; at, but on the other hand (two sides, not a first look at sed); antequam, before, with a clause.

Key terms: sī and nisi, at and antequam.

1. Translate: Sī recitātis, magister gaudet. (gaudēre, to be glad — a form you can take as taught for this sentence.) If gaudet is unfamiliar, you may instead translate: Sī recitātis, magister nōn īrātus est.

2. Write Latin for “Unless you (singular) are silent, I am not able to hear.”

3. What is the difference between at and sed, as taught today?

4. For each of today's key terms (sī and nisi, at and antequam), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
