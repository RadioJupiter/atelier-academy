Remember

Regular futures only: hablaré, comeré, viviré, and the rest of each table. Contrast with the present when the time is now.

1. Write three short original future sentences, using at least two of hablar, comer, and vivir.

2. Choose and explain: Mañana nosotros ___ (comemos / comeremos).

3. Choose and explain: Ahora yo ___ (vivo / viviré) aquí.

4. Give the six future forms of hablar from memory.

5. Give comeré, comeremos, and comerán, with meanings.

6. What remains reserved, and what official fact must you not invent in a sentence?
