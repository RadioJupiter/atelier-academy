Sequar, I shall follow; ēgrediar, I shall go out; neither is a present, and neither is a pluperfect.

1. Give the six future forms of sequor and of ēgredior.

2. Translate: Puer mē sequētur, et tum ēgrediētur.

3. Why is loquar a miss for I shall follow, and secūtus eram a miss for I shall follow?

4. Change sequimur and ēgrediuntur into futures, then into pluperfects, still in the same persons.

5. Write two original mixed sentences, each using one future and one already-taught tense.
