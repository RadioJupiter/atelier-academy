Remember

Books 5–8 bring Odysseus from Calypso’s island, through the raft and the storm, to the Phaeacians. The earlier wanderings are not yet told; that telling begins in Book 9. Hospitality here contrasts with the suitors at home.

Key terms: in medias res (Odyssey), Phaeacians.

1. What makes Odysseus weep among the Phaeacians, at the level of today’s outline?

2. Give one contrast between the Phaeacians’ hospitality and the suitors in Ithaca.

3. For each of today's key terms (in medias res (Odyssey), Phaeacians), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. In two sentences, tell the raft episode without adding later books.
