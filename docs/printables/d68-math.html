Remember

In a right triangle, c² = a² + b². A missing hypotenuse is the positive square root of a² + b². A missing leg is the positive square root of c² minus the other leg’s square. Do not add the legs themselves.

Key terms: a missing side in a right triangle.

1. A right triangle has legs 7 and 24. Find the hypotenuse.

2. A right triangle has hypotenuse 25 and one leg 15. Find the other leg.

3. For each of today's key terms (a missing side in a right triangle), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “the hypotenuse is 9 + 12 = 21” a miss for the 9-12-15 triangle?
