Remember

A forced oscillation is driven by a periodic driving force. The natural frequency is the frequency the system would have with no driving and no damping. Resonance is driving at that natural frequency, when energy is transferred most efficiently and the amplitude is greatest. OpenStax 16.8. Last week’s damping is a contrast. Wednesday treats the shape of the peak.
