Remember

A purpose clause uses ut and the present subjunctive. The future, perfect, and future perfect keep their already-taught jobs and are not purpose clauses. Do not swap sequor and ēgredior.

1. Give a form of sequor for each gloss: she comes in order that she follow; she will follow; she followed.

2. Give a form of ēgredior for: in order that he go out; he will go out; he will have gone out.

3. Why is ut sequātur a miss for “he went out”?

4. Write short original sentences for ut sequātur, ut ēgrediātur, sequētur, and ēgressus est, with glosses.

5. Contrast venit ut ēgrediātur and ēgrediētur in two short glosses.

6. What remains reserved?
