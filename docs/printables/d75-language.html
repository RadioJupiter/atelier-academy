Remember

Ser/ir: fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron. Hacer: hice, hiciste, hizo, hicimos, hicisteis, hicieron. Hizo has z; yo is hice, not hizo. Fui is not hice.

1. Write one sentence with fueron as ir, one with fueron as a near-miss to avoid if you mean hacer, and the hacer form you would use instead.

2. What regular yo forms were already taught for hablar, comer, and vivir, and why is fui not one of them?

3. Hear fuiste, hiciste, and fue, and use each in a short preterite sentence, labeling the verb.

4. Why do ser and ir share this preterite set, as far as this week’s lesson is concerned, and how do you tell the meanings apart?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
