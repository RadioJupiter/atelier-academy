Remember

The letter, in Major’s 1870 wording, claims “the greatest friendship with the king of that country,” and says “he took pride in calling me his brother, and treating me as such.” That is the letter’s claim, confirmed from the page, not a later official judgment.

1. In Major’s 1870 wording, what does the letter claim about friendship at Navidad?

2. How is that wording taught, and what is not taken from a later official source?

3. Why are later sentences on that page about hostilities a miss for today?

4. For each of today's key terms (the letter’s claim of friendship at Navidad), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
