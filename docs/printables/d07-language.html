Tengo un lápiz. Tiene una casa. Tenemos que estudiar — we have to study. Learn the six forms; do not apply the regular -er pattern here (teno is not a word).

1. What does tienes mean?

2. How do you say 'they have'?

3. Translate: (a) We are hungry. (b) Are you (tú) cold? (c) She is right. (d) They have to paint today. (e) I have three brushes (pinceles).

4. Complete with the correct form of tener: (a) Yo ___ un cuaderno nuevo. (b) Ustedes ___ que practicar. (c) ¿Tú ___ sed?
