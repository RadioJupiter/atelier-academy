Tengo un lápiz. Tiene una casa. Tenemos que estudiar — we have to study. Learn the six forms; do not apply the regular -er pattern here (teno is not a word).

Key terms: tener, irregular present.

1. What does tienes mean?

2. How do you say 'they have'?

3. For each of today's key terms (tener, irregular present), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Translate: (a) We are hungry. (b) Are you (tú) cold? (c) She is right. (d) They have to paint today. (e) I have three brushes (pinceles).
