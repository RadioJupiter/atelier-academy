Remember

List the points, write in your own words, then test against the set passage. A précis is short and faithful; it is not a quotation, a dropped-point summary, or a review.

1. Write a précis in two or three sentences. Keep the points; use your own words.

2. Name one phrase you deliberately did not copy from Dewing, and say how you restated it.

3. A classmate’s précis ends, “which shows that Justinian should be our model today.” What job has that last clause begun, and why does it not belong in a précis?

4. Why is a one-word label such as “panegyric” not, by itself, a précis?
