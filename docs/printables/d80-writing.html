Remember

A circle restates the claim as support. Post hoc treats sequence as cause. A multi-paragraph outline plans a thesis, paragraph claims and grounds, and an other-side note, before the draft. One paragraph opens with its claim and explains a ground.

1. Name the three parts of a multi-paragraph outline.

2. Write one sentence that avoids both a circle and a post hoc jump, as a ground for Virgil’s offer being a rescue.

3. How does the multi-paragraph outline extend the Day 6 single-paragraph outline?

4. Why is a true claim still badly supported if the because-clause only repeats it?
