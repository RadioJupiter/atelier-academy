Remember

A quadratic graphs as a parabola, with a vertex. Tables give points. x-intercepts, when they exist, are the roots already solved. x = −b/(2a) locates the vertex’s x-coordinate. Completing the square (Day 5) is the reason, not a new trick.

Key terms: parabola, vertex of a parabola.

1. For y = x² − 4, find y(3) and say whether (3, 5) should lie on the graph.

2. For y = x² − 2x − 3, find the x-intercepts (factor or the quadratic formula from Day 5), and check that they are symmetric about x = 1.

3. For each of today's key terms (parabola, vertex of a parabola), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Sketch y = x² (vertex and two other points).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
