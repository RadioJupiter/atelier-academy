Remember

Regular -ar imperfect: -aba, -abas, -aba, -ábamos, -abais, -aban. Hablaba, hablabas, hablaba, hablábamos, hablabais, hablaban. Use it for an ongoing or habitual past action in this first look. The preterite, already taught, is for a completed event. Yo hablaba and ella hablaba have the same form. Ir, ser, and ver remain reserved. Instruction remains in English.

Key terms: imperfect of regular -ar verbs.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of hablar, and translate them as ongoing or habitual past.

2. Why do yo hablaba and ella hablaba look the same, and how can you tell them apart?

3. Write a short sentence with caminaba for a habitual action, and contrast it with ayer caminé.

4. Why is this not a first look at the preterite of hablar?
