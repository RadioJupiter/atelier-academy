Remember

A concession fairly states a real contrary point and then shows why the thesis still stands (often an although-sentence). A limit marks what the thesis does not claim. Neither is decoration, and neither is a surrender.

Key terms: concession, limit of a claim.

1. Add a real although-sentence to your Day 16 outline, and say which paragraph it belongs in.

2. Write a concluding sentence for that outline that includes a limit and still returns to the thesis.

3. For each of today's key terms (concession, limit of a claim), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Revise the whole outline so that the concession is used, not abandoned, in the points that follow it.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
