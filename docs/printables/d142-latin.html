Remember

The perfect of ēgredior is ēgressus sum, I went out, or I have gone out. It is not ēgredior, not ēgrediar, and not secūtus sum.

1. What are the six perfect forms of ēgredior?

2. Translate: Puer ēgressus est. Nōs nōn ēgressī sumus.

3. Why is ēgressus eram a miss for I have gone out?

4. For each of today's key terms (perfect of ēgredior), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
