Remember

Origin: the usually more stable attachment; insertion: the usually more movable one; a first rule is that the muscle pulls the insertion toward the origin. Tendon: the cord that often joins muscle to bone, not the belly. Landmarks today: deltoid, biceps brachii, gastrocnemius, and the Achilles tendon.

Key terms: origin and insertion, tendon.

1. In your own words, what is the difference between a muscle belly and a tendon, and why does it matter for shading?

2. Name the deltoid’s place on the figure, and one bony landmark it is tied to from Day 5.

3. For each of today's key terms (origin and insertion, tendon), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Make the short study described in the lesson (shoulder and arm, or calf and heel). Label origin, insertion, belly, and tendon as far as you can honestly see them.
