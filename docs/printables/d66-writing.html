Remember

A précis is a short, faithful restatement in your own words, keeping the author’s points, order, and proportion. It is not a quotation, not a loose summary, and not a reaction. Today’s set passage is Procopius, Buildings I.1.6, in Dewing’s translation.

Key terms: précis.

1. Why is copying Dewing’s sentence and shortening it only by leaving out a clause still not yet a précis?

2. What does “proportion” mean in a précis of this passage?

3. For each of today's key terms (précis), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a précis of the set passage in two or three sentences of your own. Do not quote more than a single short phrase, if any.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
