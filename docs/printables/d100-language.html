Remember

Pedir, preterite: pedí, pediste, pidió, pedimos, pedisteis, pidieron (e to i in the third persons only). Dormir, preterite: dormí, dormiste, durmió, dormimos, dormisteis, durmieron (o to u in the third persons only). Pedimos and dormimos keep the original vowel. Do not swap i and u, and do not use the present patterns in the preterite.

1. Give the six preterite forms of pedir and of dormir, and mark the changed forms.

2. How is the preterite stem change narrower than the present one, for these verbs?

3. Write a short sentence with pedí, one with pidió, one with dormí, and one with durmió.

4. Why are pidimos, durmimos, duermió, and dirmió all misses?

5. Translate ayer pedimos and anoche dormimos.

6. Contrast ella pide with ella pidió, and él duerme with él durmió.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
