Remember

If chords AC and BD meet at E inside the circle, AE · EC = BE · ED. Products of segments, not a claim that the chords are equal.

Key terms: intersecting chords.

1. Chords AC and BD meet at E. AE = 5, EC = 6, BE = 3. Find ED.

2. The segments of one chord are 4 and 9. One segment of the other is 6. Find the remaining segment.

3. For each of today's key terms (intersecting chords), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. AE = x, EC = 6, BE = 4, ED = 6. Find x. (Check that E can sit on both chords with these lengths.)
