Remember

F = −kx and T = 2π√(m/k). T depends on m and k, not on amplitude. A small-swing pendulum’s period depends on L and g, not on mass.

1. An invented stretch of 0.08 m produces a restoring force of size 16 N. Find k.

2. Find T for m = 0.32 kg and k = 50 N/m, as 2π times a square root.

3. If k is multiplied by 4 and m is unchanged, what happens to T?

4. State one contrast between T = 2π√(m/k) and T = 2π√(L/g).

5. What does OpenStax 16.3 say about amplitude and T?
