Remember

The Toulmin model names a claim, the grounds (data) that support it, and a warrant that joins the two. Grounds are the same job as Day 8’s evidence. A warrant is a general rule, not a restated claim.

Key terms: Toulmin model, grounds (Toulmin).

1. In your own words, what are grounds in the Toulmin model, and how do they relate to the word evidence from Day 8?

2. What job does a warrant do that grounds cannot do by themselves?

3. Write a three-part Toulmin analysis of: “The bread is stale, because it is hard, and stale bread goes hard.”

4. Why is “therefore the bread is stale” not a warrant for that claim?
