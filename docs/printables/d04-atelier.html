Atelier · Value: the nine-step scale

Week 1 · Day 4 · Thursday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Nine even steps, white to black. Squint to check. No sudden jump. Step 5 should look like a true middle gray.

Key terms: value, nine-step scale.

1. What is value, as used here?

2. Why must the steps be visually even?

3. For each of today's key terms (value, nine-step scale), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. (45 min) Make the nine-step scale. Punch a small hole in a gray card; use it to isolate each square and compare it with its neighbors.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
