Atelier · Value: the nine-step scale

Week 1 · Day 4 · Thursday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Nine even steps, white to black. Squint to check. No sudden jump. Step 5 should look like a true middle gray.

1. What is value, as used here?

2. Why must the steps be visually even?

3. (45 min) Make the nine-step scale. Punch a small hole in a gray card; use it to isolate each square and compare it with its neighbours.

4. (30 min) Make a smooth gradation 15 cm long from 9 to 1 with no visible steps.

5. (15 min) Hold your scale against objects in the room and identify the value number of five surfaces.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
