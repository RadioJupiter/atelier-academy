History · The church’s height, proportion, and light, in Dewing I.1.27–30

Remember

In I.1.27–30, in Dewing, the church soars “to a height to match the sky,” is carefully proportioned in breadth and length, and abounds in sunlight and in light reflected from the marble. No height, length, breadth, or plan figure is invented. Dewing’s “marvellous” stays inside quotations.

Key terms: proportion of breadth and length (Procopius I.1.28), light in the church (Procopius I.1.29–30).

1. Quote or faithfully paraphrase the sentence about sunlight and the marble, keeping Dewing’s idea that radiance might seem to come into being within the church.

2. Why is “marvellous” spelled that way in the quotation?

3. For each of today's key terms (proportion of breadth and length (Procopius I.1.28), light in the church (Procopius I.1.29–30)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. What image does Dewing use for looking out over the city, and why is it still not a height in meters?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
