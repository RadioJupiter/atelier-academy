Remember

Hints from the outline, outline away, three short paragraphs, then a comparison. Repair the largest miss; do not recopy for neatness only.

Key terms: reconstruction (three paragraphs).

1. Turn your Day 26 outline (or today’s new one) into a hint-list of no more than eight short labels, then put the outline away.

2. Write three short paragraphs from those hints, then list two joints you kept and one you missed or thinned.

3. For each of today's key terms (reconstruction (three paragraphs)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Repair only the missed joint, in a new sentence or a revised paragraph, not a full recopy unless the structure is genuinely broken.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
