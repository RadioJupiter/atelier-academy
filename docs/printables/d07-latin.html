Remember

The verb ending must match the subject in number. Puella videt; puellae vident. Do not leave a plural subject with est or videt.

1. What is the plural of videt?

2. Why is puellae oppidum videt wrong?

3. Replace the object with a pronoun: (a) Dominus servum vocat. (b) Puer ancillam videt. (c) Puella puerum audit.

4. Ask the question that the underlined word answers: (a) Servus dominum videt. (b) Puella cantat. (c) Puer plōrat quia ancilla eum pulsat.

5. Answer: Cūr servus dormit? Write a reason with quia (invent a sensible one).
