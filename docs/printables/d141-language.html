Remember

The present subjunctive of ser is sea, seas, sea, seamos, seáis, sean. It is not es, not será, not sería, and not vaya.

1. What are the six present-subjunctive forms of ser?

2. Translate the invented sentence “Quiere que nosotros seamos honestos,” and say why “somos” would be a miss in that frame.

3. Why is vaya a miss for today’s first look, and what remains reserved until tomorrow?

4. For each of today's key terms (present subjunctive of ser), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
