Remember

Mēcum, tēcum, nōbīscum: cum is joined after the pronoun. Mihi necesse est… is a dative of interest, ‘for me,’ not a first look at the dative of possession.

Key terms: mēcum, dative of interest.

1. Give the English of Tēcum labōrō and of nōbīscum.

2. Write one original sentence with mēcum or tēcum.

3. For each of today's key terms (mēcum, dative of interest), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. How is today’s dative of interest not a new first look at the dative of possession?
