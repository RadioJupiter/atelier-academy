Remember

The present subjunctive of sequor is sequar, sequāris, sequātur, sequāmur, sequāminī, sequantur. Sequar is spelled the same as the future; sequātur, with ā, is not the future sequētur, with ē. A purpose clause remains reserved.

1. What are the six present-subjunctive forms of sequor, and which first-person form is spelled the same as the future?

2. Translate the original sentence “Puer eam sequātur,” and say why “sequētur” would be a miss if the job is the present subjunctive.

3. Why is a purpose clause with ut a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (present subjunctive of sequor), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
