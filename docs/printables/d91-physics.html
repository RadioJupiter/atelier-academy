Remember

If damping is neglected and the motion starts from rest at amplitude X, ½mv² + ½kx² = ½kX². At the ends, all the energy is PE_el; at x = 0, all is KE. This is OpenStax 16.5, not a first look at PE_el or at conservation of energy as a kind.

Key terms: energy in simple harmonic motion.

3. Why is “the energy is ½kx² at every point” a miss?
