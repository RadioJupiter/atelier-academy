Remember

e → ie in the present, except nosotros and vosotros: pienso / pensamos, quiero / queremos, prefiero / preferimos. The endings are the ones you already know.

Key terms: stem-changing verb (e → ie), diphthong (ie).

1. Write the present of pensar in all six forms.

2. Translate: They begin the lesson on Monday. (Use empezar and el lunes.)

3. For each of today's key terms (stem-changing verb (e → ie), diphthong (ie)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Complete: Yo _____ (querer) leer; nosotros _____ (querer) escribir.
