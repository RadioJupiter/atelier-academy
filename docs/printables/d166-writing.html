Remember

A comparison sets two subjects side by side: likeness, difference, and what the difference comes to. Today’s invented types are the careful geometer and the careless geometer. No living person is named.

1. In one sentence, what job does a comparison do that an encomium or an invective alone does not?

2. Name the two invented types compared today, and give one likeness and one difference.

3. Why must this comparison not name a living person?

4. For each of today's key terms (comparison (syncrisis), a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
