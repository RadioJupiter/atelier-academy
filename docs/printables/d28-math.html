Key terms: mixed circle problems.

1. AB is a diameter of length 10, C is on the circle, AC = 6. Find BC, and find an inscribed angle that stands on the same arc as a central angle of 80° drawn on a different pair of rays in the same circle (the 80° item is independent of the 6-8-10 triangle).

2. Chords AC and BD meet at E inside a circle, AE=4, EC=9, BE=6. Find ED. Then, separately, ABCD is cyclic with ∠B=100°. Find ∠D.

3. For each of today's key terms (mixed circle problems), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A diameter AB and a point C give a right angle at C. If AC = BC, what kind of triangle is ABC, and what is ∠BAC?
