Remember

c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C, and cos C = (a² + b² − c²)/(2ab). When C is 90°, the first formula is the Pythagorean theorem.

1. State the law of cosines, and say what it becomes when C is 90°.

2. On an invented triangle with sides 4 and 4 and included angle 120°, what is the third side? Check with the law of sines.

3. On an invented triangle with sides 3, 5, and 7, what is the angle opposite 7?

4. On an invented triangle with sides 5 and 5 and included angle 60°, what is the third side?

5. On an invented 3-4-5 triangle, what is the angle opposite 5?

6. What remains reserved?
