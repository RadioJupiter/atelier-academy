Remember

Ser for what something is; estar for where and how it is. Tener que + infinitive; ir a + infinitive. Articles match gender.

1. Ser or estar: 'We are tired.'

2. How do you say 'I have to study'?

3. Conjugate ser, estar, tener, ir in the present.

4. Translate: (a) The museum is in the centre and it is very old. (b) We have to study, but we are tired. (c) Are you (tú) going to draw on Saturday? (d) They live in a small city and they write a lot.
