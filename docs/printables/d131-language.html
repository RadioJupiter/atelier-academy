Remember

Hablaría, comería, and viviría keep the whole infinitive, like the regular future, but add -ía, not -é. Hablaría is I would speak, not hablaré, I will speak. Irregular conditionals wait until later this week.

1. How is the simple conditional of a regular verb formed, and what are the six forms of hablar?

2. Write one original sentence each with hablaría, comeríamos, and vivirían.

3. Why is hablaré a miss for I would speak, and what remains reserved until later this week?

4. For each of today's key terms (simple conditional of a regular verb), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
