Remember

The imperfect of ver is veía, veías, veía, veíamos, veíais, veían, with the stem e kept. It is not vía, not iba, and not era. In this first look, veía is ongoing or habitual past seeing; vi is a completed event, already taught. Instruction remains in English.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of ver, and translate them as ongoing or habitual past.

2. How does veía differ from last week’s comía, and why is vía a miss?

3. Contrast yo veía with yo vi, yo iba, and yo era.

4. Write a short invented habitual sentence with veía, and a completed preterite sentence with vi.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
