Remember

Books 9–12 are earlier wanderings narrated by Odysseus to the Phaeacians: a story inside the story. nostos is a homecoming, still not completed. Hold a few named episodes and the design; do not treat the list as trivia or as a historical map.

Key terms: nostos, wanderings (Books 9–12).

1. Name four episodes from the usual school list of Books 9–12, in any honest order you remember, without inventing one you have not read.

2. Give one contrast between hospitality in Scheria and the Cyclops as a host, at a careful, modest level.

3. For each of today's key terms (nostos, wanderings (Books 9–12)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. How does Telemachus’s search in Books 1–4 change the way you hear a delay in Books 9–12? One or two sentences.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
