Remember

The area of a sector with radius r and central angle θ in radians is A = (1/2)r²θ, or A = (1/2)r s. It is the same area as A = (θ°/360) × πr². Do not put a radian measure into the degree formula as if it were a degree measure.

1. State the area of a sector in radians, and show in one line that it agrees with A = (θ°/360) × πr².

2. On an invented figure, r = 6 and θ = π/3, what is the sector area? What area does the degree formula give for 60°?

3. Why is a circular segment a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (area of a sector in radians), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
