Remember

Puesto is from poner, used with the already-taught helping forms of haber, and kept distinct from visto, dicho, hecho, and escrito.

1. Give the six forms with puesto, and translate he puesto and han puesto.

2. Why is *ponido a miss, and why is visto the wrong participle with poner?

3. How is ha still distinct from hay and from haya?

4. Write three original sentences, with he puesto, ha dicho, and hemos hecho, and translate them.

5. Replace puesto with escrito in the first sentence, and translate both.
