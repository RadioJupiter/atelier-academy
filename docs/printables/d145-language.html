Remember

Sea, vaya, and esté are irregular present subjunctives. They are not es, va, or está; not será, irá, or estará; and not sería, iría, or estaría.

1. Give the six present-subjunctive forms of ser, ir, and estar.

2. Choose: “Quiere que ella ___ (vaya / va / iría) al mercado.”

3. Why is “haya” a miss in a first-look sentence this week?

4. Write one invented sentence each for the present subjunctive, the future, and the conditional of ir.

5. What is the near-miss if someone writes “está” after “quiere que él,” and if someone writes “sea” for “that he go”?

6. What remains reserved?
