Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Preterite: completed — ayer comí, ayer fui, ayer vi. Imperfect: habitual, ongoing, or background — cada mañana comía, cada mañana iba, era, veía. Ayer, mientras comía, vi el cielo.

1. State the job of each tense, and give one already-taught example of each.

2. Fill: Ayer, mientras ___ (comí / comía), ___ el cielo (vi / veía). Translate.

3. Why do ir and ser share fui, but not iba or era?

4. Write one original habitual sentence and one original paired sentence. Translate them.

5. Correct the miss: Ayer, mientras comí, veía el cielo.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
