Remember

Cos A, in a right triangle, is the adjacent side over the hypotenuse. In the classroom 3-4-5 triangle, if A is adjacent to 4, cos A = 4/5, and sin A is 3/5. The ratio is the same in a similar triangle. Cosine is a ratio, not a length. Degrees only. The complementary relation waits until tomorrow.

1. What is cos A, in this lesson’s school sense, in a right triangle?

2. In a classroom 3-4-5 triangle, A is opposite 3 and adjacent to 4. What is cos A, and what is sin A?

3. A similar 6-8-10 triangle has the same angle A. What is cos A, and why is it not a new ratio?

4. How does cosine differ from sine, and what remains reserved?
