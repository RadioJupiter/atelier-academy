Remember

The present subjunctive of estar is esté, estés, esté, estemos, estéis, estén. It is not está, not sea, and not vaya.

1. What are the six present-subjunctive forms of estar?

2. Translate the invented sentence “Quiere que nosotros estemos listos,” and say why “estamos” would be a miss.

3. Why is sea a miss for “that he be here,” in this week’s work?

4. For each of today's key terms (present subjunctive of estar), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
