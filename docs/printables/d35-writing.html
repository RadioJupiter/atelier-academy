Remember

A précis says what the model does, in your words, with attribution. It is not a quotation and not a new imitatio, though you may mention those jobs.

Key terms: précis of a model sentence.

1. Write the two- or three-sentence précis set in Learn.

2. Add one sentence naming a near-miss (a hidden quotation, or a judgment you were not asked for).

3. For each of today's key terms (précis of a model sentence), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Quote the model once, correctly attributed, then write the précis without relying on that quotation.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
