Remember

f(f(x)) means f applied to f(x), also written (f ∘ f)(x). It is not multiplication, and it is not the inverse. If f(x) = 2x + 1, then f(f(x)) = 4x + 3.

Key terms: iteration of a function.

1. If h(x) = 3x, invented, what is h(h(x))? What is h(h(2))?

2. Why is f(f(x)) not the same job as f(x) · f(x) for f(x) = 2x + 1?

3. For each of today's key terms (iteration of a function), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. For f(x) = 2x + 1, what is (f ∘ f⁻¹)(x), and why is that not iteration of f?
