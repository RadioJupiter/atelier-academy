Remember

Sin A = opposite / hypotenuse. Cos A = adjacent / hypotenuse. Sin(90° − A) = cos A. Classroom 3-4-5 and 5-12-13 triangles, invented lengths. Degrees only. Tangent remains reserved.

1. In a classroom 3-4-5 triangle, A is opposite 3. Give sin A, cos A, and cos(90° − A).

2. In a classroom 5-12-13 triangle, A is opposite 5. Give sin A, cos A, and sin(90° − A).

3. Write the two definitions, and the complementary relation, in words or in symbols.

4. Why do the radian, the unit circle, and a tangent ratio remain reserved?
