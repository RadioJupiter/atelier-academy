Remember

Secūtus erō and ēgressus erō are future perfects. They are not sequar or ēgrediar, not secūtus sum or ēgressus sum, and not the pluperfects.

1. Give the first-person singular future perfect of sequor and of ēgredior, with glosses.

2. Translate: Puella ēgressa erit. Puer eam secūtus erit.

3. Why is “secūtus erat” a miss for “he will have followed”?

4. Choose: Nōs ___ (ēgrediēmur / ēgressī erimus) ante noctem, if the going out will already be completed before night.

5. Write two short original sentences, one with secūtus erō and one with ēgressus erit, using Puer or Puella as needed.

6. What remains reserved?
