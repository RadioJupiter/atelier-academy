Remember

The Parthenon is a mid-fifth-century BCE Doric temple on the Acropolis (conventional dates about 447–432). The Doryphoros is a Polykleitos type known from later copies, a developed contrapposto. A modern photograph is not a fifth-century view. No invented canon numbers.

Key terms: Parthenon, Doryphoros (Polykleitos).

1. What does “known from later marble copies” change in a claim about the Doryphoros?

2. What is a conventional date range for the Parthenon’s main construction, and how should you say it?

3. For each of today's key terms (Parthenon, Doryphoros (Polykleitos)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Name one feature of the Doric order you could point to on a photograph of the Parthenon.
