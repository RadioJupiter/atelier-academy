Remember

Present perfect: he hablado, I have spoken. Hay: there is, or there are. Haya: that there be. Regular participles only. Irregular participles remain reserved.

1. What is the present perfect in this week’s work, and how does ha differ from hay and from haya?

2. Translate “Ustedes han vivido” and “Espero que haya tiempo,” and say why the helping forms must not be swapped.

3. Why are dicho and hecho still misses?

4. Write short original sentences for he hablado, hay, haya, and hablaría, with glosses.

5. Give the six helping forms, with one regular participle each, and glosses.

6. What remains reserved?
