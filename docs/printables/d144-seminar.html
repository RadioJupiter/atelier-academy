Remember

On invented figures, a simple budget places $80 of income beside $70 of expenses, so the remainder is $10. The remainder is not a tax, not a premium, and not a deductible. The figures are not official.

1. What is a simple budget in this invented arrangement, and, on invented income $80 and expenses $40, $20, and $10, what is the remainder?

2. If the invented expenses were $90 with the same $80 income, what would the remainder be, and what would that mean for the invented plan?

3. Why is the remainder not a tax, not a premium, and not a deductible, and why are the figures not official?

4. For each of today's key terms (a simple budget (invented figures)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
