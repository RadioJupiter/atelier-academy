Remember

A ray is a straight line that originates at some point. Geometric optics treats that ray aspect of light. The law of reflection is θᵣ = θᵢ, measured from the normal, not from the surface. Refraction remains reserved.

1. What is a ray in OpenStax 25.1, and what is geometric optics?

2. State the law of reflection, and apply it to an invented θᵢ = 30°.

3. Why is the law of refraction still a miss?

4. On an invented figure, θᵢ = 40°, what is θᵣ, and from where is it measured?

5. Name the three ways 25.1 lists for light to travel from a source to another location.

6. Why is the angle between the invented incident ray and the surface not the angle of incidence?
