Remember

c² = a² + b² in a right triangle. A missing side is found from squares, not by adding the sides. If a² + b² = c², the triangle is right (the converse). 3-4-5 is not the only example.

2. Why does the similar-triangle proof begin with an altitude to the hypotenuse, not with a median?

3. Name one integer right triangle that is not 3-4-5, and one right triangle whose hypotenuse is not an integer.
