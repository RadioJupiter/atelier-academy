Remember

In the 1813 wording of Chapter 7, Longbourn is “only one mile from Meryton.” A militia regiment has arrived, “to remain the whole winter, and Meryton was the head quarters.” Mr. Bennet tells Catherine and Lydia, “you must be two of the silliest girls in the country.” “Neighbourhood” and “head quarters” stay inside quotations. The rain and the walk wait until Thursday.

1. In the 1813 wording, how far is Longbourn from Meryton, and what has recently arrived there?

2. What does Mr. Bennet say to Catherine and Lydia in the set extract?

3. Why do “neighbourhood” and “head quarters” stay inside quotations?

4. For each of today's key terms (Longbourn, Meryton, and the militia, in Chapter 7), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
