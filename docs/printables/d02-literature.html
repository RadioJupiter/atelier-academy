Remember

An epic may open with an invocation (the Muse, and the subject: the wrath of Achilles) and start in medias res (the tenth year of the war). Watch for epithets and similes. Book 1: the quarrel over prizes, the plague, Achilles' withdrawal, Thetis and Zeus. Use one verse translation consistently and cite line numbers.

Key terms: invocation, in medias res, epithet.

1. How does Book 1 connect the world of the gods with the world of the humans? Give two examples.

2. For each of today's key terms (invocation, in medias res, epithet), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

3. What is the subject announced in the first line, and why is it a surprising subject for a war poem?

4. Who is more at fault in the quarrel, Agamemnon or Achilles? Argue one side, then state the strongest point for the other.
