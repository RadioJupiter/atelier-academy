Remember

An ellipse is a foreshortened circle: no points, long and short axes. The short axis of each end lines up with the cylinder’s axis. Sides are tangent to the ellipses. Value bands run around the curve, with the axis. Measure how open each ellipse is from the viewing point.

Key terms: ellipse, axis of the cylinder.

1. In your own words, what is an ellipse in this lesson, and how is it different from a ‘football’ with points?

2. Why must the sides of the cylinder be drawn as tangents to the end ellipses?

3. For each of today's key terms (ellipse, axis of the cylinder), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Draw a standing cylinder: axis, two ellipses, sides, then light and a cast shadow. Fixed viewing point. About 35 minutes.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
