Remember

Decir: dij- (dije … dijeron), said / told. Traer: traj- (traje … trajeron), brought. No i in dijeron or trajeron.

1. Give the six preterite forms of decir and of traer.

2. Translate the invented pair: Nosotros dijimos la verdad. Nosotros trajimos el libro.

3. Why are dijieron and trajieron misses?

4. Write one invented sentence with dije and one with traje.

5. Place dije and traje beside pude, puse, supe, and quise, with a short school sense for each.
