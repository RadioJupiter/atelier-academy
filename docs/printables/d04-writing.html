Week 1 · Day 4 · Thursday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Notes are short: nouns and verbs, not full copied sentences. Rewrite from the notes only. Then compare with the original and mark what you missed.

1. What must you do before you rewrite, in Franklin's method?

2. Why are the notes not allowed to be a full copy of the sentence?

3. Read the paragraph aloud twice. Look up any word you cannot define exactly (for example Computation, Threescore, Speculation, fallow, assiduous; note that Culture here keeps its older sense of cultivating soil).

4. Count the sentences. For each one, write a hint of no more than six words on a separate sheet. Number them.

5. Put the hints in an envelope dated today. Do not reread the paragraph before Day 7.

6. In two sentences: what is Addison's purpose in this paragraph, and who is his imagined reader?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
