Remember

Dij- is decir (said / told); traj- is traer (brought). Dijeron and trajeron have no extra i. Last week’s four stems are contrasts only.

1. Give the six forms of decir and the six of traer in the preterite.

2. Translate the invented pair: Ella dijo la verdad. Ella trajo el libro.

3. What do dijeron and trajeron have in common as spellings?

4. Write invented sentences that use dije, traje, dijeron, and trajeron, one each.

5. Place this week’s two first-person forms beside last week’s pude, puse, supe, and quise, with a short sense for each.

6. Correct the misses dijieron and trajieron.
