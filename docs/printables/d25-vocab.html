A definition you can only recognize on the card is not yet yours. Give the meaning, a fresh example, and a near-miss.

1. What is a dative, in the Latin of this week, and how is it different from the accusative in Iūlia Mārcō rosam dat?

2. How is the e → i change in pedir different from the e → ie change in pensar? Give the yo form of each.

3. What is chlorophyll’s job in the leftover-light account of a green leaf, and what near-miss does “plants are green because they absorb green light” make?

4. What is the converse of a conditional, and how is the contrapositive different (including which one is equivalent to the original)?

5. In the gustar pattern, what is the grammatical subject of Me gustan los libros, and why is *Yo gusto los libros a near-miss?

6. Why is Livy a late source for early Rome, and what can a late narrative still support?
