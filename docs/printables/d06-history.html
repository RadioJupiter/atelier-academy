Remember

Thucydides 1.22 warns that the speeches are not transcripts: he keeps the general sense and makes the speakers say what the occasion demanded. In 2.37 Pericles (as reported) claims Athens is a model, a democracy of 'the many', with equal justice and office by merit. 'The many' means many citizens — not women, slaves, or metics.

Key terms: democracy, citizenship.

1. Compare Pericles' claim of equal justice with Hammurabi's sections 196–199. In which society, according to each source, did the law treat people more equally? Is that comparison fair?

2. For each of today's key terms (democracy, citizenship), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

3. Sourcing. Whose words are these, exactly? Use the 1.22 statement to decide how far we can treat them as Pericles' own.

4. Close reading. List three claims Pericles makes about Athenian democracy.
