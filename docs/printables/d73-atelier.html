Remember

The already-placed nose, as a wedge, has a lighter plane toward the light and a darker side plane that turns away, with a terminator at the turn. It is not moved, and it is not flattened to one tone.

Key terms: value on the nose, as a wedge.

1. Why must the light on the nose be the same light already used on the sockets?

2. What is not today’s job, among lashes, a new medium, and the side plane of the nose?

3. For each of today's key terms (value on the nose, as a wedge), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Where should the terminator sit on this wedge?
