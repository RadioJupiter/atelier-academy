People wait on the bank of Acheron. Charon, hoary, comes in a boat, cries woe, and is told “Vex thee not, Charon; / It is so willed…” He is the ferryman of the livid fen, with eyes of glede, and beats with his oar whoever lags. Lines 112–136 wait until Thursday.

Key terms: Charon (Inferno III).

1. Where are the people the speaker now sees, and what shore does the guide name?

2. What does Charon cry to the souls, and what does he tell the living speaker?

3. Write out, or closely recall, the guide’s “Vex thee not, Charon…” answer, and the phrase “eyes of glede.”

4. For each of today's key terms (Charon (Inferno III)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
