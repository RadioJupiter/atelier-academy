Remember

The first-voyage letter is a voyage report in Major’s 1870 wording. The Treaty of Tordesillas is a later division of lands. Neither is converted into official modern units, and later identifications are not added.

1. What kind of document is the first-voyage letter, in Major’s heading, and what does it say about thirty-three days?

2. Name the first five islands as the letter names them, including “Guanaham,” and name “La Española” with its eighteen leagues.

3. How does the Treaty of Tordesillas differ, and what official conversions and identifications must you not add?

4. Quote the “province of Cathay” clause, and say what he later learned about that land.

5. Whose wording is “harbour” and “marvellously,” and how is that wording handled?

6. What remains reserved?
