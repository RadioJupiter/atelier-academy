Remember

A heavy thunder wakes the speaker on the verge of the abysmal valley. The pallid guide leads him into the first circle. There are sighs, not lamentations, from sorrow without torment. They sinned not; baptism or right adoration is wanting; the guide is among them; “without hope we live on in desire.” Keep Longfellow’s typography. Later names wait until Thursday.

Key terms: the first circle (Inferno IV), “without hope we live on in desire”.

1. How does Inferno IV open, after the fall at the end of III?

2. What does the speaker take the guide’s pallor for, and what does the guide say it is?

3. What is heard in the first circle, and from what does it arise?

4. What two lacks does the guide name, where does he place himself, and what is the set wording of the punishment?
