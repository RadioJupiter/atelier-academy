Remember

Sequar is I shall follow; sequor is I follow; secūtus eram is I had followed. The same three-way contrast holds for ēgrediar, ēgredior, and ēgressus eram.

1. Give the first-person singular present, future, and pluperfect of sequor, with meanings.

2. Translate: Puella ēgrediētur. Puer eam secūtus erat.

3. Why is sequuntur a miss for they will follow?

4. Form the future of “we go out” and the present of “he follows.”

5. Write two original sentences, one with sequēmur and one with ēgressī erant.

6. Why must Puer or Puella, not an unquoted English identity word, mark the person in a Latin sentence of this kind?
