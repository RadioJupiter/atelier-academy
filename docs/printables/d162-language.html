Remember

Visto takes he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han, like dicho, hecho, and escrito. They are still not hay, and not haya. Other irregular participles remain reserved.

1. Give ha visto, has escrito, and hemos hecho, with glosses.

2. Correct the miss “Ellos hay visto la carta,” and say what was wrong.

3. Why is a new irregular participle beyond visto still a miss this week?

4. Write two short original sentences, one with habéis visto and one with han dicho, each with a gloss.

5. Choose: Nosotros ___ (hemos / hay / haya) visto la carta.

6. What remains reserved?
