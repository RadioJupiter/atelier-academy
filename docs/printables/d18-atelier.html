Week 4 · Day 18 · Wednesday · one-lesson review

Remember

Edges are found, firm, soft, or lost, according to the look of the set-up. A lost edge is where values nearly meet or the form turns slowly; a found edge is a clear, seen boundary. Vary them on purpose. Avoid a wire outline and a white halo.

Key terms: lost-and-found edge, edge quality.

1. In your own words, what is a lost edge, and how is it different from a messy smear?

2. Why can a white halo around an object prevent a lost edge, and what do you do instead?

3. For each of today's key terms (lost-and-found edge, edge quality), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Continue the plaster feature or the still life: a pass of edge quality around every contour and inner edge. Bring the background up where an edge must be lost. About 35 minutes.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
