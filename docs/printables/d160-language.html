Remember

Last week: he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han plus a regular participle such as hablado, comido, or vivido. This week: the same helping forms plus dicho, hecho, or escrito. Still not hay, and not haya. Visto remains reserved.

1. Give one present-perfect sentence with a regular participle, and one with dicho, hecho, or escrito, each with a gloss.

2. Choose: Ellos ___ (han / hay / haya) escrito una carta; Ella ___ (ha / hay / haya) vivido aquí.

3. Why is visto still reserved?

4. Write a short mixed set of three original sentences: regular present perfect, irregular present perfect, and a sentence with hay, each with a gloss, and say how they differ.

5. Correct the miss “Nosotros hay dicho la verdad.”

6. What remains reserved?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
