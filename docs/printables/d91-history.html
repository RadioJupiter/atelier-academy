Dewing will not claim to describe the galleries fittingly or to enumerate the surrounding colonnades. The stones are compared to a meadow in bloom, with named colors. I.1.58–60; no invented measurements. I.1.61–63 wait until Wednesday.

Key terms: galleries of the women’s side (Dewing), meadow of stones (Dewing).
