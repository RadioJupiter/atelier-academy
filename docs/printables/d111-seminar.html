Remember

Datisi is AII-3: All M are P; some M are S; therefore some S are P. M is the subject of both premises. Darapti also concludes some S are P, but from two universal premises. Ferison concludes some S are not P.

1. For each of today's key terms (Datisi), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

2. Give the two premises and the conclusion of Datisi in letters, and name the figure.

3. Invented terms: All garden tools in this box are metal. Some garden tools in this box are spades. What may you conclude in Datisi, and what must you not claim as an official fact?

4. For each of today's key terms (Datisi), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
