Remember

A result clause with ēgredior uses ēgrediātur, not ēgrediētur. A negative result is ut nōn ēgrediātur, not nē ēgrediātur.

1. Translate and label: Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur. Puer tam lentē surgit ut nōn ēgrediātur. Puer sedet nē ēgrediātur.

2. Why is ēgrediētur a miss in all three?

3. Which of the three is a purpose clause, and how can you tell?

4. For each of today's key terms (a result clause with ēgredior, a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
