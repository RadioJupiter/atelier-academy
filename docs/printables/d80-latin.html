Remember

Uterque often takes a singular verb. The present participle agrees and names a same-time action. Cum joins after mē, tē, nōbīs. Rēs is fifth declension. Inquit is inserted in speech.

2. Write one original sentence with uterque or with a present participle.

3. How is alter limited to a pair?

4. What is the English of rēs, as taught yesterday?
