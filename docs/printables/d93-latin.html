Remember

A deponent’s present participle may stand in an ablative absolute and is translated as active: Puerō loquente, magister tacet. This is not a first look at the absolute, or at the deponent in the third person. Perfect-participle absolutes remain reserved.

Key terms: deponent present participle in the absolute.

1. Translate: Puerō loquente, magister tacet.

2. Translate: Discipulīs ēgredientibus, canis sequitur.

3. Why is “with the boy being spoken” a miss for Puerō loquente?
