Remember

Use a before a specific person as a direct object (Veo a Ana). Do not use it before a thing, and do not use it with tener for possession. This a is not the a of ir a. After a question with a person, the pronoun answer is just la veo, not a la veo.

Key terms: personal a, a specific person as object.

1. Translate: We visit our teacher. We visit the museum.

2. Answer with a pronoun: ¿Buscas a Pedro? — Sí, _____.

3. For each of today's key terms (personal a, a specific person as object), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Correct: Veo a la casa. and ¿Conoces Ana?
