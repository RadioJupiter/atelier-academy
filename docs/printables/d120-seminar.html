Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Datisi; a markup then a discount, invented figures; a typical vertebra from Gray. Official lengths reserved.

1. Give Datisi in letters, and say where M stands in the third figure. Invented terms: All garden tools in this box are metal. Some garden tools in this box are spades. What may you conclude, and what must you not claim as an official fact?

2. How does Datisi differ from Darapti, and from Ferison?

3. An invented $100 takes two successive 10 percent discounts. What is the final price, and why is $80 a miss? Then: an invented $100 is marked up 10 percent, then discounted 10 percent. What is the final price, and why are $100 and $81 both misses?

4. Name the two essential parts of a typical vertebra, what they enclose, and the seven processes. Keep Gray’s spellings.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
