Ablative list: in, ex, ab, cum, sine. Accusative list: ad, ante, post, inter, prope, circum, apud, per. Towns: Rōmam / Rōmā / Rōmae. Passive: portātur, ā + agent. Three questions: ubi, quō, unde.

Key terms: case after a preposition, three place questions.

1. Give the case after cum, ad, prope, in (place where), and write one correct phrase for each.

2. Write three short sentences about Rome, answering ubi?, quō? and unde?

3. Write the passive partner of Servus saccum portat.

4. For each of today's key terms (case after a preposition, three place questions), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
