Remember

Purpose: ut or nē. Result: ut or ut nōn, often with tam, ita, or sic. The verb is the present subjunctive, not the future.

1. Translate and label: Puella manet nē eum sequātur. Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur.

2. Why is ut nōn the right negative in Puella tam lentē venit ut eum nōn sequātur?

3. Why is sequētur still a miss in both a purpose clause and a result clause?

4. Write one original positive purpose, one original negative purpose, one original positive result, and one original negative result. Translate all four.

5. Change one result sentence so that it uses ēgrediātur, and translate it.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
