Remember

A commemorative relief is later than the event it remembers. The Arch of Titus, in Platner and Ashby, was dedicated after Titus's death; the south relief shows named spoils. Not a photograph of 70 CE, and not an invented face. The 1822 rebuild also means a modern view is not simply the Flavian fabric.
