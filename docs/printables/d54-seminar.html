Remember

Unit price = total price ÷ number of same-kind units, in a labeled invented shop. Not a profit (already taught) and not a budget. Invented numbers stay labeled. No real ticket and no historical price.

Key terms: unit price.

1. In your own words, what is a unit price, and how is it different from a profit as already taught?

2. Why must the units be of the same kind before you compare unit prices?

3. For each of today's key terms (unit price), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A labeled invented pack of 8 costs $10. Find the unit price.
