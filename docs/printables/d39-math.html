Remember

If f(x) = mx + b and m ≠ 0, then f⁻¹(x) = (x − b)/m. Check by composition. The graph is a first look at reflection in y = x. This is not the logic inverse, and not 1/f(x).

Key terms: inverse of a linear function.

3. For each of today's key terms (inverse of a linear function), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
