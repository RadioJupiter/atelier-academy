Remember

Dewing I.4.9–16 rebuilds the church of the Apostles on a cross plan, the west arm long enough “to make the form of the cross,” the dome seeming to float, “though actually it is well supported.” Official lengths are not invented. Later I.4 remains reserved.

1. In Dewing’s wording, why was the old church pulled down, and what plan is given in I.4.11?

2. What does Dewing say of the arms, including the west arm, and of the roof’s resemblance to the Church of Sophia?

3. What does he say of the dome, and what official figure must you not invent?

4. For each of today's key terms (the church of the Apostles (Dewing, Buildings I.4.9–16)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
