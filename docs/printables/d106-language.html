Remember

The imperfect of ir is iba, ibas, iba, íbamos, ibais, iban, not vivía’s -ía. Use it for an ongoing or habitual past action in this first look. The preterite fui is a completed event. Ir and ser share fui, but era is not iba and waits until tomorrow. Instruction remains in English.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of ir, and translate them as ongoing or habitual past.

2. How do these forms differ from last week’s vivía?

3. Write a short sentence with iba for a habitual action, and contrast it with ayer fui.

4. Why must iba not be used as the imperfect of ser today?
