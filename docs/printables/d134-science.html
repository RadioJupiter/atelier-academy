Malachite is a green copper carbonate mineral. The USGS record HS254 gives the formula Cu2CO3(OH)2. It is related to azurite, but it is not the same mineral, and it is not ultramarine. Invent no official copper percentage. Not a fifth atelier tube.

2. How does malachite differ from azurite, and from last week’s ultramarine?

3. What official figure must you not invent, and what remains reserved?
