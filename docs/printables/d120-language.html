Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Already-taught preterite and imperfect forms, and the choice between them. Instruction in English.

1. State the job of the preterite and the job of the imperfect, and give one already-taught example of each.

2. Give an already-taught preterite and an already-taught imperfect of comer, of ir, of ser, and of ver, with a short English meaning for each. Which pair do ir and ser share?

3. Choose and explain: Ayer ___ al mercado (fui / iba). Cada mañana ___ al mercado (fui / iba). Cada mañana el cielo ___ claro (era / fue).

4. Fill and translate: Ayer, mientras ___ (comí / comía), ___ el cielo (vi / veía). Why is mientras comí a miss here?

5. Correct the miss: Ayer, mientras comí, veía el cielo. Say why it is a miss, and why vía would also be a miss for the imperfect of ver.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
