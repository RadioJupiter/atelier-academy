Remember

After vidēre, dīcere, or putāre, an accusative plus an infinitive names a second action (AcI). The accusative is the subject of the infinitive. This is not the already-taught posse + infinitive.

Key terms: accusative and infinitive (AcI).

1. In Puella avem volāre videt, what job does avem do, and what job does volāre do?

2. Write Latin for “The teacher thinks that the boy is sleeping.”

3. Why is *Puella videt avem volat not the construction taught today?

4. How is AcI different from posse + infinitive?
