Remember

f is continuous at a when f(a) is defined and the limit equals f(a). A function not defined at a is not continuous at a, even when the limit exists.

1. On invented f(x) = 2x, show that f is continuous at 0 and at 3.

2. On invented f(x) = (x² − 4)/(x − 2), why is f not continuous at 2, and is f continuous at 3?

3. State the two conditions for continuity at a point.

4. On invented g(x) = x + 1, show that g is continuous at 2.

5. On invented h(x) = (x² − 9)/(x − 3), is h continuous at 3? At 0?
