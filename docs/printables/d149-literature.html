Remember

Elizabeth does not yet see that she has become of interest to Darcy. He finds “the beautiful expression of her dark eyes,” and later names “Miss Elizabeth Bennet” as the owner of “a pair of fine eyes.” Chapter 7 remains reserved.

1. What is Elizabeth “far from suspecting,” and what had Darcy first allowed, in the 1813 wording?

2. What does he begin to find in her dark eyes, and what does he later say of “a pair of fine eyes”?

3. Whom does he name to Miss Bingley, and what does he say a lady’s imagination “jumps” to?

4. Quote the “fine eyes” sentence, and the naming of “Miss Elizabeth Bennet.”

5. How does this later part of Chapter 6 continue, rather than cancel, the opening conversation with Charlotte?

6. What remains reserved?
