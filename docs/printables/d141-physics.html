Remember

The Doppler effect is a change in observed frequency due to motion of the source or the observer. Motion toward raises the frequency; motion apart lowers it. The two official formulas distinguish a moving source from a moving observer.

1. What is the Doppler effect, and what is a Doppler shift, in OpenStax 17.4?

2. Write the official formula for a stationary observer and a moving source, and say which sign is for approach.

3. Write the official formula for a stationary source and a moving observer, and say which sign is for motion toward the source.
