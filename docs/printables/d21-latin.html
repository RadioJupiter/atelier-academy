Remember

Dative = to/for whom. First declension singular -ae; second -ō; plural -īs. The thing given stays accusative. Iūlia Mārcō rosam dat.

Key terms: dative, indirect object.

1. Write in Latin: ‘Marcus gives water to the slaves.’

2. In Iūlia Mārcō rosam dat, which word is dative, and why is Mārcum the wrong form here?

3. For each of today's key terms (dative, indirect object), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the dative singular of puella and of servus.
