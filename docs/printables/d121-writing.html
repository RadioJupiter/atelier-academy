Remember

A fable is a short invented narrative that ends in a stated moral. Townsend’s crow drops stones until the water can be reached. The moral is “Necessity is the mother of invention.”

1. What two parts make a fable, as taught today?

2. In Townsend’s wording, what does the crow finally do, and what is the stated moral?

3. Why must you not treat the moral as an official law of nature, or add a natural-history claim about crows?

4. For each of today's key terms (fable (progymnasmata)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
