Remember

A march, in this lesson, is a short invented figure in duple time, one-two, one-two. A waltz, already taught, is in triple time, one-two-three. The figure is invented. No copyrighted score, and no official instrument length, is used.

1. What is a march in this lesson, and how are its beats grouped?

2. How does that grouping differ from last week’s invented waltz?

3. Why is a copyrighted score a miss?

4. For each of today's key terms (a march in duple time (invented figure)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
