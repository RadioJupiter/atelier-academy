Remember

Dewing I.2.7–12, already taught, is reread as a sign: a figure habited like Achilles, a globe that signifies rule, “neither sword nor spear nor any other weapon,” and “a cross stands upon the globe.” Invent no official height. Do not treat “I suppose” as a proven official campaign fact.

1. What does the globe signify, in Dewing’s wording, and what stands upon it?

2. What does the figure not carry, and how is that set beside the globe?

3. What does the right hand do, and how must Dewing’s “I suppose” about the Persians be treated?

4. Why is this not a first look at I.2.7–12, and why must you not invent an official height?
