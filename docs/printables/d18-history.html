Remember

After 323 BCE the empire splits. Ptolemaic Egypt: Ptolemy’s line in Egypt, from Alexandria, to 30 BCE, using Greek and Egyptian forms of rule. Hellenistic does not mean ‘Egypt became Greece’. The Rosetta Stone (196 BCE) shows a trilingual audience; it is later than Alexander, and you need not read it this week.

Key terms: Hellenistic, Ptolemaic Egypt.

1. Give the years of Alexander’s death and of the end of Ptolemaic Egypt, and name the capital of the Ptolemies.

2. In your own words, what does Hellenistic mean in this lesson, and what does it not mean?

3. For each of today's key terms (Hellenistic, Ptolemaic Egypt), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a six-point sequence: Old Kingdom; New Kingdom (Ramesses II); Achaemenids; Alexander; Ptolemies; (Rome, 30 BCE, named only as an end point).
