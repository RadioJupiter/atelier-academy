Remember

A drying oil (such as linseed oil) hardens by a process that is not mainly evaporation. Watercolor dries mainly because water evaporates. Record a contrast of films; do not invent a drying time.

Key terms: drying oil, evaporation.

1. In your own words, what is a drying oil, and how is it different from water as a binder?

2. A painter thins oil paint with a solvent that later evaporates. Does that fact restore the claim that the oil dries by evaporation? Why or why not?

3. For each of today's key terms (drying oil, evaporation), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
