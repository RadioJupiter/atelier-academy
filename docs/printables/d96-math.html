Remember

Surface area of a right square pyramid is the base plus four triangular faces. The slant height l is the altitude of a face, not the perpendicular height h. If h and b/2 are known, l² = h² + (b/2)². Classroom lengths are invented.

Key terms: surface area of a right pyramid, slant height of a pyramid face.

1. What is the surface area of a right square pyramid, in this lesson’s school sense?

2. A classroom right square pyramid has base 6 by 6 and slant height 5. What is the surface area?

3. If the same pyramid has perpendicular height 4, how is the slant height found, and why must you not use 4 in place of l on a face?

4. A classroom right square pyramid has base 8 by 8 and slant height 5. What is the surface area?
