Remember

Celarent is the first-figure mood EAE: No M are P; All S are M; therefore No S are P. The major term P is the predicate of the conclusion; the minor term S is its subject; the middle term M links them and is absent from the conclusion. This is not Barbara (AAA), and it is not an undistributed middle.

Key terms: Celarent, major term and minor term.

1. Write Celarent’s three lines using S, M, and P, and state what the vowels E, A, E mean.

2. In “No metals are gases. All irons are metals. Therefore no irons are gases,” name the major, minor, and middle terms.

3. How does Celarent differ from Barbara, and what do the two moods share?

4. Why is an undistributed middle still a problem, if someone offers a form that only looks like Celarent?
