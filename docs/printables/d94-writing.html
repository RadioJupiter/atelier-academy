Remember

A précis of I.1.61–63 keeps the work ascribed to God’s influence, not human power or skill; the mind lifted up; the sight new each time; and the claim that no one has had a surfeit. Yesterday’s I.1.58–60 is not to be re-précised as if it were new.

1. According to I.1.61, by what is the work so finely turned, and what happens to the mind of one who enters to pray?

2. What does I.1.62 say of a later visit, compared with the first?

3. What does I.1.63 say no one has ever had, and what do people do when they leave?

4. Write a précis of I.1.61–63 in your own words, shorter than the extract.

5. Why is “tourists get tired of it” a miss?

6. How should yesterday’s meadow of stones appear today, if at all?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
