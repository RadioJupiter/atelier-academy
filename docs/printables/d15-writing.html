Remember

A summary retells, in new words and in the same order, without opinion or new facts. A précis is a tight summary that keeps the original’s proportions. No quoted clauses; no ‘I think’.

Key terms: summary, précis.

1. Write a précis of your chosen paragraph in no more than three sentences, then state the word counts of the original and of the précis.

2. Which sentence of your précis carries the move the original spent the most time on? If none does, revise.

3. For each of today's key terms (summary, précis), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Underline any wording in your précis that is still the author’s, and replace it.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
