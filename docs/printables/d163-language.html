Remember

Visto, dicho, hecho, escrito, and a regular participle such as hablado all take he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han. They are still not hay, and not haya. Other irregular participles remain reserved.

1. Give one original sentence with visto, and one with dicho, hecho, escrito, or a regular participle, each with a gloss.

2. How do those two sentences share a helping form, and how do the participles differ?

3. Why is hay a miss in either sentence if the job is the present perfect?

4. Choose the right participle: Yo he ___ (visto / vido) la carta; Tú has ___ (hecho / hacido) la tarea.

5. Choose the helping form: Ella ___ (ha / hay / haya) visto la carta.

6. What remains reserved?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
