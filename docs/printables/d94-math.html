Remember

A = 4πr², from the same OpenStax table as V = (4/3)πr³. Area is in square units; volume is in cubic units. Mixed solids use this week’s and last week’s volume formulas. Exact answers stay in terms of π.

Key terms: surface area of a sphere.

2. A classroom cone has radius 3 and height 4. What is V, in terms of π?
