A second value pass covers the whole head under one light. Shadow family together; lights simpler. Do not finish one feature and leave the neck flat.

Key terms: second-pass value.

1. List three places that belong in the shadow family on this plate.

2. Work the pass for a set time (for example, twenty minutes) without adding a new feature.

3. For each of today's key terms (second-pass value), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Name one edge you will keep lost, and one you may sharpen, with a reason from the plate.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
