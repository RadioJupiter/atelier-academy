Remember

The lids are small planes that wrap the volume of the eye, not wires. The upper lid often has more thickness, and may show a narrow light and a small underside shade if the light is from above. The lower lid is usually thinner. Do not invent a fold. Full color remains reserved.

Key terms: the lids as planes.

1. What are the lids, in this lesson’s school sense?

2. How is the upper lid judged, if the light is from above?

3. How is the lower lid judged, and what must you not invent?

4. Against what already-seen neighbors are the lids checked?
