Modern language · Spanish: ser and estar contrasted

Remember

Ser: identity, origin, characteristic, time. Estar: place and condition. Some adjectives shift sense (aburrido, listo, verde). Location always takes estar, even if the place does not move.

Key terms: ser versus estar, adjective with ser or estar.

1. Fill in: Nosotros _____ de México. El museo _____ en el centro.

2. Write two sentences with aburrido: one with ser, one with estar, and say what each means.

3. For each of today's key terms (ser versus estar, adjective with ser or estar), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Translate: She is a student, and she is at home.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
