Remember

Vuelto is the already-taught irregular participle of volver, not *volvido. The already-taught present-perfect forms are he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, and han vuelto.

1. Give he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, and han with vuelto.

2. Why is *volvido a miss?

3. Name already-taught visto and already-taught puesto, and say why they remain distinct from vuelto.

4. Write two original present-perfect sentences with vuelto, and translate them.

5. Write one sentence with already-taught puesto, and say why it is not a form of volver.
