Remember

Estar and andar share the uv-stem pattern with tener: estuve, anduve, tuve, and the rest of that ending set. Fui remains ser or ir. Instruction stays in English.

Key terms: uv-stem preterite.

1. Write the six preterite forms of estar, or say them in order.

2. Give the English of Ayer estuve en casa and of Él anduvo al parque, as invented drills.

3. For each of today's key terms (uv-stem preterite), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. What three verbs has this week grouped as a uv-stem set so far?
