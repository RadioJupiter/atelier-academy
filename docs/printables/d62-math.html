Remember

AA similarity: if two angles of one triangle equal two angles of another, the third angles are equal and the triangles are similar. The angle sum (180°) is what forces the third pair. AA does not, by itself, give congruence.

Key terms: AA similarity.

1. Why is it enough to check two pairs of equal angles, not three?

2. △ABC has angles 30°, 60°, and 90°. △DEF has angles 60°, 90°, and 30°. Are they similar? Why?

3. △GHJ has ∠G = 50° and ∠H = 50°. △KLM has ∠K = 50° and ∠L = 80°. Are they similar?

4. A student says two triangles with a pair of equal sides and one pair of equal angles must be similar by AA. What is missing?
