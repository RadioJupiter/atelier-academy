Remember

Gray 1918, from Theodora: 33 vertebræ, 7 cervical, 12 thoracic, 5 lumbar, 5 sacral, and 4 coccygeal. The upper three regions stay true, or movable. The sacral and coccygeal, in the adult, unite as the sacrum and the coccyx, and are called false or fixed. The number is sometimes increased or diminished, usually below the cervical, which are rarely other than seven. Keep vertebræ. Official lengths and a full recitation of every process remain reserved. Bartleby no longer serves the page.

Key terms: vertebral column, from Gray.
