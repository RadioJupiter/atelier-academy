A definition you can only recognize on the card is not yet yours. Give the meaning, a fresh example, and a near-miss.

1. State A = ½bh, and find the area of an isosceles triangle with sides 5, 5, and base 6.

3. Name Ferio in mood letters, and say why its conclusion is not “no S are P.”

4. Quote Longfellow’s “Not man; man once I was,” or quote Dewing’s phrase for Anthemius’s craft. (Either set text is accepted.)

5. Give the yo and él preterite of ser/ir and of hacer, and say why hizo has a z.

6. What is an authentic cadence, and how does a plagal cadence differ?
