NameDate

Remember

Alexander: 336–323 BCE; Asian campaign from 334; Issus 333, Gaugamela 331; death at Babylon. Arrian (2nd century CE) used Ptolemy and Aristobulus; attribute quotations to Arrian, and ask how he knows. These dates are much firmer than Old Kingdom years.

Key terms: Alexander of Macedon, Arrian.

1. Give the years of Alexander’s accession, the start of the Asian campaign, Issus, Gaugamela, and his death, and name the place of the death.

2. In your own words, what does Arrian say about his sources in the opening of the Anabasis?

3. For each of today's key terms (Alexander of Macedon, Arrian), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Read a short public-domain passage of Arrian on Issus or Gaugamela. Write a five-line sourcing note: who, when, of what, from whom, and one limit.
