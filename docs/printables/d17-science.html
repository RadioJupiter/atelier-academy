Remember

Pigment: solid colored particles. Binder: the substance that forms the film and holds the particles (a drying oil in oil paint). A solvent may evaporate; the binder, in oil paint, stays and hardens. Do not handle toxic historical pigments such as lead white.

Key terms: pigment, binder.

1. In your own words, what does a pigment do in a paint, and what does a binder do?

2. A recipe says ‘thin the paint with solvent’. Which part is meant to leave, and which part is meant to stay and harden?

3. For each of today's key terms (pigment, binder), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. If you made the ochre comparison, record what you see on the two smears, without forcing a conclusion on day one.
