Remember

Town names: accusative alone = to (Rōmam), ablative alone = from (Rōmā). Common nouns still need ad / ab / ex. quō? = where to?; unde? = where from?

Key terms: town names (motion), unde.

1. Write in Latin: ‘The boy goes to the country house’ and ‘The boy goes to Rome.’

2. Answer in Latin: Unde venit Mārcus? (He comes from Tusculum.)

3. For each of today's key terms (town names (motion), unde), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the ‘to’ and ‘from’ forms of Rōma and of vīlla.
