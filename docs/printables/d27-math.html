Key terms: mixed progressions.

1. In a course problem (invented), a water store is 80 L, then 40 L, then 20 L, then 10 L at four equally spaced checks. What kind of sequence is this, and what is the sum of the four recorded amounts? (The sum is a bookkeeping total of the four readings, not a claim about how much water was used.)
