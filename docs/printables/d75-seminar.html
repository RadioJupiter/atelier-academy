Remember

Palm-forward: carpus (the wrist, as a group), metacarpals (the palm), phalanges (the digits). The thumb, on the radius side, has two phalanges; the other four digits have three each. The eight carpal names remain reserved.

Key terms: carpus, metacarpals, phalanges of the hand.

1. On which side of a palm-forward hand does the thumb sit, in last week’s terms?

2. What lies immediately proximal to the metacarpals, and what lies immediately distal to them?

3. For each of today's key terms (carpus, metacarpals, phalanges of the hand), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. How many metacarpals are named as leading toward the digits, and why is that not a carpal count?
