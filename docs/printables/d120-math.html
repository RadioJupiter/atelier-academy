Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Similarity and scale factor; sine, cosine, and tangent. Degrees only. Classroom lengths are invented.

1. What is AA similarity, and why is a third equal angle not required as a separate given? A classroom right triangle similar to a 3-4-5 triangle has hypotenuse 15. What is the scale factor from the 3-4-5 triangle, and what are the other two sides?

2. In a classroom 3-4-5 triangle, A is opposite 3. Give sin A, cos A, tan A, and tan(90° − A).

3. Show that tan A = sin A / cos A in that same triangle, and say why a similar 6-8-10 triangle is not a new tangent of A.

4. In a classroom 5-12-13 triangle, B is opposite 5. Give sin B, cos B, tan B, and tan(90° − B).
