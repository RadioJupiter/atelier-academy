Remember

A wave is a disturbance that propagates from the place it was created. Wavelength λ is the distance between adjacent identical parts. Wave velocity vw is the speed of the disturbance, vw = λ / T = fλ. In OpenStax Example 16.8, λ = 10.0 m and T = 5.00 s, so vw = 2.00 m/s. The water itself, far from shore, is not what travels forward.
