Remember

A diagonal splits a parallelogram into two equal-area triangles. Each has area ½bh, the same formula as last week, now seen as half the parallelogram on that base and height.

Key terms: triangle as half a parallelogram.

1. A parallelogram has base 9 and height 6. What is the area of each triangle made by one diagonal?

2. Why does a diagonal give two equal areas, rather than two triangles that might have different areas?

3. For each of today's key terms (triangle as half a parallelogram), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A triangle has base 15 and height 4. What is the area of the parallelogram on the same base and height?
