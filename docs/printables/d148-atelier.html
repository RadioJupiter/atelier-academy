Remember

The space between two objects belongs to the still life. It is painted in the limited palette, under the same light, and is not an empty gap left white for later.

1. What is the space between two objects, in this still life, and why must it be painted?

2. How do you keep a shadow or a bounce in the interval honest?

3. Why is the study still not a portrait, and still not a fifth tube?

4. Describe a fair order: objects, interval, then quieter bounces, with reasons.

5. What happens if the interval is left as bright as the true light?

6. What remains reserved?
