Remember

Chapter 4 begins with Jane and Elizabeth alone. Jane admires Bingley’s manners; Elizabeth notes that he is also handsome, and is not convinced by Jane’s praise of the sisters. 1813 wording stays inside quotations.

1. In the 1813 wording, what does Jane praise in Bingley, and what does Elizabeth add?

2. What does Elizabeth say about compliments and about Jane’s seeing faults?

3. What does the chapter say of the sisters’ manners and fortune, and why must “twenty thousand pounds” stay the novel’s figure?

4. For each of today's key terms (the contrast of Bingley and Darcy (Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 4)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
