Remember

Dewing I.3.10–15 names a church of the Virgin at the Heraeum, now called the Hieron, “which it is not easy to describe”; a church to St. Anna in Deuteron; a shrine to Zoê; and a small, badly lighted shrine of Michael, torn down to the foundations. Invent no official length.

1. What church does Dewing I.3.10 name, and by what later name is the place called?

2. What does the same passage say about St. Anna, about Zoê, and about the shrine of Michael?

3. What official figure must you not invent, and what remains reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (the church at the Heraeum), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
