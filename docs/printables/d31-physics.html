Remember

Power is work divided by time, P = W / t. The watt is one joule per second, named after James Watt. A larger power is a faster rate of doing work, not automatically a larger force.

Key terms: power (mechanics), watt.

1. Find P if W = 150 J and t = 5 s.

2. A 40 W average rate runs for 3 s. How much work is done in that interval?

3. For each of today's key terms (power (mechanics), watt), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is 40 W not an energy?
