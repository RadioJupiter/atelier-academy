Remember

The Pyramid Texts begin, in royal pyramids, with Unas (late 5th Dynasty) at Saqqara, later than Khufu. They concern the king’s afterlife and offerings. Do not invent the wording of a spell. Use them as evidence of kingship, with the usual sourcing questions.

Key terms: Pyramid Texts, kingship (Egyptian, this period).

1. In your own words, what are the Pyramid Texts, and where and when (in dynastic sequence) do they first appear in a royal pyramid?

2. Write one sourcing question you would ask of any published translation you later open (who translated it, from what, and is it complete?).

3. For each of today's key terms (Pyramid Texts, kingship (Egyptian, this period)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Compare, in a short paragraph, Hammurabi’s stele and Unas’s pyramid as royal self-presentation. Name two likenesses and two limits of the comparison.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
