Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Three deponent tenses, the absolute, and AcI. Original sentences. No new construction.

1. Give the present, imperfect, and perfect, first person singular, of loquor and of cōnor, with a short English meaning for each.

2. Give the six imperfect forms of loquor, and translate loquēbar and loquēbantur.

3. Translate and label the tense: Ego cōnābar. Nōs locūtī sumus. Puer loquitur.

4. Why is puerō locūtō not an answer to “the boy was speaking,” and not an AcI sentence?

5. In already-taught AcI, what jobs do the accusative and the infinitive have? Write one original AcI sentence with esse, and translate it.
