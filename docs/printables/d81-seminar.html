Remember

Camestres is AEE in the second figure: All P are M; No S are M; therefore No S are P. Cesare, already taught, is EAE in the same figure. The middle term is the predicate of both premises.

Key terms: Camestres.

1. State Camestres in letters (All P are M, and so on).

2. Is this Camestres? All metals are elements. No plants are elements. Therefore no plants are metals. Why or why not?

3. How does Camestres differ from last week’s Cesare?

4. For each of today's key terms (Camestres), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
