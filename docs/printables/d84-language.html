Remember

Querer in the preterite uses quis-: quise, quisiste, quiso, quisimos, quisisteis, quisieron. The school sense here is a completed wanting, often “tried,” and the negative is often “refused.” Keep quise distinct from pude, puse, and supe.

Key terms: quise.

1. Give the six preterite forms of querer.

2. Translate the invented sentence: Ayer quise abrir la puerta.

3. In this lesson’s school reading, what can No quiso salir mean, and why is that taught carefully?

4. For each of today's key terms (quise), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
