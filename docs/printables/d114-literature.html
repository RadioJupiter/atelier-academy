Remember

Ciacco speaks of bloodshed in the divided city, of a rustic party, of a fall “within three suns,” and of two who are just and are not understood. Envy, Arrogance, and Avarice are the three sparks. He falls prone; he wakes no more this side the angelic trumpet. After the mighty sentence the torments look to be more, not less. Plutus is found, and Canto VII is reserved. Do not officially name the two who are just.
