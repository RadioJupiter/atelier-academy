Remember

Dewing I.3.16–18 rebuilds the Michael shrine, torn down last week, into “a marvellously beautiful building,” a rectangle whose “length appears not much greater than the width.” Official measurements are not invented. I.4 remains reserved.

1. In Dewing’s wording, how is the rebuilt shrine described in I.3.16, and what shape is given in I.3.17?

2. What does Dewing say about the east wall and the columns, and about the west wall?

3. Why must an official length or column count not be invented, and what remains reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (the rebuilt shrine of Michael (Dewing, Buildings I.3.16–18)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
