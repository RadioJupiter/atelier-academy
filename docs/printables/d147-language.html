Remember

Haya is the present subjunctive of haber, “that there be,” in these invented frames. It is not hay, not sea, not vaya, and not esté.

1. Choose: “Espero que ___ (hay / haya) silencio.”

2. Give the yo present-subjunctive forms of ser, ir, estar, and haber, with short glosses for this week’s frames.

3. Why is “vaya” a miss for “that there be time”?

4. Write three short invented sentences, one each with haya, sea, and esté.

5. Give a near-miss for haya from hay, and from sea.

6. What remains reserved?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
