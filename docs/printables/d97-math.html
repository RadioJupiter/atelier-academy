Remember

The lateral area of a right cone is πrl, from a sector of radius l and arc 2πr. The total surface is πrl + πr². l = √(r² + h²). Exact answers stay in terms of π. Classroom lengths are invented.

Key terms: surface area of a right cone, slant height of a right cone.

1. How is the lateral area of a right cone obtained from a sector?

2. A classroom right cone has r = 3 and h = 4. What is l, and what is the total surface area?

3. Why is πr²h, or (1/3)πr²h, a miss for today’s surface area?

4. A classroom right cone has r = 5 and h = 12. What is the total surface area?
