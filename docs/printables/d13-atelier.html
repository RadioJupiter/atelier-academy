Remember

A sphere shows the egg’s light families on a fully round form; the terminator is a curve, the cast shadow often an ellipse. Hatching builds value with strokes that follow the surface. Correct the contour before you hatch. Cross-hatching waits until Day 14.

Key terms: hatching, the sphere as a form.

1. In your own words, what is hatching in this lesson, and what is it not?

2. How is a sphere’s terminator different from a cube’s change of plane?

3. For each of today's key terms (hatching, the sphere as a form), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Draw the sphere: contour and terminator first, then hatch the families and the cast shadow. No early smudge as a substitute for hatch. About 35 minutes.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
