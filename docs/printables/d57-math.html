Remember

III.36: PT² = PA × PB. Lengths are positive. PB is P to the farther point, not the chord AB. This is a product, not a sum, and not III.35.

1. PA = 3, PB = 12. Find PT.

2. PT = 10, PA = 5. Find PB.

3. PT = 5, PA = 4. Find PB, and then AB if A is between P and B.

4. Why is the quantity PT², not PT, the one that matches PA × PB?

5. Why must you not use this theorem on the roots of (x − 4)(x − 9) = 0 just because 4 and 9 appear?
