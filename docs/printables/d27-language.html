Modern language · Spanish: este, ese, and aquel

Remember

este = this (near me); ese = that (mid / near you); aquel = that (farther). They agree with the noun. esto / eso / aquello are neuter pronouns and do not take a noun after them.

Key terms: demonstratives (este, ese, aquel).

1. Give the four forms of este (m/f, sg/pl) with a noun of your choice, and the four of aquel.

2. Write a contrast: this class (near) and that class (farther).

3. For each of today's key terms (demonstratives (este, ese, aquel)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write two sentences with ese / esa as ‘near the person you are speaking to.’

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
