Remember

Sequor, secūtus sum, and secūtus eram are present, perfect, and pluperfect. Ēgredior follows the same three-tense pattern. Locūtus eram is still I had spoken.

1. Give the present, perfect, and pluperfect, first person singular, of sequor and of ēgredior.

2. Choose and explain: Puer ___ nunc (ēgreditur / ēgressus erat). Ego ___ anteā (locūtus eram / loquor), tum tacēbam.

3. Translate: Vōs ēgressī erātis. Ego eam secūtus eram.

4. Change sequuntur and ēgredimur into pluperfect sentences, still in the same persons.

5. Why is locūtus sum a miss if the speaking was already done before a later past action?

6. Write one original sentence with secūtī erant and one with ēgressa erat.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
