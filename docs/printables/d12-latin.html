Remember

cum + ablative = with; sine + ablative = without. The five prepositions of this chapter are in, ex, ab, cum, sine, all with the ablative. Rooms: ātrium, cubiculum, tablīnum, peristylium, hortus, ōstium.

Key terms: cum and sine, rooms of the villa.

1. What does cum servō mean, and what case is servō?

2. Write in Latin: 'The children are in the bedrooms.'

3. For each of today's key terms (cum and sine, rooms of the villa), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give Latin for: with the children; without money (pecūnia); from the door.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
