Remember

Sn = n/2 · (first + last), or n/2 · (2a + (n − 1)d). Pairing is the reason. This is a sum, not the nth term alone.

Key terms: sum of an arithmetic progression.

1. An AP has a = 6, d = 3. Find the sum of the first 20 terms.

2. The first term of an AP is 7, the last of the first 12 terms is −15. Find that 12-term sum.

3. For each of today's key terms (sum of an arithmetic progression), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Find the sum of the first 15 terms of 4, 7, 10, … .
