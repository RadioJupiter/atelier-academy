Five Check items on this week’s writing methods, on set Firth wording only. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize.

1. Write one because / but / so sentence about 79 CE as a conventional event-year.

2. Quote the later discovery of Vesuvius, and attribute Firth.
