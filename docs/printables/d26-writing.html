Remember

Three jobs: thesis; one support paragraph with evidence; a close that concedes a real limit and returns to the thesis in new words. Not three unmatched blocks.

Key terms: three-paragraph outline.

1. Write a full three-paragraph outline (phrases, not yet paragraphs) on a geometry or science thesis from these two weeks.

2. Mark, in that outline, where a source or a theorem will sit, and where a concession will sit.

3. For each of today's key terms (three-paragraph outline), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Turn yesterday’s précis skill into one sentence that could open a support paragraph about the Declaration as a model of form.
