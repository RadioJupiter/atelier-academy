Remember

hic / haec / hoc = this (near). hunc / hanc are the masc./fem. accusatives. hīc with a long ī means ‘here.’ This week’s other skills: dative -ae/-ō/-īs; sē vs eum/eam; in + acc. vs in + abl.

Key terms: hic, haec, hoc.

1. Write in Latin: ‘This boy gives a rose to Julia.’

2. In Mārcus sē in hortō videt and Mārcus in hortum it, what job does each use of in (or the lack of a new preposition) have, and why is sē used in the first sentence?

3. For each of today's key terms (hic, haec, hoc), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the masculine, feminine, and neuter nominative singular of ‘this.’

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
