Remember

Darii is AII in the first figure: all M are P; some S are M; therefore some S are P. The middle term still joins the premises. It is not Celarent (EAE), and the conclusion stays particular.

Key terms: Darii.

1. Name the mood letters of Darii, and of Celarent, and say in a few words how the two moods differ.

2. In “All soldiers are brave; some of these men are soldiers; therefore some of these men are brave,” which term is the middle term?

3. For each of today's key terms (Darii), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why can a particular minor not yield a universal conclusion in this form?
