Remember

The ear is a volume on the already-placed side of the head, not a flat symbol. The rim is a narrow plane that wraps the hollow, not a wire. A likeness check of the two ears together changes only what it shows, and watches for a stamped second ear. A written pause names only what was seen or changed.

1. What is the ear, in this week’s school sense, and what must you not draw instead?

2. How is the rim judged, if the light is from above and in front?

3. Name the four things in the likeness check of the two ears.

4. What must a written pause not invent?

5. Why must the second ear be seen, not stamped?

6. How does last week’s eye as a volume help this week’s ear?
