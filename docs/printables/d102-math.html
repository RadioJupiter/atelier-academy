Remember

Lateral area of a frustum: large cone minus small cone, or π(R + r)l, with l the slant height of the frustum. Total surface: that lateral area plus both bases, πR² + πr². Classroom lengths are invented. Exact answers stay in terms of π.

Key terms: surface area of a frustum of a right cone.

1. How is the lateral area of a frustum obtained from last week’s cone surfaces, and what is the formula π(R + r)l?

2. Yesterday’s classroom frustum has R = 6, r = 3, and l = 5. What is the total surface area?

3. Why are both bases added, unlike last week’s composite, where a joined face was hidden?

4. A classroom frustum has R = 8, r = 4, and l = 5. What is the total surface area?
