Remember

The present subjunctive of a regular verb is not the indicative, not the future, and not the conditional. “Quiere que” in these invented sentences takes the subjunctive.

1. Choose: “Quiere que nosotros ___ (comemos / comamos / comeremos).”

2. Give yo forms of hablar in the present indicative, future, conditional, and present subjunctive, with glosses.

3. Why is “habla” a miss after “quiere que ella,” in this week’s work?

4. Choose: “En tu lugar, yo lo ___ (haré / haría / haga),” and say which of those forms has not yet been taught as a first look.

5. Write two short invented sentences, one with vivan and one with vivirán.

6. What remains reserved?
