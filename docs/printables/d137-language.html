Remember

Regular -er and -ir present subjunctives use -a: coma, viva. Regular -ar present subjunctives use -e: hable. Neither is the indicative.

1. Give the six present-subjunctive forms of vivir.

2. Translate the invented sentence “Quiere que ustedes coman,” and say why “comen” would be a miss.

3. Why is an -ar ending in -a a miss for yesterday’s hable, and an -er ending in -e a miss for coma?

4. For each of today's key terms (present subjunctive of regular -er and -ir verbs), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
