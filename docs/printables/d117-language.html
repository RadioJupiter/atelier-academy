Remember

The imperfect is the ongoing, habitual, or background past: cada mañana hablaba, cada mañana comía, cada mañana iba, era, veía. Iba and era are different. Veía, not vía.

1. Give an already-taught imperfect of hablar, comer, ir, ser, and ver, with a short English meaning for each.

2. Why do ir and ser not share an imperfect, even though they share fui?

3. Correct the miss: Cada mañana vía el cielo.

4. Write two original imperfect sentences, one habitual and one a description, using already-taught verbs. Translate them.

5. Which form is not an imperfect: hablaba, iba, fui, veía? Why?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
