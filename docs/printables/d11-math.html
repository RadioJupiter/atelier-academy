Remember

Straightedge and compass only. The perpendicular bisector of AB is the locus of points P with PA = PB. Copy a length with one compass opening; copy an angle by building a congruent triangle.

Key terms: construction, perpendicular bisector.

1. Describe, in order, how to construct the perpendicular bisector of a segment PQ. Why is every point of that line equidistant from P and Q?

2. You are given ∠CAB. Describe how to copy it at a new vertex A′ on a given ray.

3. For each of today's key terms (construction, perpendicular bisector), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Copy a segment of length equal to the sum of two given segments, using the compass only to transfer lengths.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
