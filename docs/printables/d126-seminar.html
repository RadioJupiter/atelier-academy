Remember

If A and not A are complementary, P(not A) = 1 − P(A). On an invented fair six-faced cube, P(not 4) = 5/6, and P(not even) = 1/2.

1. What are complementary events, and how do you find P(not A) from P(A)?

2. On an invented fair six-faced cube, give P(not 4) and P(not even), with the subtractions.

3. Why must you not invent an official probability of the weather, or treat this as a gambling lesson?

4. For each of today's key terms (complementary events), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
