Remember

Parallel + transversal ⇒ corresponding angles equal; alternate interior equal; consecutive interior supplementary. Those equalities also let you prove lines are parallel.

1. If two parallel lines are cut by a transversal and one corresponding angle is 65°, what is the other corresponding angle?

2. What do consecutive interior angles add up to?

3. Write the angle-sum proof in two columns, naming the theorem used at each step.

4. Prove the exterior-angle corollary.

5. Show that the interior angles of a convex n-gon add to (n − 2) · 180°. How many sides has a regular polygon with each angle 156°?

6. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find them.
