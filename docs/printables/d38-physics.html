Remember

Mass is in kilograms; weight is a force, W = mg near the Earth, in newtons. The same mass has a smaller weight where g is smaller (Moon: about one sixth, an approximate first figure). G is not g.

Key terms: mass (mechanics), weight.

1. Find the weight of a 5 kg mass near the Earth.

2. A weight is 49 N near the Earth. What is the mass?

3. For each of today's key terms (mass (mechanics), weight), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “a 19.6 N mass” a near-miss in this lesson’s wording?
