Remember

The preterite of regular -ar verbs uses the endings -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron on the stem (hablé, hablaste, habló, hablamos, hablasteis, hablaron). It names a completed past action. Nosotros hablamos looks like the present; habló carries a written accent. Instruction is in English.

Key terms: preterite of regular -ar verbs.

1. Write the six preterite forms of hablar, with their English meanings.

2. Write Spanish for “I bought” and “you (familiar singular) sang.”

3. Why does habló need a written accent, and why is hablamos a special case?

4. Correct: Nosotros compró pan and Ellos compró pan.
