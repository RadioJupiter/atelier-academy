Remember

A solvent carries pigments up paper at different rates, so a green leaf can show more than one band. Carotenoids may appear yellow or orange. No invented Rf numbers. This is a method, not a full safety protocol.

Key terms: paper chromatography, carotenoid (leaf).

1. In order, name the pieces of the method: extract, spot, solvent line, solvent climbing, bands.

2. What job does “cling to the paper versus dissolve in the solvent” do in the explanation?

3. For each of today's key terms (paper chromatography, carotenoid (leaf)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is a single green band not a required result, and why is it also not a failure if a particular leaf shows mainly green?
