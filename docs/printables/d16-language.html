Remember

Reflexive pronouns me, te, se, nos, os, se match the subject and usually stand before the verb. Infinitives take -se. Body parts usually take the definite article: me lavo las manos. Stem changes still apply (me despierto, me acuesto).

Key terms: reflexive verb, the article with body parts.

1. Write the present of despertarse in the yo, tú, and nosotros forms.

2. Translate: What is your name? My name is… (leave a name blank, or use a fictional one, not a real person’s name from this school).

3. For each of today's key terms (reflexive verb, the article with body parts), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Correct: Me lavo mis manos. and Levantamos nos temprano.
