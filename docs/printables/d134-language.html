Remember

Regular verbs keep the infinitive; irregular ones keep last week’s changed stems. Future endings mark will; conditional endings mark would.

1. How do hablaría and haría still use this week’s endings, and how do their stems differ?

2. Write original sentences with hablaré, haría, and iríamos.

3. Why are hacería and tenería misses, and what remains reserved?

4. Translate, invented only: Mañana lo diré. Ella saldría. Nosotros viviríamos aquí. Tú sabrías.

5. Give vendré, vendría, and comeríamos, with meanings, and label each.

6. Change hablo, hago, and voy into one future and one conditional first-person sentence each, or choose one of each verb.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
