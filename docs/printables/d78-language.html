Remember

Choose among fui (ser/ir), tuve (tener), estuve (estar), anduve (andar), and hice (hacer). They are not interchangeable. No new verb is begun.

Key terms: choosing fui, tuve, estuve, or hice.

1. Fill in, as invented drills: Ayer yo ____ en casa (estar); ella ____ tiempo (tener).

2. Why is fue not a form of estar?

3. For each of today's key terms (choosing fui, tuve, estuve, or hice), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the él/ella preterite of hacer, and say what letter appears in that form.
