Remember

Frustum volume: large cone minus small cone, or (1/3)πh(R² + Rr + r²). Frustum surface: π(R + r)l plus both bases. Similar solids: lengths k, areas k², volumes k³. Classroom lengths are invented.

Key terms: frustums and similar solids, mixed.

1. A classroom frustum has R = 6, r = 3, h = 4, l = 5. Give the volume and the total surface area.

2. Two similar cubes have edges 2 and 6. What is k, and what is the ratio of their volumes?

3. Write the frustum volume formula and the lateral-area formula.

4. Two similar cones have k = 2. The smaller has volume 12π. What is the volume of the larger?
