Remember

Regular -er and -ir preterites share endings; regular -ar is the other set. hablé versus comí or viví. ayer, anoche, el lunes pasado still mark the past.

1. Write: “Last night they lived there.”

2. Why is *Ayer hablí a miss?

3. Hear habló, comió, and vivió, then use each in a short sentence.

4. This sitting does not begin ser or ir in the past.

5. Copy one pair of sentences that show the shared -er / -ir ending.
