Remember

Irregular yo: hago, pongo, salgo, traigo, vengo, digo, conozco, sé, veo, doy (plus soy, estoy, voy, tengo). Other persons are often regular. Saber = know a fact or how; conocer = be acquainted; conozco a someone uses the personal a.

Key terms: irregular yo form, saber and conocer.

1. Write the present of poner and of ver, all six forms.

2. Translate: I leave at five; I bring the book; I do the work.

3. For each of today's key terms (irregular yo form, saber and conocer), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Complete: Yo no _____ (saber) nadar. ¿_____ (conocer, tú) a Pedro?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
