Remember

A tangent meets the circle once, and is perpendicular to the radius at that point. The two tangent segments from an outside point are equal. OA² + AP² = OP².

Key terms: tangent to a circle, two tangents from a point.

1. A tangent from P has length 15, and the radius is 8. Find OP.

2. Two tangents from P touch a circle at A and B. If PA = 9, what is PB? Why?

3. For each of today's key terms (tangent to a circle, two tangents from a point), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A radius is 12 and a tangent from P has length 16. Find OP, then find the area of △OAP.
