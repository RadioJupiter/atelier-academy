Remember

KE = ½mv², in joules. W_net = ΔKE. Doubling speed quadruples KE. Negative net work reduces KE. Momentum is a different quantity.

Key terms: kinetic energy, work–energy theorem.

1. 18 J of net work is done on a 4 kg object that starts at rest. Find its final speed.

2. A 2 kg object slows from 5 m/s to rest. Find the net work done on it.

3. For each of today's key terms (kinetic energy, work–energy theorem), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Find the KE of a 6 kg object at 2 m/s.
