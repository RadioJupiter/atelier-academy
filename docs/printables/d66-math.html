Remember

In a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse (the side opposite the right angle). A 3-4-5 triangle satisfies 9 + 16 = 25. The theorem is about right triangles, not about every triangle.

Key terms: Pythagorean theorem, hypotenuse.

1. A right triangle has legs 5 and 12. Find the hypotenuse.

2. A triangle has sides 3, 5, and 6. Does the Pythagorean theorem say that it is right-angled? Why or why not?

3. For each of today's key terms (Pythagorean theorem, hypotenuse), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A right triangle has one leg 9 and hypotenuse 15. Find the other leg.
