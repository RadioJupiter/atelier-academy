Remember

The area is (1/2)ab sin C, with C the included angle between sides a and b. When C is 90°, this is the already-taught (1/2)ab. Lengths in the examples are invented.

1. What is the area of a triangle in terms of two sides and the included angle, and what happens when that angle is 90°?

2. An invented triangle has sides 6 and 6 and included angle 60°. What is its area?

3. Why must the 6 and the 60° be called invented, and what remains reserved?

4. An invented triangle has sides 4 and 8 and included angle 30°. What is its area?

5. Why can the same triangle’s area also be written (1/2)bc sin A?

6. If someone uses (1/2)ab and ignores the included angle when it is not 90°, what is wrong?
