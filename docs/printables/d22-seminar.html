Remember

Natural minor: W H W W H W W (half steps 2–3 and 5–6). Relative minor shares a major scale’s pitch set and starts on degree 6. A minor is relative to C major. Not parallel minor (same tonic), and not harmonic minor today.

Key terms: natural minor scale, relative minor.
