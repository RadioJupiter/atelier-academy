A definition you can only recognize on the card is not yet yours. Give the meaning, a fresh example, and a near-miss.

1. On the unit circle, what are cos θ and sin θ, and what is the point at 30°, at 150°, and at 330°?

2. Give the six present forms of sequor, and say how sequor differs from locūtus eram.

3. Give the yo future forms of hablar, hacer, tener, and querer, and choose: Mañana yo lo ___ (hago / haré).

4. What is intensity I, and what is E in Example 16.9? If two waves of 1.00 W/m² interfere perfectly constructively, why is the result 4.00 W/m², not 2.00?

5. What is lapis lazuli, in the USGS sources, and how does ultramarine differ from the rock? What official figure must you not invent?

6. In Inter Caetera, May 4, 1493, what line is drawn, and from what islands is it measured? What official conversion must you not invent?
