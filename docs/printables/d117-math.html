Remember

Sin A is opposite over hypotenuse; cos A is adjacent over hypotenuse. Sin(90° − A) = cos A. In a classroom 3-4-5 triangle, if A is opposite 3, sin A = 3/5 and cos A = 4/5. Degrees only.

1. In a classroom 3-4-5 triangle, A is opposite 3. Give sin A, cos A, and sin(90° − A).

2. Why is a 6-8-10 triangle with the same angle A not a new sine?

3. Why is sin A = 3 a miss?

4. In a classroom 5-12-13 triangle, D is opposite 12. Give sin D and cos D.

5. Why must you not invent an official degree measure for A today?
