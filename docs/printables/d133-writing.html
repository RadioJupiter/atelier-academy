Remember

A short original chreia: a named person, a brief occasion, and a saying or deed that fits, invented or taken from a set text already taught. No living person, and no fable’s moral tag.

1. Write one short original chreia, with a person, an occasion, and a saying or deed. Label invented figures invented.

2. Explain how your piece is a chreia, and not a fable or a long narrative.

3. What official claim must you not invent, and what remains reserved?

4. Rewrite yesterday’s Diogenes scene as a three-part chreia in your own words, still quoting the 1859 saying.

5. Why must a living person not be the named figure?

6. Why is a stated moral tag still a miss?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
