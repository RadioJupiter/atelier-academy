Remember

Franklin’s method: brief hints in order, then a reconstruction compared with Longfellow. A précis: a short, faithful restatement in your own words of Dewing’s points, order, and proportion. Neither method is a first look this week.

1. What are the Franklin steps used this week on Inferno III.1–9?

2. Write one sound hint for “All hope abandon, ye who enter in!” and say why the whole line is not itself a hint.

3. Name two points a précis of I.1.50–53 must still have, and two a précis of I.1.54–57 must still have.

4. Why is “the gold outshines the stones” a miss in a précis of I.1.54?

5. Why must a measured height of the piers not appear in either précis?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
