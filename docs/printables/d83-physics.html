Remember

T = 2π√(m/k), from OpenStax 16.3. T grows with √m and shrinks as k grows. T does not depend on amplitude. A simple pendulum’s small-swing period does not depend on mass; this one does.

Key terms: period of a mass-spring, simple harmonic motion.

1. An invented mass of 0.45 kg is on a spring with k = 20 N/m. Find T in the form 2π times a square root.

2. If m is multiplied by 4, and k is unchanged, what happens to T?

3. Name one way this period differs from the simple-pendulum period taught last week.

4. For each of today's key terms (period of a mass-spring, simple harmonic motion), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
