Atelier · Cast drawing 1: set-up and block-in

Week 2 · Day 7 · Tuesday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

One light, a marked viewing point, sight-size if you can arrange it. Block height, width, and large shapes. No modeling yet.

1. What is today's job on the cast, and what is not yet the job?

2. Why mark the viewing point on the floor?

3. (15 min) Set up and photograph the arrangement. Make two small two-value thumbnails to choose the view.

4. (65 min) Sight-size block-in: overall height and width first, envelope, plumb alignments, then the shadow shapes as lines. No tone.

5. (10 min) Mirror check and a measured check of three proportions.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
