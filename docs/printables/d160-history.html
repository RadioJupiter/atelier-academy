Remember

The letter dates the landfall “On the first Sunday after All Saints, namely, the third of November, about dawn.” A pilot of the ship Capitana cried out “The reward, I see the land!” Those words are the letter’s, confirmed from the 1870 page.

1. How does the second-voyage letter, in Major’s 1870 wording, date the landfall, and what does the pilot cry?

2. What ship does the letter name in that sentence?

3. Why is adding a later official island name a miss in this extract?

4. For each of today's key terms (the third of November, “The reward, I see the land!”), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
