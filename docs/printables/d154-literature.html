Remember

In the 1813 wording, Jane is sent “on horseback, because it seems likely to rain,” and “it rained hard.” Elizabeth walks to Netherfield: “only three miles.” She arrives “with weary ancles, dirty stockings,” and “was shewn into the breakfast-parlour.” “Ancles” and “shewn” stay inside quotations. Chapter 8 remains reserved.

1. In the 1813 wording, why is Jane sent on horseback, and what happens after she has gone?

2. What does Elizabeth say about the distance, and with what wording does she arrive?

3. Why do “ancles” and “shewn” stay inside quotations?

4. For each of today's key terms (Jane’s ride in the rain, and Elizabeth’s three miles, in Chapter 7), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
