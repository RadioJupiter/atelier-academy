Remember

A deponent looks passive and is translated as active. Today: loquitur, loquuntur, loquī; cōnātur, cōnantur, cōnārī. First- and second-person deponent forms are reserved. Cōnārī often takes an infinitive.

Key terms: deponent verb, loquitur and cōnātur.

1. What is a deponent verb, in this lesson’s school sense?

2. Translate: Puerī loquuntur. Discipulus recitāre cōnātur.

3. Why is “he is spoken” a miss for loquitur?

4. For each of today's key terms (deponent verb, loquitur and cōnātur), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
