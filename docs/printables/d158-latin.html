Remember

Ut sequātur and ut ēgrediātur are purpose clauses, with ā. Sequētur and ēgrediētur are futures, with ē. Sequor is to follow; ēgredior is to go out.

1. Gloss “Puella venit ut eum sequātur” and “Puer ēgrediētur,” and name the job of each verb.

2. Why is ut sequētur a miss if the job is a purpose clause?

3. Why is ut sequātur a miss if the purpose is going out?

4. Write four short original sentences using ut sequātur, sequētur, ut ēgrediātur, and ēgrediētur, with glosses.

5. Give the third-person singular purpose-clause form of both verbs, with glosses.

6. What remains reserved?
