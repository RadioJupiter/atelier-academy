Remember

A commonplace amplifies a general fault. An encomium praises a type or an already-taught work. Do not substitute one job for the other, and do not name a living person.

1. What is an encomium in this course, and how does it differ from a commonplace?

2. What invented subject was praised this week, and what already-taught reason was used?

3. Why must a living person not be named?

4. Write a short mixed piece: a brief commonplace on last week’s type, then a brief encomium on this week’s subject, clearly distinguished.

5. Name one near-miss for each job.

6. What remains reserved?
