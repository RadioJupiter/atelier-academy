Remember

Rotational equilibrium about a point means Στ = 0: clockwise and counterclockwise torques balance. A body that stays at rest also has ΣF = 0. Torque is still τ = F × d⊥, in N·m, not joules. The market sense of equilibrium is a different word.

Key terms: rotational equilibrium.

1. State the two conditions used today for a rigid body that remains at rest, and name the unit of torque.

2. A 3.00 m light rod is supported at its center. 40 N downward at the left end is already applied. Find the downward force at the right end that gives Στ = 0, and the support force if ΣF = 0.

3. A force of 10 N acts downward through the support itself. What torque does it give about that support, and why?

4. Why is a joule the wrong name for the unit in this lesson, even though both joules and newton-meters are a force times a distance?
