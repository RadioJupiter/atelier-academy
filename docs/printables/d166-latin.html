Remember

A result clause with ut marks what happens as a result and often has tam, ita, or sic in the main clause. Puella tam celeriter venit ut eum sequātur: she comes so quickly that she follows him. It is not a purpose clause, and not the future sequētur. A negative result with ut nōn waits until tomorrow.

1. Translate and label: Puella tam celeriter venit ut eum sequātur. Puella venit ut eum sequātur.

2. Why is sequētur a miss in either clause?

3. What job does tam do in today’s result sentence?

4. For each of today's key terms (a result clause with ut, a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
