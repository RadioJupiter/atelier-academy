Remember

Days other than the named day and prīdiē are counted backward inclusively from the next Kalendae, Nonae, or Īdūs. For Nones or Ides, add one to the day they fall on, then subtract the Roman number. IV. Nōn. Mār. = 4 March; IV. Īd. Sept. = 10 September.

Key terms: inclusive counting in Roman dates.

2. State the inclusive rule for a date counted from the Nones or the Ides.

3. For each of today's key terms (inclusive counting in Roman dates), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
