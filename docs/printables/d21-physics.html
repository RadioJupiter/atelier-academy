Remember

W = F s cosθ. Along the motion, W = F s. Opposite, W is negative. Perpendicular, W = 0. The unit is the joule (1 J = 1 N·m). Holding a still load is not work in this sense.

Key terms: work, joule.

1. A 5 N force acts opposite a 4 m displacement. Find the work, including the sign.

2. You hold a 30 N box still for ten seconds. How much work does your upward force do on the box? Explain.

3. For each of today's key terms (work, joule), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A 12 N force along an 8 m displacement: find W.
