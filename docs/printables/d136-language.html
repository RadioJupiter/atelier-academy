Remember

The present subjunctive of hablar is hable, hables, hable, hablemos, habléis, hablen. It is not habla, not hablará, and not hablaría.

1. What are the six present-subjunctive forms of hablar?

2. Translate the invented sentence “Quiere que nosotros hablemos,” and say why “hablamos” would be a miss in that frame.

3. Why is hablaría a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (present subjunctive of a regular -ar verb), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
