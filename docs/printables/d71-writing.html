Remember

A fallacy is a flaw in reasoning, not merely a false statement. A true conclusion can still have been reached by a bad route. This week’s later sittings name three patterns: a straw man, a false dilemma, and ad hominem.

Key terms: fallacy.

1. Is a wrong arithmetic step, by itself, the same thing as the kind of fallacy this lesson is naming? Explain.

2. What job does a warrant do, in week 13’s terms, that a fallacy often fakes or skips?

3. For each of today's key terms (fallacy), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Invent (and label invented) one short argument whose conclusion is true but whose reason does not establish it. Name the missing test or warrant.
