Remember

Locus: the set of points that satisfy a condition. Circle: distance r from O. Perpendicular bisector of AB: PA = PB. The compass construction with a same opening finds that bisector. The circle on diameter AB is a different locus.

Key terms: locus, perpendicular bisector as a locus.

1. Describe, as a locus, the circle of radius 6 cm about a point C.

2. Why is the midpoint of AB on the perpendicular-bisector locus?

3. For each of today's key terms (locus, perpendicular bisector as a locus), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A point Q is 5 cm from A and 7 cm from B. Can Q lie on the perpendicular bisector of AB? Why?
