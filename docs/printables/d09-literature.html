Remember

An analytical essay of 400–600 words: a thesis that can be argued against; a single-paragraph outline for each of three body paragraphs; two quotations per paragraph, with book and line numbers; at least one although sentence; every quotation introduced and explained. Choose one of the three prompts. This is not a retelling.

Key terms: thesis, analytical essay.

1. Why must every quotation in this essay be introduced and then explained, not left to 'speak for itself'?

2. For each of today's key terms (thesis, analytical essay), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
