Remember

A participial phrase modifies a noun (often the subject). If the implied subject of the phrase is not that noun, the modifier dangles. Repair by attaching it, or by rewriting as a clause.

Key terms: participial phrase, dangling modifier.

1. Is this a dangling modifier? Given a diameter AB, the angle at C is 90°. Explain.

2. Add a participial phrase to The cyclic quadrilateral has opposite angles that sum to 180°, correctly attached.

3. For each of today's key terms (participial phrase, dangling modifier), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Repair: Pairing the first term with the last, the sum of the AP is easy to see. (Ask: who is pairing?)
