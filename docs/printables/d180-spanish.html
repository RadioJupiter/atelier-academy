Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Dicho, hecho, escrito, visto, puesto, and vuelto. No new participle. Present-perfect subjunctive reserved.

1. Match dicho, hecho, escrito, visto, puesto, and vuelto to their already-taught verbs.

2. Give he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, and han, first with visto, then with puesto, then with vuelto.

3. Why are vido, ponido, and *volvido misses?

4. Write three short present-perfect sentences, one with visto, one with puesto, and one with vuelto, and translate them.

5. Why does the present-perfect subjunctive remain reserved, and why is no new participle opened on this examination?
