Remember

To bisect ∠BAC: equal arcs from A cut the sides at D and E; equal arcs from D and E meet at F; AF bisects the angle (SSS). To draw a parallel to ℓ through P: copy a corresponding or alternate interior angle along a transversal through P.

Key terms: angle bisector, parallel through a point.

1. Describe how to bisect ∠PQR with straightedge and compass, and give the congruence that proves the two halves are equal.

2. Describe how to construct a line through a point S, not on line m, parallel to m. Which theorem says the lines are parallel?

3. For each of today's key terms (angle bisector, parallel through a point), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Bisect a straight angle. What familiar line do you obtain, relative to the original line?
