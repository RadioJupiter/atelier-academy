Remember

An income tax is a named percent taken from invented income, not added to a price. 10 percent of $500 is $50, remaining $450. The rate is invented, not official. $550 would treat the tax as a sales tax.

1. What is an income tax, in this first look, and how does it differ from last week’s sales tax?

2. On an invented $500 at an invented 10 percent, what is the tax, and what remains? Why is $550 a miss?

3. Why must you not treat 10 percent as an official living rate?

4. For each of today's key terms (income tax (invented figures)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
