Remember

Make short hints that keep Longfellow’s points and order in IV.25–42: sighs, not lamentations; sorrow without torment; they sinned not; baptism or right adoration wanting; the guide is among them; “without hope we live on in desire.” Do not invent a later name. Reconstruction waits until tomorrow.

Key terms: Franklin hints from Inferno IV.25–42.

1. What is today’s job in Franklin’s method, and what waits until tomorrow?

2. What is heard in the first circle, and from what does it arise?

3. What two lacks does the guide name, and where does he place himself?

4. Write a short hint-list, in order, for IV.25–42.
