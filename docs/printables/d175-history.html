Remember

The letter names Turuqueira, Ceyre, and Ayay. Last week recorded twenty-five leagues, a port late in the evening, and a resolve to land at break of day.

1. Name the three islands, and say which was the first that they saw.

2. What did last week’s letter extract record about length, a port, and a resolve, without converting the leagues?

3. Why is a footnote still not the body?

4. Write a short account that places the three names after last week’s port, without reserved later sentences and without official identifications.

5. Say what remains reserved.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
