I.2.1–6: the Augusteum before the Senate House; not less than seven courses, used as seats; a column not a monolith; brass that, “in its colour, is softer than pure gold”; a bronze horse facing toward the east. The Emperor’s figure waits until Wednesday. Invent no official height. Quote “colour.”

1. What is the Augusteum, in Dewing I.2.1, and what structure stands there?

2. How many courses does Dewing name, and how are they used?

3. What must you keep about the column, and about the brass, in Dewing’s wording?

4. Which way does the horse face, and why does the Emperor’s figure still wait?
