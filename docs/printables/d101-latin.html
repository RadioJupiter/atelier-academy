Remember

Loquor, loqueris, loquitur, loquimur, loquiminī, loquuntur: I speak, you speak, and so on, looking passive and translated as active. Cōnor, cōnāris, cōnātur follow the same pattern. The perfect remains reserved. Classroom sentences only.

Key terms: first- and second-person present deponents.

1. Give the six present forms of loquor, and translate them.

2. Give the six present forms of cōnor, and translate cōnāris and cōnāmur.

3. Why do these forms look passive and get an active translation?

4. Translate Ego loquor Lātīnē and Vōs loquiminī.
