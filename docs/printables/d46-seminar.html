Remember

Modus ponens: If P then Q; P; therefore Q. Modus tollens: If P then Q; not Q; therefore not P. Not affirming the consequent, and not denying the antecedent.

Key terms: modus ponens, modus tollens.

1. Write a modus ponens argument with the square-and-rectangle conditional.

2. Why is “It is not a square; therefore it is not a rectangle” not modus tollens?

3. For each of today's key terms (modus ponens, modus tollens), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Redo today's Together task in your own words, without copying the model.
