Remember

C = 2πr. A = πr². Sector: (θ/360) × πr². Ring: π(R² − r²), not π(R − r)². Exact answers stay in terms of π.

1. A classroom circle has radius 9. Give C and A, in terms of π.

2. A 72° sector has radius 5. What is its area, in terms of π?

3. A ring has R = 7 and r = 3. What is its area, in terms of π? Why is 16π the wrong value here?

4. A circle has area 81π. What is its circumference, in terms of π?

5. Using π ≈ 3.14, estimate the circumference of a classroom circle of radius 5, and label the estimate.
