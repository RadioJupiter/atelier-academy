Remember

Insurance, here, is an invented premium paid in advance, and an invented payment if a named event happens. Twelve times $10 is $120. The $500 is invented, not official. This is not a tax, and not a gambling lesson.

1. What is insurance, in this first look, and how does a premium differ from last week’s income tax?

2. On the invented figures, what is the year’s premium, and when would the $500 be paid?

3. Why must you not treat the arrangement as an official policy, or as a gambling lesson?

4. For each of today's key terms (insurance (invented figures)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
