Remember

Perfect, future, present, and pluperfect of sequor and of ēgredior must not be swapped, and the two verbs must not be swapped either.

1. Give secūtī estis and ēgressa erat, with meanings.

2. Translate: Ego nōn ēgrediar. Amīcī mē secūtī sunt.

3. Why is ēgrediuntur a miss for they have gone out?

4. Put sequeris, sequēris, secūtus es, and secūtus erās in four short original sentences, and name the tense of each.

5. Write one original sentence that uses both ēgressus sum and sequētur, with the times kept distinct.

6. What still remains reserved, and is not today’s job?
