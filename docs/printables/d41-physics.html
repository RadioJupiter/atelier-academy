Remember

Velocity along the tangent. Constant speed can still mean changing velocity. A center-seeking (centripetal) force is required to stay on the circle. No invented orbital speed, and no invented string measurements.

Key terms: circular motion, centripetal.

1. At a point on a circle, name the direction of the velocity and the direction of the centripetal force.

2. Why is constant speed not enough to say “there is no acceleration”?

3. For each of today's key terms (circular motion, centripetal), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is centripetal not “a force that flings the ball outward” in this lesson?
