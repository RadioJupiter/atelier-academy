Remember

Nominative and accusative. Agreement of adjectives. Verb endings for number. Salvē / salvēte. Read first, then say what the sentence tells you.

1. In puellae oppidum magnum vident, why is the verb vident?

2. What is the difference between salvē and salvēte?

3. Give nominative plural, genitive singular and accusative singular: fluvius, insula, oppidum, puer.

4. Translate into Latin: (a) The islands of Greece are not large. (b) Why does the slave call the master? (c) Slave, listen! (d) The girl sees the boy, but the boy does not see her.

5. Correct the error in each: (a) Dominus servus vocat. (b) Ancilla, venit! (c) Oppida magnī sunt.
