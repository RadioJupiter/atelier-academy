Remember

At 45°, P is (√2/2, √2/2). At 30°, P is (√3/2, 1/2). At 60°, P is (1/2, √3/2). Scale the already-taught triangles to hypotenuse 1; do not treat a decimal as a replacement of the exact value.

1. Derive P at 45°, and give sin 45° and cos 45°.

2. Derive P at 30° and at 60°, and give sine and cosine of each.

3. Why must you scale the triangles, and why is a decimal not a replacement of √2/2?

4. For each of today's key terms (sine and cosine of 30°, 45°, and 60° on the unit circle), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
