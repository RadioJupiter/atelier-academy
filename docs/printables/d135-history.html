Remember

Tordesillas, June 7, 1494: 370 leagues west of the Cape Verde Islands; east to Portugal, west to Castile, forever. Last week’s May 4 bull: 100 leagues from the Azores and Cape Verde.

1. What line does the Treaty of Tordesillas draw, and on what date was it concluded?

2. Who holds lands east of the line, and who holds lands west of it? What if Castile discovers land to the east?

3. How does the measure differ from last week’s May 4 bull, and what official conversion must you not invent?

4. Quote, or closely paraphrase, the 370-league wording, and the “forever” wording for Portugal.

5. Why is Davenport’s introduction, on Portugal finding the hundred-league limit too narrow, not itself the treaty?

6. What remains reserved?
