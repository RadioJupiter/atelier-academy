Remember

Undefined terms, axioms, definitions, then theorems. Every step in a proof needs a reason. Do not assume a picture proves the claim; the picture only helps you see it.

Key terms: axiom, theorem, vertical angles.

1. Lines AC and BD meet at O, and ∠AOB = 70°. What is the vertical angle to ∠AOB, and what is an adjacent angle on the straight line? Give reasons.

2. Two adjacent angles on a straight line measure 3x and x + 20°. Find x and the two angles.

3. For each of today's key terms (axiom, theorem, vertical angles), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. One of the four angles formed by two intersecting lines is 40° greater than another. Find all four angles.
