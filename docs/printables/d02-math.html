Remember

Undefined terms, axioms, definitions, then theorems. Every step in a proof needs a reason. Do not assume a picture proves the claim; the picture only helps you see it.

1. Name two things a step in a proof may use as its reason.

2. Does a careful drawing, by itself, count as a proof?

3. One of the four angles formed by two intersecting lines is 40° greater than another. Find all four angles.

4. Prove that the bisectors of two adjacent supplementary angles are perpendicular.

5. Prove that the bisectors of two vertical angles lie on one straight line.

6. Three lines pass through one point. How many pairs of vertical angles are formed? (Count only angles smaller than 180°.)
