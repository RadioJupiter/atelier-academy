Remember

The future perfect marks a later completion. Agreement of the participle still matters, and sequor must not be swapped with ēgredior.

1. Translate: Puer mē secūtus erit. Nōs nōn ēgressī erimus.

2. Give the six future-perfect forms of sequor and of ēgredior.

3. Why must Puer or Puella, not an unquoted English identity word, mark the person in a Latin sentence of this kind?

4. Choose: Puella ___ (ēgrediētur / ēgressa erit / ēgressa est) ante nōs, if she will already have gone out before we do.

5. Write one original sentence in which a following will already be completed later, and one in which a going out is still only ahead.

6. What remains reserved?
