Remember

Part-whole: A + B = W. Comparison: larger = smaller + difference. Change: start ± change = end. Draw the bars before you write the equation.

Key terms: bar model, remainder, ratio.

1. A chemist used 3/8 of a bottle in the morning and 1/5 of the remaining solution in the afternoon. 28 mL were left. How much was in the bottle at the start?

2. Pencils and pens were in the ratio 5 : 8. After 12 more pencils were bought, there were equally many pencils and pens. How many pencils were there at first?

3. For each of today's key terms (bar model, remainder, ratio), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A painter used 2/7 of a tube of white on Monday and 1/3 of what remained on Tuesday. 20 mL were left. How much was in the tube?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
