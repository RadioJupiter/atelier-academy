ir (present) + a + infinitive: ‘to be going to…’ The a is required. Still not a past tense.

Key terms: ir a + infinitive.

1. Write the tú and usted (or él/ella) forms of ir a leer.

2. Write one question with ¿Qué vas a… and a short answer.

3. For each of today's key terms (ir a + infinitive), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write: “She is going to ask for water” and “You (plural, vosotros) are going to practice.” If you have not used vais much, you may also write the ustedes form, and label which you chose.
