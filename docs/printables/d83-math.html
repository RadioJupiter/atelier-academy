Remember

A sector is bounded by two radii and an arc. Its area is (θ/360) × πr², in degrees. The matching arc length is (θ/360) × 2πr. A central angle is not a first look.

Key terms: sector of a circle.

1. A 90° sector has radius 10. What is its area, in terms of π?

2. A sector of radius 8 has area 16π. What is its central angle, in degrees?

3. How is a sector different from a triangle formed by two radii and a chord?

4. For each of today's key terms (sector of a circle), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
