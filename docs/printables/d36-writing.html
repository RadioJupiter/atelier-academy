A short quotation is introduced, attributed, and explained. It does not replace the topic sentence. No ending of the play before Thursday’s literature lesson.

Key terms: integrated quotation.

1. Write a two-sentence frame (topic sentence + a later explanation) with a gap marked for a short quotation, then fill the gap with the Bacon opening.

3. For each of today's key terms (integrated quotation), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
