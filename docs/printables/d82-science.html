Remember

Density is m / V. Ice floats because it is less dense than liquid water. At 0 °C, NIST’s figures used here are 0.9167 g/cm³ for ice Ih and 0.99984 g/cm³ for the liquid. Do not invent a blended official percentage.

Key terms: density, why ice floats.

3. Why does this lesson refuse a single official “percent less dense” figure?
