Remember

A quotation keeps the wording and names the source. A précis restates the points faithfully in your own words. A loose summary can drop or rearrange until the author’s shape is gone. Keep the three jobs distinct.

1. Write one quotation (a short phrase only), one précis sentence, and one loose-summary sentence of the set passage, and label each.

2. Why must a quotation of Dewing keep “shewing” or any other wording only if you are actually quoting that word — and why will a précis use American spelling?

3. A student puts quotation marks around a précis. What has gone wrong?

4. This sitting does not invent a later section of the chapter.
