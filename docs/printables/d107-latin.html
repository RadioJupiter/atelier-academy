Remember

Use locūtus sum and cōnātus sum with the person of sum matching the subject, and with the participle agreeing in number. The present is a different tense. The absolute has no sum. Classroom sentences only.

1. Translate Ego locūtus sum and Nōs cōnātī sumus.

2. Translate Puer locūtus est and Puerī locūtī sunt, and name what agrees.

3. Contrast ego loquor with ego locūtus sum.

4. Why is puerō locūtō not a sentence in the perfect tense?
