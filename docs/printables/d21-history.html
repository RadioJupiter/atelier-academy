Remember

Livy is a major narrative source for early Rome and is late (late 1st c. BCE–early 1st c. CE). Founding stories and the year 753 BCE are tradition. Archaeology is independent evidence; it does not automatically confirm every named story.

Key terms: Livy, early Rome (tradition and evidence).

1. Place Livy in time relative to the early kings he describes. Use “centuries later,” not a fake precision you were not given.

2. Give one example of a kind of evidence that is not a late narrative, and say what it can show about early Rome.

3. For each of today's key terms (Livy, early Rome (tradition and evidence)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “Livy is late, so we can ignore him” a near-miss?
