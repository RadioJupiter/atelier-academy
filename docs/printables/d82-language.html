Remember

Poner in the preterite uses pus-: puse, pusiste, puso, pusimos, pusisteis, pusieron. Puse is “I put,” not pude “I managed.”

Key terms: puse.

1. Give the six preterite forms of poner.

2. Translate the invented sentence: Ayer puse el libro en la mesa.

3. What is the difference between pude and puse?

4. For each of today's key terms (puse), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
