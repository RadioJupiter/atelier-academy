Remember

Dewing I.3.6–9 describes another shrine of the Virgin at Pegê, in a cypress grove by a silent spring, a church that “surpasses most shrines both in beauty and in size,” outside the wall near the Golden Gate, with Blachernae by the sea. Invent no official length.

1. What shrine does Dewing I.3.6 name, and what surroundings does he give the place?

2. What is he willing to say about the church itself, and what official figure must you not invent?

3. Where does I.3.9 place both churches, and how do you tell Pegê from Blachernae?

4. For each of today's key terms (the shrine at Pegê), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
