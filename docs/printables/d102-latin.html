Remember

Use the whole present table of loquor and of cōnor, with the person matching the subject, and with an active meaning. The perfect, and untaught first-person forms of other deponents, remain reserved.

Key terms: present deponent table in use.

1. Translate Ego loquor, Tū loqueris, and Nōs loquimur.

2. Give the form for “you (plural) speak,” and for “you (singular) try.”

3. Why is nōs loquor a miss?

4. Write a short classroom sentence with cōnāmur, and translate it.
