Remember

A purpose clause with ut uses ut and the present subjunctive. Ut eum sequātur means in order that he follow him, or in order that she follow him, according to the subject. It is not the future sequētur. A negative purpose with nē remains reserved.

1. What is a purpose clause with ut in this lesson, and which already-taught form of sequor does it use?

2. Translate the original sentence “Puella venit ut eum sequātur,” and say why “sequētur” would be a miss in that clause.

3. Why is a purpose clause with nē a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (a purpose clause with ut, a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
