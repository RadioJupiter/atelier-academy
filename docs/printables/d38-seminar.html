Remember

The Pantheon we have is a Hadrianic rebuild, conventionally early second century CE (around 118–125). The Agrippa inscription is older, conventionally tied to 27 BCE, and does not date the present dome. Oculus named; no invented measurements.

Key terms: Pantheon (Hadrianic building, Agrippan inscription).
