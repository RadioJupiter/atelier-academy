Remember

A two-week critique is specific and uses the terms taught: construction, light families, hatch, edges, accents. A plan for the next sittings names one or two skills, a set-up, and a check. No scores, no talent talk, no ten-item wish list.

Key terms: two-week critique, plan for the next sittings.

1. Name one strongest passage from the fortnight and one clearest weakness, each in a specific sentence.

2. Write a plan for the next two sittings: the skill, the set-up, and how you will know it worked.

3. For each of today's key terms (two-week critique, plan for the next sittings), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write the four-part critique (construction, light, surface tools, strongest / weakest) for the drawings in front of you.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
