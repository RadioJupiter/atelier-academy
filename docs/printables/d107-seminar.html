Remember

Retrograde inversion is a short invented figure turned upside down and then played backwards. Inversion alone is upside down, still forwards. Retrograde alone is backwards, not inverted. A sequence keeps the same shape at a new pitch, still forwards. Invent the figure; do not copy a copyrighted score. Invertible counterpoint remains reserved.

1. What is retrograde inversion, in this lesson’s school sense?

2. How does it differ from last week’s retrograde, and from inversion alone?

3. Take the invented figure “rise a step, rise a step, fall a third,” and write its inversion and its retrograde inversion.

4. Why must the figure be invented, and what remains reserved?
