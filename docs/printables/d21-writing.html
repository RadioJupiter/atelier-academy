Remember

Items in the same grammatical slot take the same shape. A coordinated series is the usual test. Repair by rewriting all parts to match, not by adding a synonym.

Key terms: parallelism, coordinated series.

1. Is this series parallel? Clear thinking, careful evidence, and to concede a limit. If not, repair it.

2. Copy the two parallel infinitives from the model (you may trim the modifiers) and then write a new pair of your own in the same shape, on a school subject, not on politics.

3. For each of today's key terms (parallelism, coordinated series), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Repair: He is responsible for gathering sources, for drafting a précis, and he proofreads the typography.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
