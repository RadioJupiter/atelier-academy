Remember

Net photosynthesis: carbon dioxide and water, with light, yield carbohydrate and oxygen. The school equation is a summary, not a one-step mechanism. Carbon in the sugar comes from CO2. Chlorophyll helps capture light; it is not the sugar.

Key terms: photosynthesis, carbon dioxide.

1. Name the two reactants, the energy input, and the two kinds of product in the net account taught today.

2. A student says, “The oxygen we breathe from plants comes from carbon dioxide, because CO2 has oxygen in it.” What does this lesson say about that claim?

3. For each of today's key terms (photosynthesis, carbon dioxide), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write the net school equation from memory, then state one thing it does not show.
