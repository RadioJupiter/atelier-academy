Remember

Dewing will not claim to describe the galleries fittingly or to enumerate the colonnades. The stones are a meadow of named colors. The work is ascribed to God’s influence, not human skill; the sight is new each time; no one has had a surfeit. I.1.58–63.

1. What two things does I.1.58 say no one could fittingly describe or enumerate?

2. To what are the columns and stones compared, and which colors does I.1.60 name?

3. By what, according to I.1.61, has the work been so finely turned?

4. What does I.1.62 say of each successive occasion, and what does I.1.63 say no one has ever had?

5. Why must no official count of columns be added?
