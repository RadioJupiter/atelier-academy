Remember

para: purpose, deadline, destination, recipient. por: duration, exchange, through/along, a general reason. English ‘for’ is not a sufficient clue. Core uses only.

Key terms: para (core uses), por (core uses).

1. Write: “Thank you for the water” and “We walk through the park.”

2. Write: “I have to study in order to learn” (tener que + para).

3. For each of today's key terms (para (core uses), por (core uses)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give one original sentence for each: recipient para, deadline para, duration por, reason por eso.
