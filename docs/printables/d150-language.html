Remember

Haya is the present subjunctive of haber, “that there be.” It is not hay, not sea, not será, and not sería.

1. Give the six present-subjunctive forms of haber, ser, ir, and estar.

2. Choose: “Espero que ___ (haya / hay / sea) tiempo.” “Quiere que ella ___ (vaya / haya / va) al mercado.”

3. Why is a helping-verb use of haber a miss in a first-look sentence this week?

4. Write one invented sentence each for haya, the present subjunctive of ser, and the already-taught future of ir.

5. What is the near-miss if someone writes “hay” after “espero que,” and if someone writes “esté” for “that there be time”?

6. What remains reserved?
