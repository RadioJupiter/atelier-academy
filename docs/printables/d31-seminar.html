Remember

A syllogism has two premises, a conclusion, and a middle term. Barbara is: All M are P; All S are M; therefore All S are P. Other moods are not granted today by being named.

Key terms: syllogism, Barbara (first look).

1. Write a Barbara with original, honest content (no invented census), and mark M, S, and P.

2. Given All M are P and All S are M, write the conclusion of Barbara, in letters.

3. For each of today's key terms (syllogism, Barbara (first look)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. What happens if the conclusion still contains the middle term?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
