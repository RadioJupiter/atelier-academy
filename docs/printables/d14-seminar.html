Remember

A market is the meeting of buyers and sellers of a good, not only a building. A price is a payment and a signal: other things equal, a higher price discourages some buying and encourages some selling. ‘Other things equal’ is part of the claim.

Key terms: market, price (as a signal).

1. Is a used-book stall a market? Is a closed storeroom of unsold books, with no offers, a market in this lesson’s sense? Explain.

2. Give an invented example in which a price falls, other things equal, and say what the signal is to buyers and to sellers.

3. For each of today's key terms (market, price (as a signal)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why does ‘other things equal’ have to be said when we talk about a price change?
