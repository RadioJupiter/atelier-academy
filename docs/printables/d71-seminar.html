Remember

Ferio is EIO in the first figure: no M are P; some S are M; therefore some S are not P. The conclusion stays particular and negative. It is not Darii (AII) and not Celarent (EAE).

Key terms: Ferio.

1. State Ferio in mood letters, and write the three lines in “no / some / therefore some … not.”

2. How does Ferio’s conclusion differ from Darii’s?

3. For each of today's key terms (Ferio), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “some M are P; some S are M; therefore some S are not P” not Ferio?
