Remember

The femur is the thigh bone (hip to knee). The tibia is the larger, inner bone of the lower leg (the shinbone). The fibula is the thinner, outer bone of that same pair. The lower leg has two long bones, not one. No invented muscle counts.

Key terms: femur, tibia, fibula.

1. What is the femur, and between which two joints does it run?

2. How do the tibia and the fibula differ, and where do they lie?

3. Why is “the lower leg has one long bone, like the thigh” the wrong sentence?

4. What does the name fibula record, and what does that name not require you to draw?
