Remember

Cycladic folded-arm marble figures (third millennium BCE, use unknown with certainty). Minoan: Evans’s name, Knossos, frescoes often restored. Mycenaean: Lion Gate at Mycenae, two lionesses and a column, heads lost. These are not yet Classical Greece.

Key terms: Aegean Bronze Age, the name ‘Minoan’.

1. What is the Lion Gate’s relieving triangle, and what does the relief show (including what is missing)?

2. Why is it a mistake to label a Cycladic figure ‘Classical Greek sculpture’?

3. For each of today's key terms (Aegean Bronze Age, the name ‘Minoan’), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a short looking-note (a paragraph) on one Cycladic figure: pose, simplification, and one thing you cannot know from the object alone.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
