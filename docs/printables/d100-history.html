Remember

I.1.64–66: a precise account of all the treasures is impossible; the Inner Sanctuary has forty thousand pounds’ weight of silver; the church is called the Great Church. I.1.67–73: not money alone; the eastern arch waited; the piers cracked; the Emperor told them to complete the curve; the arch hung secure. Invent no conversion and no measurement. 4. What does the Emperor command, and how does the story end in I.1.73?

5. Why is a precise account of all the treasures impossible, in Dewing’s wording?
