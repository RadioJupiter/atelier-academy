Remember

A refutation examines a claim. A maxim is a precept. A chreia needs a person and an occasion. A narrative tells an event in order. A fable carries a moral, often after an animal story.

1. What is a refutation, and what is the set invented claim?

2. Distinguish a refutation from a maxim, a chreia, a narrative, and a fable’s moral.

3. Write one short invented refutation, labeled invented, and say what it must not become.

4. Which of the already-taught exercises would you use for a general precept with no speaker and no day?

5. Which would you use to examine a claim and give reasons not to accept it?

6. What remains reserved?
