Remember

Regular -ar preterite: -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron. Present and preterite stay distinct (hablo / hablé; canta / cantó). hablamos may be either tense; ayer, anoche, and el lunes pasado make the past clear. Instruction is in English.

1. Write the six preterite forms of hablar.

2. Write “you (tú) bought” in the present and in the preterite.

3. Write one original sentence with ayer and one with anoche.

4. Why does habló need a written accent, and why does nosotros hablamos need a time word or a clear context?

5. Correct: Ayer yo hablo, Ella canto, and *Ellos compró.

6. Write a three-sentence invented picture story in the preterite, with at least one time word.
