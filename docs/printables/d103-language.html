Remember

Regular -er and -ir imperfect: -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían. Comía, vivía, leía. The first-person and third-person singular share a form. Use the imperfect for ongoing or habitual past action in this first look, and the already-taught preterite for a completed event. Ir, ser, and ver remain reserved.

Key terms: imperfect of regular -er and -ir verbs.

2. How do these endings differ from the -ar imperfect taught on Monday?

3. Write a short sentence with leía for an ongoing action, and contrast it with ayer leí.
