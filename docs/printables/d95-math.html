Remember

Pyramid V = (1/3)Bh. Cone V = (1/3)πr²h. Sphere V = (4/3)πr³ and A = 4πr². A prism or cylinder of the same base and height is three times the pyramid or cone. Keep area and volume distinct.

1. A right square pyramid has base 5 by 5 and height 12. What is V?

2. A classroom cone has radius 2 and height 9. What is V, in terms of π?

3. A classroom sphere has radius 3. Give V and A in terms of π, and name the units of each.

4. A classroom sphere has volume 36π cubic units. What is r?

5. Why is “the cone’s volume is πr²h” a miss, and why is 36π not enough, by itself, to tell area from volume when r = 3?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
