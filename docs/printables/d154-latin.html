Remember

Match the form to the time: sequitur, present indicative; sequētur, future; sequātur, present subjunctive; secūtus est, perfect; secūtus erit, future perfect. Do the same for ēgredior. Do not swap the two verbs.

1. Give a third-person singular form of sequor for each of these glosses: she follows (indicative); she will follow; she follows (present subjunctive); she followed.

2. Give a third-person singular form of ēgredior for: he goes out (indicative); he will go out; he goes out (present subjunctive); he will have gone out.

3. Why is sequātur a miss for “he went out”?

4. Write short original sentences for sequētur, sequātur, ēgreditur, and ēgrediātur, with glosses.

5. Contrast secūtus erat and ēgressus erit in short glosses.

6. What remains reserved?
