Week 19 · Day 95 · Friday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Ver: vi, viste, vio, vimos, visteis, vieron — saw. Dar: di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron — gave. Di is not dije; dio is not dijo.

1. Give the six preterite forms of ver and of dar.

2. Translate the invented pair: Nosotros vimos la puerta. Nosotros dimos el libro.

3. Why is dije a miss for “I gave,” and why is dijo a miss for “he gave”?

4. Write one invented sentence with vi and one with di.

5. Place vi and di beside dije and traje, with a short school sense for each.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
