Remember

H is silent (hora). J, and g before e or i, is a breathy back sound (jardín, gente). Ñ is like ny in canyon. Greet with hola or buenos días.

Key terms: vowel (Spanish), greeting.

1. How many vowel sounds does Spanish have, and do they glide like many English vowels?

2. What does hola mean?

3. For each of today's key terms (vowel (Spanish), greeting), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Mark the stressed syllable and name the rule (1, 2 or 3): ventana, reloj, papel, árbol, canción, joven, ciudad, lápiz.
