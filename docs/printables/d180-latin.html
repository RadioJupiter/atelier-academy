Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Purpose (ut, nē) and result (ut, ut nōn). Present subjunctive, not the future. Original sentences. No new construction.

1. Translate and label: Puella venit ut eum sequātur. Puella tam celeriter venit ut eum sequātur.

2. Translate and label: Puella manet nē eum sequātur. Puella tam lentē venit ut eum nōn sequātur.

3. Why is nē the wrong negative in a result clause?

4. Give the present subjunctive and the future, third person singular, of sequor and of ēgredior, and say why the future is a miss in these clauses.

5. Translate and label: Puer surgit ut ēgrediātur. Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur. Puer sedet nē ēgrediātur.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
