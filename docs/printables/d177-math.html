Remember

Sin A = opposite/hypotenuse, cos A = adjacent/hypotenuse, tan A = opposite/adjacent, and tan A = sin A / cos A. In an invented 3-4-5 triangle, if A is opposite 3, tan A = 3/4.

1. Write the already-taught definitions of sin A, cos A, and tan A, and the already-taught relation tan A = sin A / cos A.

2. In an invented 3-4-5 triangle, with A opposite 3, give the three ratios.

3. In an invented 5-12-13 triangle, with A opposite 5, give sin A, cos A, and tan A.

4. In an invented 8-15-17 triangle, with A opposite 8, give tan A, and check it as sin A / cos A.

5. Why are these lengths labeled invented?
