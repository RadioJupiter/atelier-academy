Remember

Oscillation: a repeating back-and-forth. T is the time for one cycle; f = 1/T. For a simple pendulum and a small swing (less than about 15°), T = 2π√(L/g); T depends on L and g, not on mass.

1. What limit does OpenStax 16.4 place on the simple formula, in degrees of amplitude?

2. Does a heavier bob change T, in this model?

3. If f = 2 cycles per second, what is T?

4. Why is a 2 s classroom timing labeled invented, while ‘about 2.0 s’ for L = 1.00 m is a calculation?
