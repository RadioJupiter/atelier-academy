Remember

A perpendicular from the center to a chord bisects the chord. Then OM² + (half-chord)² = radius². Equal chords are at equal distances from the center.

Key terms: chord, distance from the center.

1. A circle has radius 15 and a chord of length 18. How far is the chord from the center?

2. A circle has radius 25 and a chord of length 14. How far is the chord from the center?

3. For each of today's key terms (chord, distance from the center), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A chord of length 16 lies 6 from the center. Find the radius.
