Remember

Book 4: the truce is broken (Pandarus wounds Menelaus). Book 5: Diomedes' aristeia. Book 6: Hector in Troy, and the meeting with Andromache and the child at the Scaean Gate. Hector's aidōs (shame before the Trojans) outweighs his own safety, even though he foresees the fall of the city. Copywork: the helmet and the child.

Key terms: aristeia, aidōs.

1. Compare Hector and Achilles so far: what does each fight for?

2. For each of today's key terms (aristeia, aidōs), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

3. Why place the scene of Hector and Andromache in the middle of so much killing? What does it change for the reader?

4. Andromache asks Hector to stay on the wall and fight defensively. What is his answer and what does it reveal about honor (aidōs, shame before others)?
