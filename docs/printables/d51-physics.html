Remember

g = G M / R², with G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ (3 s.f.). Mars: M = 0.642 × 10²⁴ kg, R = 3,396 km, g ≈ 3.7 m/s², matching the fact sheet’s listed 3.7. About 0.38 of Earth’s 9.8 (about 19/50), a little less than 2/5. No invented orbital speed.
