Remember

Hable, coma, and viva are regular present subjunctives. They are not habla, come, or vive; not hablará, comerá, or vivirá; and not hablaría, comería, or viviría.

1. Give the six present-subjunctive forms of hablar, comer, and vivir.

2. Choose: “Quiere que ella ___ (coma / come / comería).”

3. Why is “vaya” a miss in a first-look sentence this week?

4. Write one invented sentence each for the present subjunctive, the future, and the conditional of vivir.

5. What is the near-miss if someone writes “habla” after “quiere que él”?

6. What remains reserved?
