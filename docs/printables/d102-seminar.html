Remember

Retrograde is a short invented figure played backwards: the last note becomes the first. Inversion, already taught, is upside down, not backwards. A sequence keeps the same shape at a new pitch, still forwards. Invent the figure; do not copy a copyrighted score. Invertible counterpoint remains reserved.

Key terms: retrograde of a short figure.

2. How does retrograde differ from last week’s inversion, and from a sequence?

3. Take the invented figure “rise a step, rise a step, fall a third,” and write its retrograde.

4. Why must the figure be invented, and what remains reserved?
