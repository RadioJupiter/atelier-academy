Remember

The opening names the question or set text and states the thesis, without a tour of the outline. The close returns to the thesis in new wording and stops, without a new reason.

Key terms: essay opening, essay close.

1. What must an opening do, and what must it refuse to do?

2. What must a close do, and why is a new reason a miss?

3. Why is “In this essay I will…” a weak opening in this course?

4. For each of today's key terms (essay opening, essay close), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
