Remember

Est / sunt. Adjectives agree. Read aloud, then say what the sentence tells you, using the picture or map.

1. Why is it sunt and not est in oppida magna sunt?

2. Which adjective form goes with fluviī?

3. Copy the table from memory, then check it.

4. Write a paragraph of eight Latin sentences describing a region of the map (rivers, islands, towns, provinces), with at least two questions and two answers.

5. Translate into Latin, without looking at the book until you check: (a) The Nile is not small but large. (b) Are Sicily and Sardinia islands? (c) Brundisium and Tusculum are towns in Italy.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
