Remember

A feature study is a constructed form from a Bargue plate, not a remembered symbol. Block in large shapes and angles first; measure alignments; model families only after the shapes are true. An iris at an angle is often an ellipse. Work large enough to correct.

Key terms: feature study, symbol versus form.

1. In your own words, what is the difference between a symbol of a mouth and the form of a mouth on a plate?

2. Why should the feature be drawn large, in this lesson?

3. For each of today's key terms (feature study, symbol versus form), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Draw the chosen Bargue feature: 15-minute block-in, correction of alignments, then a first modeling of the families. Keep the viewing point fixed.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
