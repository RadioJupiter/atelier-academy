Remember

The six come to a noble castle, seven times encompassed with lofty walls, defended by a fair rivulet, which they pass as firm ground, and they enter through seven portals into a meadow of fresh verdure. The people have solemn eyes and slow, and speak but seldom, with gentle voices. Opposite, on the green enamel, the mighty spirits are pointed out. The catalog from line 121 waits until Thursday. Keep Longfellow’s typography.

Key terms: the noble castle (Inferno IV).

1. Where do the six go after “the sixth was I,” and what must they keep silent, in Longfellow’s wording?

2. How is the noble castle described, and how do they pass the rivulet?

3. What do they enter after the seven portals, and how are the people there described?

4. What is pointed out upon the green enamel, and what waits until Thursday?
