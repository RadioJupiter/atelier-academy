Remember

In Dewing I.1.31–32, the east face of the church (toward the rising sun, where the mysteries are performed) is built as a half-cylinder: a curving, receding wall, rising precipitously to a height. No official measurement is invented.

Key terms: half-cylinder (Dewing).

1. Quote or closely paraphrase Dewing’s name for the curved form, and say what it is not (a straight wall).

2. Why is a classroom sketch of a half-cylinder not a plan of the church?

3. For each of today's key terms (half-cylinder (Dewing)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. How does today’s extract continue last week’s I.1.27–30, rather than replacing those sentences as a first look at the church?
