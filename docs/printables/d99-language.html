Remember

Pedir: e to i in the third-person preterite only. Dormir: o to u in the third-person preterite only. Do not swap the vowels. Pedimos and dormimos keep the original vowel.

Key terms: pedir and dormir in the preterite, contrasted.

1. Give the six preterite forms of pedir and of dormir.

2. Which forms change, and to which vowels?

3. Write a short sentence with pidió, and one with durmió.

4. Why are durmió ayuda and pidió temprano misses in this contrast, if you meant “asked for help” and “slept early”?
