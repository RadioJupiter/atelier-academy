Remember

Forces come in pairs, equal and opposite, on two bodies. They never cancel as if they acted on the same body. Fnet = ma* still holds for each body separately.

Key terms: Newton's third law, action–reaction pair.

1. Why do a third-law pair of forces not cancel each other?

2. A box sits at rest on a table. Is the table's upward force on the box the third-law pair of the box's weight, or of the box's push on the table?

3. For each of today's key terms (Newton's third law, action–reaction pair), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A ball is thrown up at 20 m/s. How high does it rise? How long until it returns?
