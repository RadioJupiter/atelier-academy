Remember

Stable: returns after a small nudge. Unstable: runs away after a small nudge. Neutral: rests in the new nearby place. A center of mass on a flat card is at the crossing of two plumb lines from two hangings.

1. A marble on a flat, level table is moved a little and stays there. Which kind of equilibrium is that?

2. Where do you mark the center of mass after two suspensions?

3. Why is “at rest” not, by itself, enough to name the kind of equilibrium?

4. Why must the two hanging-points be different?
