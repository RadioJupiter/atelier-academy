Remember

Gray, from Theodora: four curves — cervical, convex forward, the least marked; thoracic, concave forward; lumbar, convex anteriorly; pelvic, concavity downward and forward. Thoracic and pelvic are primary; cervical and lumbar are secondary, or compensatory. Official 71 cm and 61 cm lengths, and a slight lateral curvature, remain reserved.
