Remember

Purpose: ut or nē. Result: ut or ut nōn, often with tam, ita, or sic. The verb is the present subjunctive, not the future.

1. Translate and label: Puella manet nē eum sequātur. Puer tam celeriter surgit ut ēgrediātur.

2. Why is sequētur a miss in both a purpose clause and a result clause?

3. What job does tam do in a result sentence?

4. Write four original sentences, one for each of the four jobs, and translate them.

5. Change one result sentence so that it uses ēgrediātur, and translate it.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
