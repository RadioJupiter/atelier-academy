Remember

Ser and ir share the preterite fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fuisteis, fueron. The sentence tells whether the meaning is “was/were” or “went.” These are not the regular endings -é or -í.

Key terms: the preterite of ser and ir.

1. Write one short sentence with fue as ser, and one with fue as ir, using ayer, anoche, or el lunes pasado.

2. Why is hablé not in this shared set?

3. For each of today's key terms (the preterite of ser and ir), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the nosotros and vosotros forms of this preterite.
