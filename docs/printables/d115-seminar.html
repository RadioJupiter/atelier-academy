Remember

A typical vertebra has a body and a vertebral or neural arch, enclosing the vertebral foramen. The arch has two pedicles and two laminæ, and seven processes: four articular, two transverse, and one spinous. Stacked foramina form a canal for the spinal cord; intervertebral foramina transmit nerves and vessels. Keep vertebræ and laminæ. Official lengths and regional special features remain reserved.
