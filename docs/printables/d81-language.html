Remember

Poder in the preterite uses pud-: pude, pudiste, pudo, pudimos, pudisteis, pudieron. The usual school sense is a completed ability, “managed to,” on that occasion. It is not last week’s uv-stem.

Key terms: pude.

1. Give the six preterite forms of poder.

2. Translate the invented sentence: Ayer pude terminar el trabajo.

3. How is pude not the same pattern as tuve?

4. For each of today's key terms (pude), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
