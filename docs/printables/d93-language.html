Remember

Dar in the preterite: di, diste, dio, dimos, disteis, dieron. Di is not dije; dio is not dijo. The school sense is a completed giving. Vi is a contrast, not a first look today.

Key terms: di.

1. Give the six preterite forms of dar, and mark the I-form carefully against dije.

2. Translate the invented sentence: Ayer di el libro.

3. Why is “ayer dije el libro,” meaning “I gave the book,” a miss?

4. For each of today's key terms (di), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
