Remember

A frustum of a right cone is a large cone minus a smaller similar cone. V = (1/3)πh(R² + Rr + r²), with h the height of the frustum. Classroom lengths are invented. Exact answers stay in terms of π.

Key terms: volume of a frustum of a right cone.

1. What is a frustum of a right cone, in this lesson’s school sense, and how is its volume found from cones already taught?

2. A classroom large cone has radius 6 and height 8. A similar small cone of height 4 is cut away. What is the small radius, and what is the volume of the frustum?

3. Write V = (1/3)πh(R² + Rr + r²), and check it on those numbers.

4. A classroom large cone has radius 8 and height 12. A similar small cone of height 6 is cut away. What is the volume of the frustum?
