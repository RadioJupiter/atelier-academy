Remember

The imperfect of ser is era, eras, era, éramos, erais, eran, not iba. Ir and ser share the preterite fui, but not the imperfect. Era is a past state in this first look; iba is “was going.” Fui is a completed event, already taught. Instruction remains in English.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of ser, and translate them as a past state.

2. How do yo era, yo iba, and yo fui differ in this course’s first look?

3. Write a short invented sentence with era, and contrast it with ayer fui.

4. Why must classroom sentences not identify a living person?
