Remember

A successive discount takes the second percent off the remainder, not off the original price again. Ten percent off $100, then ten percent off $90, leaves $81, not $80. Two discounts of 10 percent are not 20 percent off the original. Last week’s single discount is a different job. Invented figures only. Tax and compound interest remain reserved.

1. An invented price is $100, and 10 percent is taken off, then 10 percent is taken off the remainder. What final amount is paid, and why is $80 a miss?

2. Of what amount is the second 10 percent a part, and why must you not take it of the original $100 again?

3. An invented price is $80, and 25 percent is taken off, then 10 percent is taken off the remainder. What final amount is paid, and why is 35 percent of 80 a miss?

4. Why are the figures invented, and why is this not a first look at last week’s single discount?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
