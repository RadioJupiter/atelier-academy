Remember

Present pedir changes e to i except in nosotros and vosotros. Preterite pedir changes e to i only in the third persons. Pedimos keeps e in both tenses; the time word and the ending tell them apart.

Key terms: pedir in present and preterite, contrasted.

2. Where does the stem change in each tense?

3. How can pedimos be present in one sentence and preterite in another?
