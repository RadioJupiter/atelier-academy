Remember

Linear: graph is a straight line. f(x) = mx + b: b is the intercept at x = 0; m is slope, rise over run. A table becomes points; a third point checks the line.

Key terms: linear function, slope.

1. For f(x) = 3x − 1, find f(0) and f(2), and name m and b.

2. A line passes through (0, 2) and (4, 10). Find the slope, then suggest f(x) = mx + b.

3. For each of today's key terms (linear function, slope), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Graph, as a sketch, y = x (what are m and b?).
