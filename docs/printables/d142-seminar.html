Remember

Minuet and trio is A–B–A: a minuet, a contrasting trio, and the minuet again. The classroom figure is invented. It is not theme and variations, and not a rondo.

1. What is minuet and trio, and how does it differ from last week’s theme and variations?

2. State the invented minuet and trio in scale degrees, and say what returns after the trio.

3. Why must the figure not be replaced by a copyrighted tune, and how does the form differ from a rondo?

4. For each of today's key terms (minuet and trio), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
