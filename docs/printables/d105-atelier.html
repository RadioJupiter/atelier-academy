Remember

The eye is a volume in the already-placed socket, not a flat symbol. The lids are small planes that wrap that volume, not wires. A likeness check of the two eyes together changes only what it shows, and watches for a stamped second eye. A written pause names only what was seen or changed.

1. What is the eye, in this week’s school sense, and what must you not draw instead?

2. How are the upper and lower lids judged, if the light is from above?

3. Name the four things in the likeness check of the two eyes.

4. What must a written pause not invent?

5. Why must the second eye be seen, not stamped?

6. How does last week’s mouth as a slot help this week’s eye?
