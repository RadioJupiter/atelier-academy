Four triangles stand between the arches and end in the circle that carries the huge spherical dome. The dome seems suspended from Heaven, yet it is said to rest upon that circle. The eye cannot linger, and observers leave overwhelmed. Dewing I.1.42–49; I.1.50 remains reserved.

Key terms: four triangles under the circle (Dewing), dome that seems suspended (Dewing).
