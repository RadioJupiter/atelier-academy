Remember

The close grants a real limit, then returns to the thesis in new words. If deleting the concession changes nothing, it was decoration. Do not end on the limit with no return.

Key terms: concession (three-paragraph close).

1. Write a concession-and-return close for your Friday thesis (two to four sentences).

2. Apply the deletion test: what in the thesis had to be qualified once the concession was granted?

3. For each of today's key terms (concession (three-paragraph close)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a fake concession on the same thesis and say why it fails.
