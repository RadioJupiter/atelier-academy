Remember

F = −kx. The force is opposite to the displacement. k = |F| / |x|, in N/m, and is larger for a stiffer spring. This is OpenStax 16.1, not a first look at oscillation itself.

Key terms: Hooke’s law, force constant.

1. An invented spring stretches 0.10 m under 25 N. What is k?

2. If k = 200 N/m, what is the size of the restoring force at a stretch of 0.03 m?

3. What does the minus sign in F = −kx tell you, and what does it not change in the calculation of k?

4. For each of today's key terms (Hooke’s law, force constant), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
