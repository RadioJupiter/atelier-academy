Remember

You may add or subtract the same number on both sides. Multiplying or dividing by a negative reverses the sign. Check with a value from the solution set.

1. Solve x² − 5x + 6 ≥ 0.

2. Two positive numbers add to 10. What is the largest possible value of their product, and when does it occur?

3. Solve: (a) 3x − 5 > 7 (b) −2x + 1 ≤ 9 (c) x² − 5x + 6 < 0 (d) x² ≥ 9.

4. Prove a² + b² ≥ 2ab for all real a, b.

5. Prove x + 1/x ≥ 2 for all x > 0. When does equality hold?

6. A frame is made from 2 m of moulding. What are the dimensions of the largest-area rectangular frame?
