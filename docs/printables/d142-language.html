Remember

The present subjunctive of ir is vaya, vayas, vaya, vayamos, vayáis, vayan. It is not va, not irá, not iría, and not sea.

1. What are the six present-subjunctive forms of ir?

2. Translate the invented sentence “Quiere que ustedes vayan,” and say why “van” would be a miss.

3. Why is sea a miss for “that she go”?

4. For each of today's key terms (present subjunctive of ir), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
