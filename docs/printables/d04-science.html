Remember

Combustion of a hydrocarbon consumes oxygen and produces carbon dioxide and water; it also releases heat. A quantitative model must match measured volumes and temperatures, not only the word 'burning'. Day 2 already showed that the water rise in the jar is not simply the 21% oxygen fraction.

Key terms: combustion, quantitative.

1. Why does 'wax uses up oxygen, which is 21% of the air' fail as a complete quantitative explanation of the water rise, even though the candle really does consume oxygen?

2. For each of today's key terms (combustion, quantitative), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
