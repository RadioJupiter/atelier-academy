Remember

An encomium praises one. An invective blames one. A comparison sets two side by side: likeness, difference, and what the difference comes to.

1. In one sentence each, distinguish an encomium, an invective, and a comparison.

2. Why would a piece that only praised the careful geometer not yet be a comparison?

3. Why is no living person named?

4. Write three short paragraphs, labeled Encomium, Invective, and Comparison, on the same two invented types.

5. In the comparison paragraph, mark the likeness, the difference, and what the difference comes to.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
