Remember

In Buildings I.1.20–22, Procopius names the Nika Insurrection in Byzantium, the burning of the church called Sophia, the ruins, and Justinian’s rebuilt church, “so finely shaped.” Quote Dewing exactly, including “shewing” if you quote that word. No death toll and no invented plan.

Key terms: Nika Insurrection (as named by Procopius), the church called Sophia.

1. What does Procopius say Justinian built “not long afterwards,” and how does Procopius praise it?

2. Why will this lesson not add a death toll for the Nika rising?

3. For each of today's key terms (Nika Insurrection (as named by Procopius), the church called Sophia), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is Procopius’s remark, that people would have prayed to see the old church destroyed in order to have the new one, to be read as his praise rather than as a counted vote?
