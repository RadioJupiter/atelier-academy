Remember

Investing, in this invented arrangement, uses some of a leftover to buy an invented item that is later sold; a gain or a loss on that invented sale can be named. It is distinct from saving, which only sets the remainder aside. Official interest is not added. The figures are invented.

1. What is investing in this lesson, and how does it differ from last week’s saving?

2. On the invented figures, a $40 remainder is set aside and $20 is used to buy an item later sold for $25. What is the gain, and what remains saved?

3. Why is official interest a miss here?
