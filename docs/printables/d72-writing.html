Remember

A straw man answers a weaker, distorted, or invented version of someone else’s claim. A faithful restatement, or a genuine objection to the claim as made, is not a straw man. A précis is a near-miss only in the sense that it is the faithful job this fallacy refuses.

Key terms: straw man.

1. Why is a précis of a set passage not a straw man of that passage?

2. What makes B’s invented reply easier than answering A, and why is that not a sound method?

3. For each of today's key terms (straw man), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Invent (and label invented) a short straw-man reply to this claim: “Prīdiē means the day immediately before a named Roman day.” Then write a sound reply instead.
