Remember

ΔsublH(0 °C) = 2834 J/g, from Harvey/NIST: the energy to turn one gram of ice directly into vapor at 0 °C. An invented 10 g needs 28.34 kJ. This is not last week’s melting figure, and not a 100 °C boiling figure.

Key terms: enthalpy of sublimation of ice.

1. What does ΔsublH(0 °C) = 2834 J/g mean, and where is the figure from?

2. An invented 2 g of ice, already at 0 °C, is sublimed at 0 °C. What energy is required?

3. Why is “then subtract 333.4 and call that the boiling figure at 100 °C” a miss?

4. For each of today's key terms (enthalpy of sublimation of ice), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
