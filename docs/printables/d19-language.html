Remember

más + adj + que; menos + adj + que; tan + adj + como. Adjectives agree. Before a number: más de diez. Irregular: mejor, peor, mayor, menor — not más mejor.

Key terms: comparative, irregular comparative.

1. Translate: The class is less difficult than the exam, and it is as long as an hour. (Use el examen and una hora.)

2. Correct: más mejor, tan libros como, and más que diez páginas.

3. For each of today's key terms (comparative, irregular comparative), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write four comparisons: más, menos, tan…como, and mejor or peor.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
