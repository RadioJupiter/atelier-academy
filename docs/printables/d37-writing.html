Three topic sentences first, holding two claims fairly. No ending of the play in this plan. A short quotation slot is allowed if it is attributed and already taught.

Key terms: fair two-claim plan.

1. Add two supporting notes under each of your three topic sentences.

2. Mark one place where a short attributed quotation could go, and name the source you would use (Bacon, or a Jebb line already set).

3. For each of today's key terms (fair two-claim plan), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Revise any topic sentence that buries a verdict in a single adjective (for example, “Creon’s cruel edict” as a finished judgment).
