Remember

One thesis; two topic sentences, each with supporting points; a concluding sentence in new words. The second paragraph must do work the first cannot do alone. Do not write ‘In conclusion’ as a substitute for a close.

Key terms: two-paragraph outline, concluding sentence.

1. Write a two-paragraph outline for this thesis: ‘Town names in Latin drop the preposition that a common noun still needs.’

2. Revise this concluding sentence: ‘In conclusion, as I said, town names are different.’

3. For each of today's key terms (two-paragraph outline, concluding sentence), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a complete two-paragraph outline on a history or science question from this week, and keep it for Day 20.
