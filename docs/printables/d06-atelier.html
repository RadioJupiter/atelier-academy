Atelier · Light on form: the egg

Week 2 · Day 6 · Monday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

One main light. Map light family, terminator, shadow family, reflected light, and cast shadow. The highlight is small. Values from the scale, not from invention.

1. Name the main value families on a simply lit egg.

2. Where does the cast shadow fall?

3. (15 min) Five thumbnails (5 cm) of the egg as two values only: light and shadow (including cast shadow). Move the lamp for each.

4. (65 min) One careful drawing of the egg, about 12 cm long: block-in, shadow shapes as flat tone, then modeling. Name each zone in the margin with a small arrow.

5. (10 min) Squint test: does the shadow family read as one mass, separate from the light?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
