Remember

The rim of the ear is a narrow plane that wraps the hollow, not a wire. It may take a narrow light, and the turn into the hollow may go into shade. Do not invent a curl. The second ear is seen, not stamped. Full color remains reserved.

1. What is the rim of the ear, in this lesson’s school sense?

2. How is it judged if the light is from above and in front?

3. What must you not invent, and what must you not do with the second ear?

4. Against what already-seen neighbors is the rim checked?
