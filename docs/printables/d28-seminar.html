Remember

The Nike of Samothrace is a fragmentary Hellenistic Nike, usually dated to the second century BCE, now in the Louvre. The Laocoön is a famous Vatican group whose date and original-or-copy status are debated. Describe first; do not invent an exact year; a museum view is not the original setting.

Key terms: Nike of Samothrace, Laocoön (date caution).

1. How should you date the Nike of Samothrace in a sentence in this course?

2. Why is “the marble feels Hellenistic, so it must be from the year X” a bad method here?

3. For each of today's key terms (Nike of Samothrace, Laocoön (date caution)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Name one loss or later change in the Nike’s situation (fragment, missing head, museum setting) and say what it changes in a claim about “the” ancient view.
