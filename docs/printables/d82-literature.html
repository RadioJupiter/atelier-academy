Remember

A fair, saintly Lady calls Virgil from among those in suspense, asks help for a friend impeded by terror on the desert slope, and names herself Beatrice. Love moved her to speak. Longfellow II.52–74; later ladies remain for Thursday.

Key terms: the saintly Lady, Beatrice.

1. Where was Virgil when the Lady called to him, in the set wording?

2. What does she call the person who needs help, and what has happened to him on the slope?

3. Quote the line in which she names herself, and the line that names what moved her.

4. For each of today's key terms (the saintly Lady, Beatrice), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
