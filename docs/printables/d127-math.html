Remember

At 180°, P is (−1, 0): cos 180° = −1, sin 180° = 0. At 270°, P is (0, −1): cos 270° = 0, sin 270° = −1.

1. Give P, cos θ, and sin θ at 180° and at 270°.

2. Complete the four axis angles: 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°, with cosine and sine.

3. Why is sine not always positive, and what remains reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (sine and cosine at the axis angles), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
