Remember

The speaker beholds the Master of those who know, with Socrates and Plato nearer him. The catalog continues, but he cannot portray them all. The sixfold company divides, and the canto closes, “to a place I come where nothing shines.” Keep the set wording. A complete memorized roll-call is not required. Canto V is not begun.

Key terms: the Master of those who know (Inferno IV), “where nothing shines” (Inferno IV).

1. Whom does the speaker behold when he lifts his brows, and who stand nearer him?

2. What does the speaker say about portraying the spirits in full?

3. What happens to the sixfold company, and what is the set wording of the close?

4. Why must a later identification of the Master of those who know not be imported as if it were in these lines?
