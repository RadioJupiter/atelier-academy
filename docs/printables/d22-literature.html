Remember

Books 1–4 follow Telemachus in Ithaca, Pylos, and Sparta; the suitors occupy the house; Odysseus is not yet on stage. The name Telemachy is a later convenience for this opening. Quotes, if used, are short and attributed.

Key terms: Telemachy, suitors.

1. Who are the suitors, in the working sense of this lesson, and what pressure do they put on the house?

2. Butler’s translation is used for short public-domain quotation. What is it not, in this course’s warning?

3. For each of today's key terms (Telemachy, suitors), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. What news does Telemachus hear that Odysseus is not dead but delayed? Keep it at the level of today’s outline (Calypso’s island is enough).
