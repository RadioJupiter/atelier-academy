Remember

Tener, poner, salir, and venir form the future with a d in the stem: tendré, pondré, saldré, vendré, plus the same endings. Not *teneré.

1. Give the yo future forms of tener, poner, salir, and venir, with meanings.

2. Write one original sentence with tendremos and one with vendré.

3. Why is *teneré a miss, and what remains reserved until tomorrow?

4. Translate: Mañana lo pondré aquí, invented place. Ellos saldrán. Nosotros tendremos tiempo, invented, not an official schedule.

5. Give tendrá, pondrá, and vendremos, with meanings.

6. Change tengo and vengo into simple-future first-person sentences about tomorrow.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
