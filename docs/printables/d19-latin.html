Remember

Passive present, 3rd person: −tur (singular), −ntur (plural). The subject receives the action. The doer, if named, is ā/ab + ablative (agent), not cum. portātur is not a past tense.

Key terms: passive, ablative of agent.

1. What is the difference in meaning between ā servō and cum servō after a verb like portātur versus est?

2. Write the active partner of Saccus ā servō portātur.

3. For each of today's key terms (passive, ablative of agent), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
