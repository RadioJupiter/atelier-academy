Remember

A cube is planes, not a rounded box. Foreshortening: measure the receding shape you see, from a fixed point, not the square you know. Each visible plane takes a family of value, with a clear change at the edge.

Key terms: plane (of a form), foreshortening.

1. What is foreshortening, in your own words, and where will it appear on a cube below eye level?

2. How do you use a plumb line or a pencil at arm’s length (Day 1) to check a receding edge?

3. For each of today's key terms (plane (of a form), foreshortening), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Draw the cube: 10-minute block-in, then correction, then three plane values and the cast shadow. Keep the viewing point fixed. (About 35 minutes of drawing.)

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
