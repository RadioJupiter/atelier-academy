Remember

Similar triangles have corresponding angles equal and corresponding sides proportional. We write △ABC ∼ △DEF. Congruence (≅) is same shape and same size; similarity (∼) is same shape, with a scale factor that may be other than 1.

Key terms: similar triangles.

1. What is the difference between △ABC ≅ △DEF and △ABC ∼ △DEF?

2. Sides 5, 7, 8 and 10, 14, 16: are the triangles similar? Give the scale factor from the first to the second, or say why not.

3. If △ABC ∼ △DEF, ∠A = 50°, and ∠E = 80°, what is ∠C?

4. A student writes 3/6 = 5/12 for the figure in Learn. What has gone wrong?
