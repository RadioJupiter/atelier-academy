Remember

Ut marks a positive purpose; nē marks a negative purpose. Both take the present subjunctive already taught. The futures sequētur and ēgrediētur are a different job. A result clause remains reserved.

1. How do ut and nē differ in a purpose clause, and what mood do both take?

2. Translate “Puer surgit ut ēgrediātur” and “Puer sedet nē ēgrediātur.”

3. Why is a result clause a miss in this mix?

4. Write four short original clauses: ut sequātur, nē sequātur, ut ēgrediātur, and nē ēgrediātur, each in a sentence with a gloss.

5. Correct the miss “Puella manet ut eum sequētur” if the job is a negative purpose.

6. What remains reserved?
