Remember

Draw a bar first when the story is part–whole or comparison. Substitution: express one unknown and replace it. Elimination: add or subtract to cancel an unknown. Always check the pair in both equations.

Key terms: system of equations, substitution.

1. A 90 cm ribbon is cut into two pieces, one twice as long as the other. How long is each piece?

2. Solve x + y = 15 and 2x − y = 6. Check both equations.

3. For each of today's key terms (system of equations, substitution), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Solve, by substitution, x = 3y and x + 2y = 20.
