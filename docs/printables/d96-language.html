Remember

Pedir in the preterite: pedí, pediste, pidió, pedimos, pedisteis, pidieron. Only the third persons change e to i. The present-tense pattern is wider, and is not today’s job. Instruction remains in English.

Key terms: preterite of pedir.

1. Give the six preterite forms of pedir, and mark which two change e to i.

2. Why is pidimos a miss for “we asked for,” in the preterite?

3. Write a short sentence with pedí, and one with pidieron.

4. How is this change narrower than the already-taught present of pedir?
