Platner: Vespasian built the Colosseum on the stagnum of Nero’s domus Aurea, to the second arcade, and dedicated it before his death in 79. Titus added the third and fourth stories; dedication of the enlarged building in 80, with games lasting one hundred days (Platner, citing Suetonius, Titus 7, among others). A later building, not a photograph of 79.
