Remember

Water can evaporate below the boiling point, and more energy is required than at the boiling point, in OpenStax 14.3. At 37.0 °C, Lv = 2430 kJ/kg (580 kcal/kg), from footnotes 4 and 5 to Table 14.2, which is more than 2256 kJ/kg at 100.0 °C. Q = mLv, with the matching Lv. Invented masses are labeled invented. Other unofficial temperatures are not invented. Harvey’s 0 °C figures remain a different table.

Key terms: latent heat of vaporization of water at 37.0 °C (OpenStax).

2. What value does the table’s footnote give for Lv of water at 37.0 °C, and how does it compare with last week’s 2256 kJ/kg at 100.0 °C?

3. An invented 10 g, already at 37.0 °C, is vaporized. What is Q?
