Remember

The May 3 grant assigns lands discovered and to be discovered to the kings of Castile and León and their heirs, with a proviso about a Christian owner. It does not, in this text, draw a line of leagues.

1. To whom is the grant made, for how long, and what lands does it include, in Davenport’s wording?

2. What proviso is already in the grant clause, and what does the later sentence say about a right already acquired?

3. Why must you not draw a line of leagues from this May 3 text?

4. Quote, or closely restate, the phrase “to be discovered hereafter,” and say why it matters.

5. What comparison does the bull already make with earlier grants to kings of Portugal, without asking you to quote that earlier grant in full?

6. What official figures must you still not invent?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
