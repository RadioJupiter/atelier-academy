A definition you can only recognize on the card is not yet yours. Give the meaning, a fresh example, and a near-miss.

1. What is (f ∘ f⁻¹)(x) for an invertible f, and how is this “inverse” different from the logic inverse?

3. Why is a full circle x² + y² = r² not generally the graph of a single function y = f(x)?

4. In Dryden’s 1697 opening, what is being sung, and why must we attribute Dryden rather than say “Virgil wrote these English lines”?

5. tan… como and más… que: what job does each have, and which one is equality?
