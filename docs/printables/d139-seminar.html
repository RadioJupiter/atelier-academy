Remember

On invented figures, a $100 deductible means that if the named event happens, the invented $500 payment is reduced by $100, so $400 is paid. The premium is still paid in advance. The figures are not official.

1. What is a deductible in this invented arrangement, and what is paid if an invented $500 meets an invented $100 deductible when the named event happens?

2. What is the year’s premium if the monthly premium is still an invented $10, and when is that premium paid?

3. Why is the deductible not a tax, and why are the figures not official?

4. For each of today's key terms (a deductible (invented figures)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
