Remember

Vuelto from volver, puesto from poner, and visto from ver, each with the already-taught helping forms of haber.

1. Translate and label: Ella ha vuelto a casa. Ella ha puesto el libro. Ella ha visto el libro.

2. Which verb does vuelto belong to, and why is *volvido still a miss?

3. Why is a present-perfect subjunctive still reserved?

4. Write three original sentences, one each with ha vuelto, ha puesto, and ha escrito, and translate them.

5. Change hemos visto to hemos vuelto, and translate both.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
