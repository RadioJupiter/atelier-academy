Remember

A triangle’s area is ½bh, with h perpendicular to the chosen base. A triangle is half of a rectangle with the same base and the same perpendicular height. A slanted side is not h.

Key terms: area of a triangle, perpendicular height.

1. A triangle has base 10 and perpendicular height 6. What is its area?

2. A rectangle is 9 by 4. What is the area of one of the two triangles made by a diagonal?

3. For each of today's key terms (area of a triangle, perpendicular height), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Find the area of a triangle with base 7 and perpendicular height 8.
