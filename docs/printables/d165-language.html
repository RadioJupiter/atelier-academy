Remember

Last week: dicho, hecho, and escrito, with he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han. This week: the same helping forms plus visto, from ver. Still not hay, and not haya. Other irregular participles remain reserved.

1. Give one present-perfect sentence with visto, and one with dicho, hecho, or escrito, each with a gloss.

2. Choose: Ellos ___ (han / hay / haya) visto la carta; Ella ___ (ha / hay / haya) escrito una carta.

3. Why are other irregular participles still reserved?

4. Write a short mixed set of three original sentences: visto, last week’s irregular participle, and a sentence with hay, each with a gloss, and say how they differ.

5. Correct the miss “Nosotros hay visto la carta.”

6. What remains reserved?
