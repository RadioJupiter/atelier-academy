“Many skilful devices”; one is recorded: four-cornered courses bound by lead, not lime or asphalt. The ceiling is gold, yet the light from the stones prevails. Two equal stoas are assigned one to men worshippers and one to women. I.1.50–57.

1. What one device does Dewing record in I.1.50–53, and what binding material does he name?

2. What two materials does he say did not hold the stones together?

3. In I.1.54, what is overlaid with gold, and what prevails?

4. What are the two stoas, to whom are they assigned, and what does Dewing say distinguishes them?

5. Why must no official width or height be added, and why is I.1.58 not in this mix?
