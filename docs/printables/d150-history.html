Remember

The letter’s own figures are kept distinct: 107 leagues along Juana, 178 leagues along the north coast of Española, and 188 leagues on one side of Española, this last in the letter’s body, not in a footnote. Official areas and conversions are not invented.

1. What league figure does the letter give for Juana, and what comparison does it draw from that survey?

2. What league figure does it give for one side of Española, and why is that not Major’s footnote on the 178 leagues?

3. What does the letter say of Villa de Navidad, and what official quantities, areas, and identifications must you not add?

4. Quote one comparison with Spain or with England and Scotland, keeping Major’s wording.

5. How does this letter still differ from the Treaty of Tordesillas?

6. What remains reserved?
