Remember

The work is ascribed to God’s influence, not human power or skill. The experience comes again on later visits, as though the sight were new. No one has had a surfeit; they rejoice and later talk of it. I.1.61–63; no invented measurements.

Key terms: the sight new each time (Dewing), no surfeit of the spectacle (Dewing).

1. According to I.1.61, by what is the work so finely turned, and what happens to the mind?

2. What does I.1.62 say of each successive occasion?

3. What does I.1.63 say no one has ever had, and what do people do when they leave?

4. For each of today's key terms (the sight new each time (Dewing), no surfeit of the spectacle (Dewing)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
