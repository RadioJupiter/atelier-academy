Remember

Check tilt, triangle, and terminator first. Likeness is to this plate or plaster, by measurement, not a prettier invention. Then two or three accents, late and true. No named living sitter.

Key terms: accent (head), likeness to the plate.

1. Write three measurements you will take for the likeness check (ratios or alignments).

2. Name at most three accents you can actually see, and cross out any you cannot see with half-closed eyes.

3. For each of today's key terms (accent (head), likeness to the plate), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. What does “the plate wins” forbid you to do to a “too long” nose on a Bargue head?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
