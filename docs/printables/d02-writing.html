Remember

Copy exactly first (spelling, punctuation, line breaks if asked). Then vary: same skeleton, new nouns and verbs. Do not paraphrase loosely until the copy is accurate.

Key terms: imitatio, copia.

1. What do you do first in imitatio: copy or vary?

2. What is copia, in one sentence?

3. For each of today's key terms (imitatio, copia), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Copy the passage by hand, exactly, including the 18th-century spellings try'd and compleat. Then proofread letter by letter against the original. Count your errors.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
