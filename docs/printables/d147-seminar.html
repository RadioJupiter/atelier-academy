Remember

A waltz, in this first look, is a short invented figure in triple time, counted in threes. It is not minuet and trio, and not a copyrighted score.

1. What is a waltz in this first look, and how does it differ from last week’s minuet and trio?

2. State the invented figure in scale degrees, and say that it is counted in threes.

3. Why must the figure not be replaced by a copyrighted waltz?

4. For each of today's key terms (a waltz (invented figure)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
