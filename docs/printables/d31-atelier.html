Remember

Place the ear from the plate or plaster: height, distance, tilt, then a simple envelope. A remembered proportion is a check; the plate wins. No pasted diagram-ear.

Key terms: ear in context.

1. Draw the envelope of one ear from the plate, without inner ridges first.

2. Check its height against the brow and the base of the nose, and record what you found.

3. For each of today's key terms (ear in context), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Name one change in the ear’s silhouette when the head turns from front to three-quarter.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
