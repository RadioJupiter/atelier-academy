Remember

A grant, not a journal. A first-discovery claim kept together with peoples already living there. A forever grant, with a proviso, and no line of leagues in this text.

1. What does the recital claim was discovered, and what does it already say about the people living there?

2. To whom is the grant made, for how long, with what proviso, and with what later sentence about a right already acquired?

3. What remains reserved, and what official figures must you not invent?

4. On what date, and in what place, is the bull given, in Davenport’s close?

5. Why must the first-discovery claim be taught as a claim?

6. Why is a line of leagues not part of this week’s close?
