Remember

70 CE is the conventional event-year of the destruction Josephus dates to Vespasian's second year. He writes that the house was burnt without Caesar's approbation, and later that Vespasian housed, honored, and pensioned him. Read the first sentence with the second in view. Not a fall of Rome, and not a trial.
