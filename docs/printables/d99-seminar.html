Remember

A three-year table: each year’s simple interest is taken on the opening principal; a labeled invented principal repayment is then subtracted; interest is paid separately and is not added to the principal. Invented example: $300 at 10%, $100 principal a year, interest $30, $20, $10. Not a bank APR, and not compound interest in this table.

Key terms: three-year repayment table (invented).

1. What is a three-year repayment table, in this lesson’s school sense?

2. For the invented $300 at 10% with $100 principal a year, write the three rows and the total interest.

3. Why is the interest not added to the principal in this table?

4. For the invented $400 at 5% with $100 principal a year, write three rows and the total interest, and say what principal would still remain.
