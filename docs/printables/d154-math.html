Remember

A circular segment is the region between an arc and its chord. Its area is the sector area minus the triangular piece. For a sector with angle θ at the center, the triangle’s area is (1/2)r² sin θ, and the segment area is (1/2)r²θ − (1/2)r² sin θ.

1. What is a circular segment in this lesson, and how is its area found from a sector?

2. On an invented figure, r = 2 and θ = π/2, what is the segment area?

3. Why is Dewing’s “segment of a circle” a miss for this area?

4. For each of today's key terms (area of a circular segment), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
