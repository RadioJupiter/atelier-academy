Remember

Imperfect -ar: hablaba, with -ábamos in the “we” form, for ongoing or habitual past action in this first look. Preterite -ar: hablé, with hablamos in the “we” form, for a completed event. The two tenses are not interchangeable.

Key terms: imperfect and preterite of regular -ar verbs, contrasted.

1. Give the six imperfect forms and the six preterite forms of hablar.

2. How do hablábamos and hablamos differ in tense and in use, in this first look?

3. Write one habitual imperfect sentence with caminaba, and one completed preterite sentence with caminé.

4. Why is this not a first look at the preterite of hablar?
