Third figure: the middle term is the subject of both premises. Darapti is AAI-3: All M are P; all M are S; therefore some S are P, not all S are P. Last week’s Baroco is a second-figure contrast, not a first look. Classroom terms are invented.

Key terms: third figure, Darapti.

1. What is the third figure, in this lesson’s school sense, and how does it differ from the second?

2. What is Darapti, in letters and in words?

3. Why is “all S are P” a miss as a conclusion from those two premises?

4. Give a classroom invented Darapti, and mark the middle term.
