Remember

Darcy speaks of a “country neighbourhood.” Mrs. Bennet says, “I know we dine with four and twenty families.” “Every body was surprised.” “Neighbourhood” and “Every body” stay inside quotations. Poetry is called “the food of love,” and Elizabeth answers that “one good sonnet will starve it entirely away.” Chapter 10 remains reserved.

1. What does Darcy say of a “country neighbourhood,” and what does Mrs. Bennet claim about families?

2. What does Darcy say of poetry, and what does Elizabeth answer?

3. Why do “neighbourhood” and “Every body” stay inside quotations?

4. For each of today's key terms (Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 9, a “country neighbourhood” and four and twenty families), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
