Remember

Canto II opens at nightfall; the speaker invokes the Muses, doubts that he is sufficient, says he is not Æneas and not Paul, and, in lines 37–42, unwills what he willed — thinking consumes the emprise that was prompt at the start. The saintly Lady of II.52 is reserved.
