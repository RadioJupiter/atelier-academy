Remember

Old Kingdom: the pyramid age (3rd–6th Dynasties); years in BCE are uncertain by decades. The Great Pyramid is Khufu’s royal tomb at Giza, not a riddle without evidence. Herodotus is a later traveler: use him as a source for what he heard, not as a 4th-Dynasty record.

Key terms: Old Kingdom, pyramid (Egyptian).

1. In your own words, what is a pyramid of this period for, in the evidence we have, and what is a common later guess that the lesson told you not to prefer?

2. Name one earlier form of royal tomb that the true pyramid developed from.

3. For each of today's key terms (Old Kingdom, pyramid (Egyptian)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Read a short public-domain passage of Herodotus, Book 2, on the pyramids. List three claims, and for each say whether it can be checked against the monument or only against other texts.
