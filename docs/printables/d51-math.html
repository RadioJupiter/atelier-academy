Remember

Read a quadratic inequality from the graph. Roots mark the splits. For a parabola that opens upward, y 0 outside them. Strict inequalities do not include the roots. Do not treat it as a linear “move the terms” problem.

Key terms: quadratic inequality.

1. Solve (x − 3)(x − 7) < 0.

2. Solve (x + 1)(x − 4) ≥ 0. (The roots are −1 and 4; now include them.)

3. For each of today's key terms (quadratic inequality), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “both factors less than 0” not a complete solution of (x − 2)(x − 6) < 0?
