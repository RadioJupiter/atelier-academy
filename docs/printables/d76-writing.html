Remember

Circular reasoning supports a claim with a restatement of the claim, or with a premise that already assumes the conclusion. It is a fallacy, not merely a false claim. Invented examples are labeled invented.

Key terms: circular reasoning.

1. Rewrite the invented ‘fair rule’ sentence so that it offers a ground instead of a circle (the ground may also be invented and labeled).

2. How is a circle different from a straw man?

3. For each of today's key terms (circular reasoning), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Invent one short circular argument, label it invented, and name the repeated claim.
