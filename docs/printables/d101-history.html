Remember

The eastern arch is finished. The south and north arches are another story: the lōri had been raised; everything underneath was “labouring under their load”; the columns threw off tiny flakes, as if planed; once more the master-builders, dismayed, reported the problem to the Emperor. Quote “labouring.” Invent no measurement. The remedy waits until Wednesday.
