The law of refraction is n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂, with both angles measured from the normal. Official Example 25.2: air n₁ = 1.00, θ₁ = 30.0°, θ₂ = 22.0°, n₂ = 0.500 / 0.375 = 1.33, the book’s official three-digit index for water. Example 25.3 remains reserved.

3. Why is official Example 25.3 a miss for today?
