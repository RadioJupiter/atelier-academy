Remember

Lines 112–136, in Longfellow, continue Virgilius’s reply after a reserved passage: he will be the guide through the eternal place; a more worthy soul is named for a later ascent; the speaker entreats him and then follows. Quote Longfellow’s wording. Lines 91–111 remain reserved.

Key terms: Virgilius as guide (Longfellow, Inferno I).

1. What, in the set lines, will the speaker hear and see in “the eternal place”? Give Longfellow’s wording, not a later canto from memory.

2. Who, according to these lines, will be left for an ascent that Virgilius says is not through him, and what reason does he give?

3. For each of today's key terms (Virgilius as guide (Longfellow, Inferno I)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Quote the speaker’s entreaty beginning “Poet, I thee entreat,” through “Saint Peter,” or give those lines faithfully with Longfellow’s wording.
