Remember

Iba (ir), era (ser), veía (ver). Ir and ser share the preterite fui, but not the imperfect. Veía keeps the stem e; vía is a miss. In this first look, the imperfect is for ongoing or habitual past action, or for a past state with era; the already-taught preterite is for a completed event. Instruction remains in English.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of ir, of ser, and of ver.

2. How do yo iba, yo era, and yo veía differ, and what preterite do ir and ser share?

3. Write a short habitual imperfect sentence and a short completed preterite sentence, using ir or ver.

4. Why is vía a miss, and how can you tell yo era from ella era?

5. Translate cada mañana íbamos and ayer fuimos.

6. Give yo veía / yo vi and nosotros éramos / nosotros fuimos.
