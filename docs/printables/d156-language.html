Remember

The present perfect may use the irregular participles dicho, hecho, and escrito, with the same helping forms he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han. He dicho is I have said, or I have told. It is still not hay, and not haya. Visto remains reserved.

1. What are the three irregular participles taught today, and from which infinitives do they come?

2. Translate the original sentence “Nosotros hemos escrito,” and say why “hay escrito” would be a miss.

3. Why is visto a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (irregular participles dicho, hecho, and escrito, a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
