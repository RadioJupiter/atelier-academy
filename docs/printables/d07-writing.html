Remember

Scramble the hints, then choose an order (often time order, or claim then reason). Write from that order. Compare with the original and with your first rewrite.

Key terms: revision, diction.

1. Why scramble the hints at all?

2. Name one standard order you might choose.

3. For each of today's key terms (revision, diction), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. (20 min) From the hints alone, write Addison's paragraph out in full, "as fully as it had been expressed before, in any suitable words that should come to hand" (Franklin's own phrase). Aim for the same number of sentences and a similar length.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
