Week 2 · Day 7 · Tuesday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practise only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Scramble the hints, then choose an order (often time order, or claim then reason). Write from that order. Compare with the original and with your first rewrite.

1. Why scramble the hints at all?

2. Name one standard order you might choose.

3. (20 min) From the hints alone, write Addison's paragraph out in full, "as fully as it had been expressed before, in any suitable words that should come to hand" (Franklin's own phrase). Aim for the same number of sentences and a similar length.

4. (10 min) Now open Day 4's lesson and compare sentence by sentence. Mark: ✓ where your version says the same thing; ✗ where you lost an idea; ★ where you think your sentence is clearer.

5. (15 min) List five specific differences in style (word choice, sentence length, order, figures of speech) between your version and Addison's. For each, say which is better and why.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
