Remember

Chapter 6 opens with the visit to Netherfield. Charlotte says a woman “had better shew more affection than she feels.” Elizabeth answers that Jane “has known him only a fortnight.” 1813 wording stays inside quotations. Darcy’s later interest waits until Thursday.

1. In the 1813 wording, how does the chapter open, and what does Elizabeth see in Jane, including her “composure of temper”?

2. What does Charlotte advise, including “shew,” and how does Elizabeth answer with “a fortnight”?

3. What does Charlotte say of happiness in marriage, and why must “shew,” “fortnight,” “to-morrow,” and “twelvemonth” stay inside quotations?

4. For each of today's key terms (Charlotte on Jane and Bingley (Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 6)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
