Remember

A sequence repeats an invented figure from a new starting degree. Retrograde reverses it. Inversion turns it upside down. Retrograde inversion does both. Do not use a copyrighted tune.

1. What is a sequence, in the already-taught first look, and what must you not use as the figure?

2. Sequence E–F–G–B one step higher, and name the shape you are keeping.

3. How does a sequence differ from retrograde inversion?

4. Give the retrograde of C–D–E–G, and say what remains reserved.

5. Sequence G–A–C one step lower, keeping the same intervals.
