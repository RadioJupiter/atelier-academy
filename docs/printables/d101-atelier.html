Remember

The eye is a volume in the already-placed socket, not a flat symbol. The lids wrap that volume. The opening is a slot, not a black outline. A seen highlight stays small and is not invented. Do not stamp the second eye from the first. Full color remains reserved.

Key terms: the eye as a volume in the socket.

1. What is the eye, in this lesson’s school sense, on the monochrome head?

2. How are the lids and the opening judged?

3. If the light is from above, what shade may the upper lid throw, and how light may a highlight become?

4. What must you not do with the second eye?
