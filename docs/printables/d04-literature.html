Literature · Iliad, Books 2–3

Week 1 · Day 4 · Thursday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

NameDate

Remember

Book 2: a false dream, Agamemnon's test, the rush to the ships, Odysseus and Thersites, the Catalogue of Ships (which may be skimmed). Book 3: the duel of Paris and Menelaus, Helen on the wall, Aphrodite's rescue of Paris. Thersites says some of the same things Achilles said; the poem treats them differently because of rank.

Key terms: catalogue, simile.

1. Write one sentence beginning with Although that states a judgment about Paris in Book 3.

2. For each of today's key terms (catalogue, simile), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

3. Why might the poet show the whole army nearly running home in Book 2? What does it reveal about Agamemnon's leadership?

4. Thersites says many of the same things Achilles said in Book 1. Why is his speech treated so differently? What does this suggest about the poem's view of rank?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
