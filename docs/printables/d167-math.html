Remember

If f is not defined at a, f is not continuous at a, even when lim x→a f(x) exists. Week 32’s invented f(x) = (x² − 4)/(x − 2) has limit 4 at 2, but is not continuous at 2.

1. Why is week 32’s invented f(x) = (x² − 4)/(x − 2) not continuous at 2, even though the limit is 4?

2. On invented p(x) = x + 2, why is p continuous at 2?

3. Is invented h(x) = (x² − 1)/(x − 1) continuous at 1? Give the limit, and say why.

4. For each of today's key terms (a function not continuous at a point where it is not defined), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
