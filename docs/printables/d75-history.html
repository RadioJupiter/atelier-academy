Remember

Anthemius prepares designs and regulates the work; Isidorus, a Milesian, is associated with him as another master-builder. The church soars “to a height to match the sky,” is carefully proportioned in breadth and length, and abounds in sunlight and in light from the marble. No figure is invented.

1. What is Isidorus called in the set extract, and of what city is he “by birth”?

2. What, according to Dewing, reflects the sun’s rays in the church?

3. Quote “a spectacle of marvellous beauty,” and say what a paraphrase may do with the spelling.

4. How do this week’s extracts continue last week’s, without beginning the fire again as a first look?
