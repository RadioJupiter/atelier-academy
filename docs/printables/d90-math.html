Remember

V = B × h. A box is the rectangular case. A cylinder uses B = πr². A tube uses B = π(R² − r²). Keep area and volume distinct.

1. A classroom box is 9 by 3 by 4. What is its volume?

2. A right prism has a triangular base of area 11 and height 6. What is V?

3. A classroom cylinder has radius 2 and height 9. What is V, in terms of π?

4. A classroom tube has R = 5, r = 4, and h = 6. What is the volume of the material, in terms of π?

5. A classroom cylinder has volume 81π cubic units and radius 3. What is its height?
