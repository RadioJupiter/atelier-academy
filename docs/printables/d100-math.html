Remember

Right square pyramid: B + 2bl, with l the face slant height, and l² = h² + (b/2)² if needed. Right cone: πrl + πr², with l = √(r² + h²). A composite volume adds the pieces; a composite surface counts only exposed faces.

1. State the surface-area formulas for a right square pyramid and for a right cone.

2. A classroom right square pyramid has base 6 by 6 and l = 5. What is the surface area?

3. A classroom right cone has r = 3 and h = 4. What is the total surface area?

4. A cube of edge 6 has a right square pyramid of height 4 and slant height 5 on the top. Give the volume and the surface area, and say why they are not found in the same way.

5. A classroom right cone has r = 6 and l = 10. Find the total surface area, in terms of π.

6. A cylinder of radius 3 and height 10 has a cone of radius 3 and height 4 on one end. Find the volume, in terms of π.
