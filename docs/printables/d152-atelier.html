Remember

A small light value on the cylinder is a lighter touch from the same four paints, placed where the one light reaches the form most directly. It is not a fifth tube, and it does not replace the turning values.

1. What is a small light value on the cylinder in this lesson, and from which paints is it mixed?

3. Why is a fifth tube a miss?
