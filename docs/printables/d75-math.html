Remember

A = ½bh, with h perpendicular. In a right triangle, A = ½ab with a and b the legs. In an isosceles triangle, the altitude to the base bisects the base; find h from a² + b² = c², then use ½bh.

1. A triangle has base 10 and perpendicular height 9. What is its area?

2. A rectangle 8 by 5 is divided by a diagonal. What is the area of one of the triangles?

3. An isosceles triangle has equal sides 10 and 10 and base 12. Find h and the area.

4. Why is a slanted side not used as h in A = ½bh?
