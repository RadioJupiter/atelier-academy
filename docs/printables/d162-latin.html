Remember

Nē ēgrediātur is a negative purpose with ēgredior: in order that he not go out, or in order that she not go out. It is not ēgrediētur, and not last week’s ut ēgrediātur. A result clause remains reserved.

1. What is nē ēgrediātur, and how does it differ from ut ēgrediātur?

2. Translate “Puella sedet nē ēgrediātur,” and say why “nē ēgrediētur” would be a miss.

3. Why is a result clause still a miss?

4. For each of today's key terms (a purpose clause with nē and ēgredior), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
