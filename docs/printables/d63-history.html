Remember

476 CE is a conventional later marker for the deposition of Romulus Augustulus by Odoacer and the end of the western imperial line in Italy. The eastern empire continued. 476 is not 410, not a sourced quotation in this course, and not a day on which the empire vanished.

Key terms: 476 as a conventional year.

1. What happened in 476, as this lesson uses the year, and who were Romulus Augustulus and Odoacer?

2. Why is “the empire vanished in 476” the wrong sentence for this course?

3. How does this lesson know 476, compared with how it knew 410?

4. Where does the eastern empire fit in a careful sentence about 476?
