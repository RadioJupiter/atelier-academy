Remember

Scattered sunlight is partly polarized, most strongly about 90° from the Sun. For any wave in vacuum, c = fλ, with c = 299,792,458 m/s exactly (the definition of the meter). Never look at the Sun itself. Use a polarizing filter or sunglasses only on the sky, away from the Sun's disk.
