Remember

AA similarity: two equal angles are enough. Corresponding sides are in the same ratio k. A 3-4-5 triangle and a 6-8-10 triangle have k = 2. Classroom lengths are invented.

1. What is AA similarity, and why is a third equal angle not required as a separate given?

2. A classroom 5-12-13 triangle is enlarged by k = 2. What are the new sides?

3. Why is “the triangles are the same size” a miss for similar?

4. A classroom 6-8-10 triangle is reduced so the hypotenuse is 5. What is k from the 6-8-10 triangle, and what are the new legs?

5. Two right triangles share an acute angle. One has legs 3 and 4. The similar triangle has the side corresponding to 3 equal to 9. Find the other leg and the hypotenuse.
