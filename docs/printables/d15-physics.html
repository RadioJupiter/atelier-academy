Remember

Horizontal: v_x is constant. Vertical: free fall, a_y = g down. Time of flight comes from the vertical motion; range = v_x t. The two components do not change each other.

Key terms: projectile, range (of a projectile).

1. A body is thrown horizontally at 15 m/s from a height of 4.9 m. Find the time of flight and the range. (g = 9.8 m/s².)

2. From 19.6 m, a horizontal throw of 8 m/s is made. Find the range. Would a faster throw land later?

3. For each of today's key terms (projectile, range (of a projectile)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A launch at 30° with speed 10 m/s has what vx and vy at the start? (Use Day 13’s 30° values.)

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
