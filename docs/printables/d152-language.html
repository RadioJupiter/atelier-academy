Remember

He, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han, plus a regular participle. Ha is not hay, and not haya. Irregular participles remain reserved.

1. Give the six helping forms, and build he vivido, ha comido, and hemos hablado, with glosses.

2. Choose: Espero que ___ (ha / hay / haya) tiempo, and say why the present perfect is a miss in that sentence.

3. Why is hecho a miss today?

4. Write short original sentences for he hablado, habéis vivido, and han comido, with glosses.

5. Contrast has comido, hay, and haya in three short glosses.

6. What remains reserved?
