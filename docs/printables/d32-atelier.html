Remember

Hair is two or three value masses on the skull first. Strands, if any, come after. The plate wins. Do not bury the ear’s placement under an invented style.

Key terms: hair as mass.

1. Squint at the plate and list the hair masses before you draw them.

2. Add or correct the masses on the study, still without a full head of strands.

3. For each of today's key terms (hair as mass), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Where a mass overlaps the ear, what do you keep from yesterday?
