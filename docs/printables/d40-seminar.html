Shelley, “Ozymandias,” 1818, public domain. Short attributed extracts only. The inscription boasts; the traveler sees a wreck. The poem is not a source for the Pantheon or for 27 BCE.

Key terms: Ozymandias (Shelley, 1818).

1. Write the opening fragment from the lesson, and attribute the poem.

2. In your own words, what does the inscription claim, and what does the traveler report seeing?

3. For each of today's key terms (Ozymandias (Shelley, 1818)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A short paragraph whose topic sentence is “A boast and a wreck can sit in the same poem.” Use at most one short attributed phrase.
