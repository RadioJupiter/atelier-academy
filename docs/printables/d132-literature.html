Remember

Chapter 3, in the 1813 wording, brings the Netherfield party to the assembly. Darcy is noticed for his person and for the report of “ten thousand a year,” the novel’s figure, then “discovered to be proud.” The overheard “tolerable” remark waits until Thursday.

1. Who makes up the party that enters the assembly, and what report circulates about Darcy within five minutes?

2. How does Bingley’s behaviour contrast with Darcy’s, and why is “ten thousand a year” not an official salary?

3. What 1813 forms must stay inside quotations, and what remains reserved until Thursday?

4. For each of today's key terms (the assembly (Pride and Prejudice, Chapter 3)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
