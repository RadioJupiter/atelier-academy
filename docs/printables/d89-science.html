Remember

At 0 °C, Harvey/NIST gives ice Ih cp = 2.10 J g⁻¹ K⁻¹. Melting 1 g at 0 °C takes 333.4 J, about 159 times the 2.10 J needed to warm that gram of ice by 1 °C, using those two 0 °C figures. Liquid-water cp is not invented.
