Remember

A précis restates meaning in short, complete sentences, in your own words. No quotation leftovers, no hints-only list, no opinion.

Key terms: précis.

1. Write a one- or two-sentence précis of the first model sentence.

2. Name one phrase you were tempted to copy, and show the wording you used instead.

3. For each of today's key terms (précis), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a structural précis of whichever sentence you did not use in Check 1 (one sentence).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
