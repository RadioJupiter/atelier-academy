Remember

The set opening of Inferno, Canto I, lines 1–9, is Longfellow’s 1867 translation. The speaker is midway in life, lost in a dark forest, the straightforward pathway gone. Later lines remain reserved. Quote Longfellow’s wording, not a remembered version. The English lines are unrhymed; they do not keep Dante’s terza rima.

Key terms: Inferno, canto.

1. Quote the first three lines exactly, as Longfellow wrote them.

2. What does “the straightforward pathway had been lost” tell you, in your own words?

3. Why must you not quote a rhyming English version as if it were this translation?

4. What remains reserved this week, and why does that matter?
