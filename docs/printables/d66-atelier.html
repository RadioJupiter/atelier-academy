Remember

On the blocked-in head, each eye is first a socket: a shallow plane under the brow, level with its pair, without lashes or a premature highlight. Features are fitted to last week’s proportions, not drawn as a mask.

Key terms: the eye socket, as a plane.

1. How do you keep the two sockets level on a head that is not turned?

2. Why is a copied photographic sparkle in the eye a miss at this stage?

3. For each of today's key terms (the eye socket, as a plane), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. If the head is slightly turned, what happens to the farther socket, as a form, before any lash is drawn?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
