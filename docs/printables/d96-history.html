Remember

Dewing cannot give a precise account of all the treasures, and offers one example: the Inner Sanctuary, for priests only, has forty thousand pounds’ weight of silver. The church of Constantinople is called the Great Church. The figure is Dewing’s and is not converted. No second count is invented.

Key terms: Inner Sanctuary (Dewing), forty thousand pounds’ weight of silver (Dewing).

1. Why will Dewing not give a precise account of all the treasures?

2. What is the Inner Sanctuary, and who may enter it?

3. What figure does he give, and what must this course not invent from it?

4. What are men accustomed to call the church of Constantinople, in I.1.66?
