Remember

A triad is in root position when the root is in the bass, in first inversion when the third is in the bass, and in second inversion when the fifth is in the bass. The three tones stay the same. This is not interval inversion (Day 37).

Key terms: triad inversion.

1. In your own words, what is first inversion of a triad, and what is second inversion?

2. For an F-major triad (F, A, C), name the bass in root position, first inversion, and second inversion.

3. How is triad inversion different from the interval inversion taught on Day 37?

4. A student hears E–G–C and calls it a new triad, not a C-major triad. What has been missed?
