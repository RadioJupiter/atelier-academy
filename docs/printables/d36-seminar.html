The undistributed middle is an invalid form: the middle term never covers its whole class, so it does not link S to P. Barbara is different: “All M are P” distributes M. Day 6’s propositional fallacies are a different family.

Key terms: undistributed middle.

2. State Barbara in letters, and mark where M is distributed, as taught today.

3. For each of today's key terms (undistributed middle), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. How is this fallacy different from affirming the consequent (Day 6)?
