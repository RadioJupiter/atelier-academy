Remember

Take a known invented total — principal plus an already-computed simple-interest charge — and divide it into equal installments. Simple and compound interest are not first looks. No official bank rate is invented. A declining-balance method is reserved.

Key terms: equal installment repayment.

1. An invented $500 plus $40 is repaid in 6 equal monthly installments. What is each payment, and what total is being split?

2. Why is “$500 ÷ 6” a miss for that example?

3. Why is this not a first look at simple interest?

4. For each of today's key terms (equal installment repayment), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
