Remember

A short invented story can be told with regular -ar preterite forms (miré, compraste, cocinamos, cantaron). Keep the tense consistent. Nosotros -amos still looks like the present. Do not retell this week’s history in Spanish, and do not use an irregular past that has not been taught.

1. Write Spanish for “I cooked,” “she looked,” and “you (ustedes) bought.”

2. Write three original preterite sentences that make a short invented kitchen story.

3. In Nosotros cocinamos, how do you know the tense if there is no time word?

4. Why does this lesson refuse a Spanish retelling of the sack of Rome?

5. Change to the preterite: Yo compro pan. Ella canta. Ustedes miran.

6. Write “we spoke” and “we speak,” and say how a reader can tell them apart.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
