Remember

Dormir in the preterite: dormí, dormiste, durmió, dormimos, dormisteis, durmieron. Only the third persons change o to u. Do not copy pedir’s i, and do not use the present ue in the preterite.

Key terms: preterite of dormir.

1. Give the six preterite forms of dormir, and mark which two change o to u.

2. Why are duermió and dirmió both misses?

3. Write a short sentence with dormí, and one with durmieron.

4. How is this the same shape as pedir, and how are the vowels different?
