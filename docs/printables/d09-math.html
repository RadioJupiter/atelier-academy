Remember

One input, one output. Graph = points (x, f(x)). Vertical-line test. For y = mx + c, m is the slope (rise/run) and c is where the line meets the y-axis.

Key terms: function, vertex.

1. Rewrite y = x² + 4x − 1 by completing the square, and state the vertex.

2. The graph of y = f(x − 3) + 2 is the graph of y = f(x) shifted how?

3. For each of today's key terms (function, vertex), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Sketch on one set of axes, without plotting points: y = |x|, y = |x − 2|, y = |x| − 2, y = |x − 2| − 1.
