Remember

In Gray’s 1918 wording, the twelfth thoracic vertebra has the same general characteristics as the eleventh, but its inferior articular surfaces are “convex and directed lateralward, like those of the lumbar vertebræ,” and the body, laminae, and spinous process “resemble the lumbar vertebræ.” Each transverse process is “subdivided into three elevations.” “Vertebræ” stays inside the quotation. Official lengths remain reserved. The lesson is not a diagnosis. The lumbar vertebrae remain reserved.
