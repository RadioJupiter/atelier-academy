Remember

Gray names eight carpals in two rows, radial to ulnar. Proximal: navicular, lunate, triangular, pisiform. Distal: greater multangular, lesser multangular, capitate, hamate. The carpus as a group was already taught. Later names are a gloss only.

Key terms: the eight carpal bones.

1. How many carpal bones does Gray name, and in how many rows?

2. Give the proximal row, from the radial to the ulnar side, in Gray’s names.

3. Give the distal row, in the same order, in Gray’s names.

4. For each of today's key terms (the eight carpal bones), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
