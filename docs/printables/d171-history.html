Remember

The letter names three islands: Turuqueira, Ceyre, and Ayay. Ceyre was the first that they saw. There is a resemblance among the natives of all these, as if they were of one race, and they do no injury to each other. Footnotes are not the body.

1. Name the three islands, in the letter’s wording, and say which was the first that they saw.

2. What does the letter say of the resemblance among the natives of all these?

3. Why is a footnote naming “Guadaloupe” not today’s extract?

4. For each of today's key terms (three islands, Turuqueira, Ceyre, and Ayay, from the second-voyage letter), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
