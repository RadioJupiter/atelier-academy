Remember

A = (1/2)r s, and also A = (1/2)r²θ, with θ = s/r in radians. Do not write A = (1/2)r²s. Do not treat s as if it were already the area.

1. State A = (1/2)r s, and show in one line how it comes from A = (1/2)r²θ and s = rθ.

2. On an invented figure, r = 10 and s = 4, what is the sector area, and what is θ?

3. Why is a circular segment still a miss today?

4. For each of today's key terms (sector area from the arc, A = (1/2)r s), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
