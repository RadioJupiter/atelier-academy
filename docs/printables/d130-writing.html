Writing · Friday mix: a narrative, and a fable distinguished from it

Week 26 · Day 130 · Friday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

A narrative: a short complete action, in order, with when and where, and no moral. A fable: the same kind of short action, with a stated moral at the end.

1. Write a short narrative, with when and where, in order, and with no moral.

2. In a separate paragraph, say how a fable differs from that narrative.

3. What would break either piece, and what remains reserved?

4. Show the invented ink-jar event once as a narrative and once as a fable.

5. Why must the moral stay off the narrative?

6. Quote last week’s Townsend moral only if you are naming a fable, not a narrative.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
