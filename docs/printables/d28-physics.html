Remember

In the no-friction model, KE + PE is constant. From rest, m g h = ½ m v², so v = √(2 g h). Pick one zero of height. Friday will treat friction as a case where this sum is not constant.

Key terms: mechanical energy (no friction), conservation (mechanical, first model).

1. From rest at 2 m, no friction, find the speed at the floor. (Leave a square root if it is not a perfect square, or compute 2gh first.)

2. A 2 kg object starts from rest at 2.5 m and passes a point 1 m above the floor. Find KE and PE there, and v².

3. For each of today's key terms (mechanical energy (no friction), conservation (mechanical, first model)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. A 3 kg object is moving at 4 m/s at a height of 2 m (floor = 0). Find the mechanical energy, and find v² at the floor if no friction acts.
