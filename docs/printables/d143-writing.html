An original refutation names an invented claim, labeled invented, and gives short reasons why the claim should not be accepted. No living person.

1. Write one short invented claim, labeled invented, and two short reasons it should not be accepted.

2. Why is this a refutation rather than a maxim or a chreia?

3. Why must you not present the claim as a sourced proverb or as a living person’s statement?

4. Revise an invented claim that names a living person, and say why the name must come out.

5. Write a second invented claim, still general, and one reason not to accept it.
