Remember

Rēs, reī: a thing or matter, fifth declension, feminine. Nihil: nothing. Inquit: says, inserted in direct speech. Not a first look at the Res Gestae as a source title.

Key terms: rēs, inquit.

1. Give the English of Nihil videō.

2. Write one original sentence that uses inquit with a short quoted phrase already taught (for example mēcum).

3. For each of today's key terms (rēs, inquit), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is today’s rēs not the same job as the Res Gestae on Day 33?
