Remember

Pud- poder, managed. Pus- poner, put. Sup- saber, found out. Quis- querer, wanted / tried. Negative quiso is often “refused,” as a careful school pattern. These are not last week’s uv-stems.

1. Give the six preterite forms of poder and of poner.

2. Give the six preterite forms of saber and of querer.

3. Translate, in this lesson’s school senses: Ayer supe la verdad; no quiso salir; pude terminar; puse el libro en la mesa. (Invented.)

4. How is supe not pude, and how is puse not tuve?

5. What first-person preterites did last week already teach, and why are they not first looks today?
