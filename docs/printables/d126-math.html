Remember

On the unit circle, radius 1, cos θ = x and sin θ = y. At 0°, (1, 0); at 90°, (0, 1). This agrees with last term’s right-triangle ratios in the first quadrant. Radians remain reserved.

1. What is the unit circle, and, if the terminal ray meets it at P(x, y), what are cos θ and sin θ?

2. Give the coordinates of P, and the sine and cosine, at 0° and at 90°.

3. Why does this agree, in the first quadrant, with last term’s right-triangle cosine, and what remains reserved?
