Remember

On an invented fair cube, E = (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6) / 6 = 21/6 = 7/2. The figure is invented-classroom arithmetic, not an official value of a real game. Independent events and mutually exclusive events remain different jobs, already taught.

1. What is the expected value, in this lesson, of an invented fair cube, and what is that number?

2. Show the arithmetic for E = 7/2 in two ways: as an average of the faces, and as a sum of (value × probability).

3. Why is last week’s independent-events formula a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (expected value on an invented fair cube), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
