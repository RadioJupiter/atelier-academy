Remember

From P outside a circle, a tangent touches at T and a secant cuts at A (nearer) and B (farther). Euclid III.36: PT² = PA × PB. Lengths are positive. This is not the intersecting-chords theorem (III.35).

Key terms: secant, the tangent-secant theorem.

1. PA = 3, PB = 12. Find PT.

2. PT = 10, PA = 5. Find PB.

3. For each of today's key terms (secant, the tangent-secant theorem), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is PT², and not PT, the quantity that matches PA × PB?
