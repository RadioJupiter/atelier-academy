Remember

If A and 90° − A are the two acute angles of a right triangle, tan A and tan(90° − A) are reciprocals, because the opposite and adjacent sides swap. In a classroom 3-4-5 triangle, tan A = 3/4 and tan(90° − A) = 4/3. Degrees only.
