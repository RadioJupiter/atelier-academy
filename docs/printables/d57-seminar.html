Remember

Authentic cadence: V–I, a close. Half cadence: a pause on V, not an end. Major key; I, IV, and V only. A deceptive cadence can still wait.

1. In your own words, how is a half cadence different from an authentic cadence?

2. Write I–V and V–I in C major, and label which is the pause and which can close.

3. Why is “every stop in a song is a half cadence” a miss?

4. Why does a deceptive cadence still wait?

5. A short because / but / so about a pause on V.
