Remember

Authentic cadence: V–I, a close. Half cadence: a pause on V, not an end. Major key; I, IV, and V only. A deceptive cadence can still wait.

Key terms: a close and a pause, a Term 1 close.

1. In your own words, how is a half cadence different from an authentic cadence?

2. Write I–V and V–I in C major, and label which is the pause and which can close.

3. For each of today's key terms (a close and a pause, a Term 1 close), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why is “every stop in a song is a half cadence” a miss?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
