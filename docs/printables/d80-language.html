Remember

Tuve, estuve, anduve share the uv-stem. Fui is ser or ir; hice / hizo is hacer; regular preterites stay regular. Do not swap fui and estuve.

1. Write the six forms of the preterite of tener.

2. Give the English of Ayer estuve en casa and of Ayer fui a la tienda, as invented drills, and name the verbs.

3. Hear tuvo, hizo, and fue. Give the English of each, and the verb.

4. Why are tuve, estuve, and anduve taught as one pattern?
