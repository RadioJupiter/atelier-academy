Remember

Regular -ar imperfect: hablaba, with -aba. Regular -er and -ir imperfect: comía, vivía, with -ía. In this first look, the imperfect is for ongoing or habitual past action; the already-taught preterite is for a completed event. Yo hablaba and ella hablaba share a form; yo comía and ella comía share a form. Ir, ser, and ver remain reserved.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of hablar, of comer, and of vivir.

2. How do the -ar endings differ from the -er and -ir endings in the imperfect?

3. Write a short habitual imperfect sentence and a short completed preterite sentence, using caminar or comer.

4. Why are iba, era, and veía reserved, and how can you tell yo hablaba from ella hablaba?

5. Translate cada mañana practicábamos and ayer practicamos.

6. Give yo leía / yo leí and nosotros vivíamos / nosotros vivimos.
