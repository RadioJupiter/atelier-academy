Remember

Desde = from / since (a start). Hasta = until / as far as (an end). Camino desde aquí hasta allí. Not a past tense, and not a swap of the two.

Key terms: desde and hasta.

1. Write: “I walk as far as there.”

2. Correct: Camino hasta aquí desde allí if the intended meaning is “I walk from here to there.”

3. For each of today's key terms (desde and hasta), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
