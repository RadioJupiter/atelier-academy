Remember

Two upright objects in the same light make similar right triangles with their shadows (AA: both have a right angle, and the sun’s rays are parallel, so the acute angles match). A known height and shadow find an unknown height from the same scale factor.

1. Why are the pole-and-shadow and tree-and-shadow triangles similar?

2. A 2.5 m post casts a 4.0 m shadow. A building’s shadow is 20 m. Find the height of the building.

3. A student writes 2/9 = 3/h for the Learn figure. What has gone wrong?

4. If a 3 m pole casts a 3 m shadow, what can you say about a nearby tree whose shadow is 12 m?

5. A 0.8 m rod casts a 1.2 m shadow. A tower in the same light casts a 15 m shadow. Find the height of the tower.

6. A tree is 8 m tall and casts a 6 m shadow. How long is the shadow of a 2 m post in the same light?
