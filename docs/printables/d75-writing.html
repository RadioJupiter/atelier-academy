Remember

A fallacy is a flaw in reasoning. A straw man answers a distorted claim. A false dilemma hides a genuine option. Ad hominem uses a personal trait in place of a test of the claim.

1. Why can a conclusion be true and the argument still be a fallacy?

2. In one sentence each, distinguish a straw man from ad hominem.

3. Invent (and label invented) one straw man, one false dilemma, and one ad hominem, each in a single short exchange.

4. Why is a genuine “either right-angled or not” not a false dilemma?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
