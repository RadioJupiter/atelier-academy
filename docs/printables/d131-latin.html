Remember

The pluperfect of sequor is secūtus eram, I had followed, the participle secūtus plus the imperfect of sum. It is not sequor, and not locūtus eram.

1. How is the pluperfect of sequor formed, and what are the six forms?

2. Translate: Puer mē secūtus erat. Nōs eum secūtī erāmus.

3. Why is locūtus eram a miss for I had followed, and what remains reserved until tomorrow?

4. For each of today's key terms (pluperfect of sequor), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
