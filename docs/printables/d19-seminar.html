Remember

Demand and supply are schedules, not single numbers. Equilibrium: quantity demanded = quantity supplied. Below that price, a shortage; above, a surplus. The canteen table is invented. A shift of a whole schedule is not the same as a movement along it.

Key terms: demand and supply, equilibrium, shortage, and surplus.

1. If the price is $5, what happens to unsold sandwiches in the model, and which way is the pressure on the price?

2. Invent a one-line change that would shift demand (not just move along it), and say whether the new equilibrium price would rise or fall, other things on the supply side equal.

3. For each of today's key terms (demand and supply, equilibrium, shortage, and surplus), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Fill in: at $4 there is neither shortage nor surplus in this table, because _____.
