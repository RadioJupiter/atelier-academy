Remember

On the aforesaid Sunday the letter records six islands: the first high and mountainous, the second flat and wooded. The pilots reckoned 800 leagues, or 780, from Ferro to first land, and 300 more from Ferro to Cadiz, 1100 in all. Those figures are the letter’s. Major’s footnotes name Dominica and Marigalante; those names are the notes, not the body.

1. How many islands does the letter record on the aforesaid Sunday, and how does it describe the first two?

2. What leagues do the pilots reckon, and why is an official modern conversion a miss?

3. What do Major’s footnotes name, and how must those names be kept distinct from the body?

4. For each of today's key terms (six islands on the aforesaid Sunday, and the pilots’ leagues), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
