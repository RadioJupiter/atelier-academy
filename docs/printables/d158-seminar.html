In Dewing I.5.1–6, the sea “adorns Byzantium,” “forming curving bays, contracting into narrow straits, and spreading into a great open sea,” and offering “the quiet shelter of harbours to navigators.” Two seas, the Aegean and the Euxine, “unite with each other to the east of the city.” “Shew” and “harbours” stay inside quotations. No official measurement is invented.
