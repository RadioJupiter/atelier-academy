Remember

A deceptive cadence is V–vi (or V–VI in minor): the ear is prepared for I and lands on vi instead. It is not a half cadence (ends on V, Day 52) and not an authentic V–I. Last week’s authentic and plagal closes stay those jobs.

Key terms: deceptive cadence.

1. Name the authentic, plagal, half, and deceptive landings, as taught in this course, in short Roman-numeral form.

2. In C major, why is G to C not a deceptive cadence?

3. For each of today's key terms (deceptive cadence), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write or play (if a keyboard is at hand) V–vi in C major, and say the two chord names.
