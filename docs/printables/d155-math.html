Remember

A = (1/2)r²θ = (1/2)r s, with θ in radians. A circular segment is sector minus triangle. θ = 2π recovers the disk. Figures are invented.

1. State both forms of the sector area in radians, and say what θ = 2π recovers.

2. On an invented figure, r = 2 and θ = π/2, what is the segment area?

3. On an invented figure, r = 10 and s = 4, what is the sector area?

4. On an invented figure, r = 6 and θ = π/3, give the sector area and the segment area.

5. On an invented figure, r = 4 and θ = π/2, find the sector area, and check it with the degree formula for 90°.

6. Why is (π/3)/360 × πr² a miss if θ is already π/3 in radians?
