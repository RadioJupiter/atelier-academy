Money, in this working sense, is a medium of exchange, a unit of account, and a store of value. The jobs can come apart. A numbered paper is not automatically money. No invented first-coin year, and no invented inflation rate.

Key terms: money (three jobs), medium of exchange.

1. Why is “money is paper with a number on it” incomplete?

2. In the invented Ana-and-Beau table, what would a clearly labeled classroom token be doing if both students accepted it for pages and staves?

3. For each of today's key terms (money (three jobs), medium of exchange), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Name the three jobs, in your own words, with a fresh example of something that does only one of them well.
