Remember

Hablaba (-ar, -aba), comía and vivía (-er and -ir, -ía). Imperfect: ongoing or habitual past action, in this first look. Preterite: a completed event, already taught. Ir, ser, and ver remain reserved.

Key terms: three-conjugation imperfect, contrasted with the preterite.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of hablar, of comer, and of vivir.

2. How do the -ar endings differ from the -er and -ir endings in the imperfect?

3. Write one habitual imperfect sentence that uses caminaba and comía, and one completed preterite sentence that uses caminé and comí.

4. Why are iba, era, and veía reserved?
