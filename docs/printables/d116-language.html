Remember

The preterite is the completed past: ayer hablé, ayer comí, ayer fui, ayer vi. Ir and ser share fui. Instruction is in English. No new forms.

1. Give an already-taught preterite of hablar, comer, ir, ser, and ver, with a short English meaning for each.

2. Why do ir and ser share fui?

3. Translate: Ayer pidió. Ayer durmió. (Already-taught third persons.)

4. Write two original preterite sentences with already-taught verbs. Translate them. Do not invent identifying characters.

5. Which form is not a preterite: hablé, fui, iba, vi? Why?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
