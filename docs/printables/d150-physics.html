Remember

An open tube has antinodes at both ends, and f_n = n v_w / (2L), with n = 1, 2, 3, …. Its fundamental is twice that of a closed tube of the same length. Official Example 17.5 remains the closed 0.672 m tube at 344 m/s.

1. State the closed-tube and open-tube formulas, including the values of n in each.

2. What official values does Example 17.5 give for v_w, L, and the fourth overtone?

3. On an invented length L = 1.00 m, labeled invented, with this example’s official 344 m/s, what would the closed and open fundamentals be, and why are those frequencies not official?

4. For each of today's key terms (resonance in an open air column), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
