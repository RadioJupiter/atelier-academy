Remember

A narrative is a short complete action, in order, with who, what, when, and where, and no stated moral. A fable is the same kind of short action with a moral at the end.

1. What is a narrative, as taught today, and how does it differ from a fable?

2. In the invented ink-jar model, who does what, when, and where, and in what order?

3. What would turn that model back into a fable, and what remains reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (narrative (progymnasmata)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
