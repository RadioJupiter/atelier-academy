Gray: the mandible is the largest and strongest bone of the face, receives the lower teeth, and consists of a body and two rami. It articulates with the two temporal bones, and is last week’s movable exception. Recognize the mental foramen, the coronoid and condyloid processes, and the mandibular notch. A full recitation of fossae, ossification, and age-changes is reserved. Cite Theodora’s Gray 1918 page.
