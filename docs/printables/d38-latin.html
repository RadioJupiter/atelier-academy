Remember

Passive infinitives in this first look: vidērī, vocārī, habērī (‘to be seen / called / held’). After posse, the infinitive may be active or passive; they are not the same.

Key terms: present infinitive (passive).

1. Write the active and passive infinitives of habēre.

2. Write: “You can be seen” and “A person can be called.”

3. For each of today's key terms (present infinitive (passive)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Contrast, in one line, Rēx vidēre potest and Rēx vidērī potest.
