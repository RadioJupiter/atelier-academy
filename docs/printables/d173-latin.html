Remember

Purpose: ut or nē. Result: ut or ut nōn, often with tam, ita, or sic. The verb is the present subjunctive, not the future.

1. Translate and label four sentences, one for each already-taught job, using sequor or ēgredior.

2. Why is nē the wrong negative in a result clause?

3. Give the present subjunctive and the future, third person singular, of sequor and of ēgredior.

4. Write four original sentences, one for each job, and translate them.

5. In one sentence, replace the present subjunctive with the future and say why that form is a miss.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
