Remember

Year 21 of Ramesses II: a treaty with Ḫattušili III, after Kadesh, not a battle report. Sources: the Akkadian tablet from Ḫattuša and the hieroglyphic text at Karnak. Convert years to BCE only with a chronology warning. Compare the two official voices; do not invent clauses.

Key terms: treaty of year 21, diplomatic copy.

1. In your own words, who the two parties were, and why the text is not a report of the Battle of Kadesh itself.

2. Name the two main ancient versions and the language or script of each.

3. For each of today's key terms (treaty of year 21, diplomatic copy), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. From a published translation you attribute, list two provisions both versions share, and one way a royal version might still be self-presentation.
