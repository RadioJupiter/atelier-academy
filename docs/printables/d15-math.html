Remember

Rectangle: parallelogram with a right angle; diagonals equal. Rhombus: parallelogram with four equal sides; diagonals perpendicular and they bisect the vertex angles. A square is both. Trapezoid (this course): exactly one pair of parallel sides.

Key terms: rectangle, rhombus.

1. A rectangle is 5 by 12. Find a diagonal.

2. A rhombus has all sides 13, and one diagonal 10. Find the other diagonal.

3. For each of today's key terms (rectangle, rhombus), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. The diagonals of a rhombus are 12 and 16. Find a side, and find the area (half the product of the diagonals).
