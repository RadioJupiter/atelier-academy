Remember

With cōnstāre, emere, vēndere, the price is ablative (decem sēstertiīs). With pretium est, the price is nominative. tantus = so great. decem and vīgintī are indeclinable here.

Key terms: ablative of price, tantus, -a, -um.

1. Write: ‘Julia buys that ring for ten sesterces.’

2. Correct the case of the coin if needed: *Hic ānulus decem sēstertiī cōnstat.

3. For each of today's key terms (ablative of price, tantus, -a, -um), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the ablative plural of sēstertius as used in a price, and the nominative plural as used after pretium est.
