Remember

Comeré and viviré use the same endings as hablaré. The whole infinitive is still kept. Irregular futures remain reserved.

1. Give the six future forms of comer, with meanings for comeré and comeremos.

2. Give viviré, vivirá, and vivirán, and write one original sentence with viviremos.

3. Why are the endings the same as for hablar, and what remains reserved?

4. Translate: Mañana comeré. Ellos comerán. Nosotros viviremos.

5. Write one original sentence with comeremos and one with vivirá.

6. Change como to the simple future in the first person.
