Remember

An identity is true for all allowed values. Expand carefully. (a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²; (a−b)² = a² − 2ab + b²; (a+b)(a−b) = a² − b².

1. Is (a+b)² = a² + b² an identity? Why or why not?

2. What is (a+b)(a−b)?

3. Compute mentally, showing the identity used: 47 × 53; 99²; 1001 × 999; 65².

4. Factor completely: x² − 9y²; x⁴ − 1; 8a³ − 27; x² + 6x + 9; a³ + 1.

5. Simplify (x + 1)² − (x − 1)².

6. If a + b = 7 and ab = 10, find a² + b² and a³ + b³ without finding a and b.
