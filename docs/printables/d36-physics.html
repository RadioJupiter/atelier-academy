Remember

F = G Mm / r², with G = 6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg² as a standard constant, not a classroom measurement. Inverse square: double the distance, one fourth the force. g and G are not the same. No invented orbital speed.

Key terms: Newton’s law of gravitation (first look), inverse-square (gravitation).

1. If r is halved, masses unchanged, what happens to F?

2. If one mass doubles, r unchanged, what happens to F?

3. For each of today's key terms (Newton’s law of gravitation (first look), inverse-square (gravitation)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Distinguish G from g in one or two sentences.
