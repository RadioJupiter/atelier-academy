Pliny the Younger to Tacitus, in Firth (1900; Attalus text). The uncle is at Misenum, in command of the fleet. “24th of August, about the seventh hour” is Firth’s wording. 79 CE is the conventional event-year, in Titus’s reign. No invented death toll; later scenes reserved.
