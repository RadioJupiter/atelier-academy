Remember

Secūtus erō, I shall have followed; ēgressus erō, I shall have gone out. These are not the futures, not the perfects, and not the pluperfects.

1. Give the first-person singular present, future, perfect, pluperfect, and future perfect of sequor, with glosses.

2. Translate: Puer ēgressus erit, et tum eum secūtī erimus.

3. Why is “ēgressus est” a miss in that first clause if the going out is still only later?

4. Write two short original Friday sentences, one future perfect of sequor and one future of ēgredior, using Puer or Puella as needed.

5. What is the near-miss if someone writes secūtus eram for “I shall have followed”?

6. What remains reserved?
