Remember

The humerus is the single bone of the upper arm. The radius (thumb side, palm forward) and the ulna (little-finger side; elbow point) are the two bones of the forearm. This is a first look, after last week’s femur, tibia, and fibula. No identifying person, and no invented measurements.

Key terms: humerus, radius and ulna.

1. Match each arm bone to the nearest “same place” in the leg: femur, tibia, or fibula — as a rough place-comparison, not as a claim that the joints are the same.

2. Why is a drawing of a named person a miss on this page?

3. For each of today's key terms (humerus, radius and ulna), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Why does this first look stay with the palm-forward position?
