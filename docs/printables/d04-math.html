Remember

Congruent means same shape and same size, with a stated correspondence. SSS, SAS, ASA (and RHS for right triangles) are the usual tests. AAA is not enough (that gives only similarity).

1. An isosceles triangle has a base angle of 72°. What is the vertex angle?

2. Does AAA prove two triangles congruent? Why or why not?

3. Segments AB and CD bisect each other at O. Prove that AC = BD.

4. On one side of an angle with vertex O, mark points A and C; on the other, B and D, so that OA = OB and OC = OD. Prove AD = BC.

5. In isosceles triangle ABC (AB = AC), M is the midpoint of BC. Prove that AM bisects angle A and is perpendicular to BC.

6. Prove the converse of the base-angle theorem: if ∠B = ∠C in triangle ABC, then AB = AC.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
