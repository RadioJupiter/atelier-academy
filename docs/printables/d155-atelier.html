Remember

A cylindrical form in this week’s work is an upright bottle or can, or another cylinder, in four paints and one light, turned by value, with a small light value from the same paints, sitting on a simple table plane. No portrait, and no fifth tube.

1. What is a cylindrical form in this week’s work, and how does it turn?

2. What is a small light value on that form, and from which paints is it mixed?

3. Why must the cylinder sit on a plane, and why is a fifth tube a miss?

4. Describe the invented study from first lay-in to close, still in four paints and one light.

5. Name two reserved things that were not begun this week.

6. How does this week’s cylinder continue last week’s limited palette without replacing it?
