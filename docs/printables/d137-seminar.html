Remember

Theme and variations states a short theme and then repeats it with changes. The set figure is invented, in scale degrees, not a copyrighted score. It is not rondo form.

1. What is theme and variations, and how does it differ from last week’s rondo?

2. State the invented theme in scale degrees, and give the two set variations.

3. Why must the figure not be replaced by a copyrighted tune?

4. For each of today's key terms (theme and variations), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
