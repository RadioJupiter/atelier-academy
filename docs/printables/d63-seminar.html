Remember

Platner records the Arch of Constantine as erected by the senate to commemorate the victory over Maxentius in 312 CE, completed in 315–316 CE, unmentioned in the literary sources he uses, and decorated with sculpture of several periods — some earlier, some Constantinian. This is not the Arch of Titus. Later fabrics, not photographs, remain the method.

Key terms: Arch of Constantine.

1. According to Platner, what event of 312 CE does the arch commemorate, and what years does he give for its completion?

2. What two cautions does the set extract give — one about literary sources, and one about the sculpture?

3. Name, from the entry’s later grouping, one earlier source of sculpture and one Constantinian part.

4. How is this monument different from the already-taught Arch of Titus, and what method do the two lessons share?
