Remember

The simple future keeps the whole infinitive and adds -é, -ás, -á, -emos, -éis, -án. Hablaré, I will speak, is not hablo and not voy a hablar.

1. How is the simple future of a regular verb formed, and what are the six forms of hablar?

2. Write one original sentence with hablaré, and one with hablaremos.

3. How does hablaré differ from hablo, and from a near future with ir a? What remains reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (simple future of a regular verb), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
