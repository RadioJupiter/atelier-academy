Remember

A = bh for a parallelogram; ½bh for the triangle on the same base and height; ½(a + b)h for a trapezoid; ½ × perimeter × apothem for a regular polygon. Height is perpendicular. A slanted side is not the height.

1. A regular polygon has perimeter 48 and apothem 7.2 (invented). Find the area.

2. A regular hexagon has side 6. What is its area, in the √3 form taught yesterday?

3. A parallelogram has base 12 and height 7. Find its area, and the area of one diagonal triangle.

4. Why is a trapezoid not treated as a parallelogram in this course’s usage?
