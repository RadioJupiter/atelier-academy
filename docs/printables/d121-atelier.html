Four paints only: yellow ochre, an earth red, black, and white. Today mix ochre with white and ochre with black, and compare their value. No portrait, and no fifth tube.

1. Name the four paints of this week’s limited palette, and describe today’s two ochre mixtures.

2. Why is the palette limited, and why is a portrait not today’s job?

3. What must you not treat a store tube as, and what colors remain reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (limited palette), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
