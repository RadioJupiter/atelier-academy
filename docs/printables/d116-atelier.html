The eye, the ear, and the neck are volumes on the monochrome head, not outlines. Likeness checks compare both sides. Written pauses record what sits and what remains reserved, including full color.

1. What is the neck, in the already-taught first look, and how does it meet the jaw?

2. What already-taught method do you use to compare the two ears, or the two sides of the neck?

3. Why is a first pass in full color still a miss?

4. Name two misses, one for the eye and one for the neck.

5. What does a written pause after these volumes record?
