Latin · Capitulum VIII, part 1: ille, and hic against is

Week 6 · Day 26 · Monday · one-lesson review

Remember

hic = this (near); ille = that (far or contrasted); is = the one just mentioned. illud / hoc end in -d, not -um. illīc is the adverb ‘there.’

Key terms: ille, illa, illud, is, ea, id (reminder).

1. Write a contrasting pair with hic and ille (two boys, or two books).

2. In Mārcus librum habet; eum Iūliae ostendit, why is eum a better choice than hunc or illum if no pointing is intended?

3. For each of today's key terms (ille, illa, illud, is, ea, id (reminder)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the masculine, feminine, and neuter nominative singular of ‘that.’
