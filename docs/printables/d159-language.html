Remember

He dicho, ha hecho, and hemos escrito are present-perfect forms. They are not the already-taught present, not a preterite used in place of the participle, not hay, and not haya.

4. Write three short original sentences: one present, one present perfect with a regular participle, and one present perfect with dicho, hecho, or escrito, each with a gloss.
