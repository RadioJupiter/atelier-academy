Remember

Same base, multiply → add exponents. Divide → subtract. Power of a power → multiply exponents. a⁰ = 1 and a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ for a ≠ 0. Do not add exponents when you add the powers themselves.

Key terms: exponent, power.

1. Simplify and evaluate: 2⁵ · 2³ / 2⁴.

2. Simplify and evaluate: 5⁰ and 2⁻³.

3. For each of today's key terms (exponent, power), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Simplify (2⁵ · 2⁻²) / 2.
