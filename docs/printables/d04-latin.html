Latin · Capitulum II, part 1: gender and the genitive

Week 1 · Day 4 · Thursday · one-lesson review

Review sheet — use this after the lesson. These questions practice only what this lesson taught. Answers are on the day's answer-key page, not here.

Remember

Ask quis? for 'who?' and quid? for 'what?'. The noun's gender decides the adjective's ending, not the English meaning.

1. What does quid mean?

2. Is oppidum masculine, feminine or neuter?

3. Give the genitive singular and plural: fluvius, insula, oppidum, prōvincia, servus.

4. Complete: (a) Sicilia est insula Itali__. (b) Nīlus est fluvius Aegypt__. (c) Numerus insul__ magnus est (plural). (d) Numerus oppid__ parvus est (plural).

5. Answer with the genitive: (a) Cuius prōvincia est Sicilia? (Sicily is a province of the Roman Empire: imperium Rōmānum.) (b) Cuius fluvius est Nīlus?
