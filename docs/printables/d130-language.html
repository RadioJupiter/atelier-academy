Remember

Same future endings. Regular verbs keep the infinitive; the irregular set uses changed stems such as har-, tendr-, and querr-.

1. How do this week’s irregular futures still use last week’s endings, and give haré, tendré, and podré with meanings?

2. Write original sentences with hablaré, diré, and vendremos.

3. Why is *teneré a miss, and what remains reserved?

4. Translate, invented only: Mañana lo haré. Ella no podrá ir. Nosotros saldremos. Ellos querrán verlo.

5. Give irás, tendrá, and sabremos, with meanings.

6. Change voy, digo, and quiero into simple-future first-person sentences about tomorrow.
