Remember

A simple discount takes a percent off an invented original price. 25 percent off $80 is a $20 discount, and $60 is paid. The percent is a part of the original price, not of the amount paid. The figures are invented, not a real store sale. Last week’s repayment table is a different job. Tax and compound interest remain reserved.

Key terms: simple discount, percent off.

1. An invented price is $80, and 25 percent is taken off. What is the discount, and what amount is paid?

2. Of what amount is the 25 percent a part, and why must you not take 25 percent of the amount paid instead?

3. An invented price is $50, and 10 percent is taken off. What is the discount, and what amount is paid?

4. Why are the figures invented, and why is this not a first look at last week’s repayment table?
