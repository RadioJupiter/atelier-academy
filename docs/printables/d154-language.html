Remember

He hablado is I have spoken, and sits beside hablo, hablaré, and hablaría. Hay and haya are not helping forms here. Irregular participles remain reserved.

1. Give he comido, comeré, and hay, with short glosses, and say why they must not be swapped.

2. Choose: Ellos ___ (han / hay / hayan) vivido, if the job is “they have lived.”

3. Why is hecho still a miss?

4. Write short original sentences for has hablado, hablaremos, hay, and Espero que haya tiempo, with glosses.

5. Contrast ha, hay, and haya once more in three short glosses.

6. What remains reserved?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
