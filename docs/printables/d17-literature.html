Remember

Books 17–22: the body, the shield (ekphrasis of a wider world), the formal reconciliation, Achilles’ return, and the death of Hector. The Iliad does not end with that death. Book 6’s Hector is still in memory when Book 22 happens.

Key terms: ekphrasis, the death of Hector.

1. What does the formal reconciliation in Book 19 do, and what does it not do, for Achilles’ anger?

2. In Book 22, who sees the death, and who does not yet know? Why does that matter for Andromache?

3. For each of today's key terms (ekphrasis, the death of Hector), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Write a page on the shield as ekphrasis: four scenes, the craftsman, and one claim about why the poem stops the war to describe them.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
