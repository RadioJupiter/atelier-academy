Remember

Visto is the already-taught irregular participle of ver. Puesto is the already-taught irregular participle of poner, not *ponido. Both use he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, and han.

1. Give he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, and han, first with visto, then with puesto.

2. Why are vido and ponido misses?

3. What already-taught participle waits until tomorrow?

4. Write two original present-perfect sentences, one with visto and one with puesto, and translate them.

5. Name already-taught dicho, hecho, or escrito, and say why they remain distinct from visto and puesto.
