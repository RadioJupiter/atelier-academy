Remember

A vector has magnitude and direction. Perpendicular components add separately; the magnitude is √(v_x² + v_y²). v_x = v cos θ, v_y = v sin θ, with θ measured from the x-axis.

Key terms: vector, component.

1. A displacement is 12 m west and 5 m south. Find its magnitude.

2. A force of 20 N acts at 45°. Find the components. (cos 45° = sin 45° = 1/√2.)

3. For each of today's key terms (vector, component), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Add two displacements: 2 m east, then 3 m east, then 4 m north. Find the magnitude of the total displacement.
