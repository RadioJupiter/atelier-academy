Remember

If a² + b² = c², the triangle is right-angled opposite c. That is the converse, not the theorem itself. 3-4-5, 5-12-13, and 6-8-10 are integer examples; they are not the only right triangles, and 3-5-6 is not one of them.

Key terms: converse of the Pythagorean theorem.

1. A triangle has sides 9, 12, and 15. Is it right-angled, and how is it related to 3-4-5?

2. Why is “every right triangle is a 3-4-5 triangle” a miss?

3. For each of today's key terms (converse of the Pythagorean theorem), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. State the Pythagorean theorem in one sentence, and its converse in another, without mixing the two.
