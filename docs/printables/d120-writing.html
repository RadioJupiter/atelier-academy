Five Check items. You need four out of five to move on. Not a score or a prize. Franklin on Inferno VI.1–57; a précis of Dewing I.2.17–19. No new passage.

1. What are the two steps of Franklin’s method, as already taught, and what is the check against?

2. List, as hints, the rain, Cerberus, and Ciacco’s wording from already-set Inferno VI.1–57. What official claim must you not add?

3. Write a four- or five-sentence reconstruction of Inferno VI.1–57 from those hints, and name one set-text word you would check.

4. Write a précis of Dewing I.2.17–19 in no more than eight sentences, keeping the two house names and the two quoted phrases “shewing honour to God” and “labouring.”

5. How does that précis end, what official figure must you not invent, and why are Canto VII and Buildings I.3 not in this examination?
