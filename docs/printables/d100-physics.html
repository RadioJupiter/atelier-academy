Remember

A projection of uniform circular motion of radius X is SHM. T = 2πX / vmax, and it recovers T = 2π√(m/k). v = vmax √(1 − x²/X²), the same speed as from energy. Classroom numbers are invented unless a sourced OpenStax figure is named.

1. What motion does the projection of uniform circular motion undergo, in OpenStax 16.6?

2. Write T in terms of X and vmax, and give T for the invented case X = 0.10 m, vmax = 2.0 m/s.

3. Write the speed formula, and give v at x = 0.06 m in that invented case.

4. Why are the cosine-in-radians form and damped motion reserved?

5. OpenStax Example 16.6 has X = 0.100 m and vmax = 0.852 m/s. Find T from Monday’s formula.

6. An invented case has X = 0.20 m and vmax = 1.0 m/s. Find v at x = 0.12 m.
