Remember

p = m v, a vector. If the net external force is negligible, the system’s total momentum is conserved, even when kinetic energy is not (as when bodies stick). Choose a positive direction and keep signs.

Key terms: momentum, conservation of momentum.

1. A 4 kg body at 5 m/s collides with a 2 kg body at rest, and they stick. Find the common velocity.

2. A 1 kg cart at 6 m/s is brought to rest by a constant force in 0.3 s. Find the change in momentum and the size of the force. (Use F = Δp/Δt.)

3. For each of today's key terms (momentum, conservation of momentum), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Two 3 kg carts move toward each other at 2 m/s each, and stick. Find the common velocity.
