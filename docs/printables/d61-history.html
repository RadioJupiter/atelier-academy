Remember

In 410 CE, as footnoted in the Dods edition, Rome was sacked by the Goths under Alaric. The set extract is Augustine’s later account of why he wrote The City of God, not a battlefield report. Late antiquity is a period name, not a single day. No death toll is invented.

Key terms: late antiquity, sack of Rome (410).

1. According to the set extract, who sacked Rome, and under which king?

2. What explanation does Augustine say some pagans gave for the calamity, and what did that prompt him to write?

3. How do we have the year 410 for this sack in this lesson, and is that year a number inside Augustine’s own sentence?

4. Why is the extract not a battlefield report?
