Remember

Puesto, visto, dicho, hecho, and escrito are five already-taught irregular participles, each with its own verb. The helping forms of haber do not change.

1. Translate and label: Ella ha puesto el libro. Ella ha visto el libro. Ella ha escrito el libro.

2. Which verb does puesto belong to, and why is *ponido still a miss?

3. Why is a present-perfect subjunctive still reserved?

4. Write three original sentences, one each with ha puesto, ha dicho, and ha hecho, and translate them.

5. Change hemos visto to hemos puesto, and translate both.

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
