Remember

Iba (ir), era (ser), veía (ver). Ir and ser share fui, not the imperfect. In this first look, the imperfect is ongoing, habitual, or, with era, a past state; the preterite is a completed event. Instruction remains in English.

1. Give the six imperfect forms of ir, of ser, and of ver.

2. How do yo iba, yo era, and yo veía differ, and what preterite do ir and ser share?

3. Write one habitual imperfect sentence that uses iba and veía, and one completed preterite sentence that uses fui and vi.

4. Why is vía a miss, and why must classroom sentences not identify a living person?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
