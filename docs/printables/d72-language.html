Remember

Hacer’s preterite is hice, hiciste, hizo, hicimos, hicisteis, hicieron. Hizo (with z) is the él/ella/usted form; hice is the yo form. This set is not fui and not comí.

Key terms: the preterite of hacer.

1. Why is *yo hací not the preterite taught today?

2. Write one short sentence with hice and one with hizo, using a time word already taught.

3. For each of today's key terms (the preterite of hacer), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Give the tú, nosotros, and ellos forms of this preterite.
