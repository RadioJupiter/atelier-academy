Remember

In any triangle, c² = a² + b² − 2ab cos C. When C is 90°, this is the Pythagorean theorem. The work stays in degrees.

1. State the law of cosines, and say what it becomes when C is 90°.

2. On an invented triangle with sides 3 and 4 and included angle 90°, what is the third side?

3. On an invented triangle with sides 5 and 5 and included angle 60°, what is the third side, and why are the figures invented?

4. For each of today's key terms (the law of cosines), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
