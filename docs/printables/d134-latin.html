Remember

Present or pluperfect, chosen from the time in the sentence. The participle agrees: secūtus, secūta; ēgressus, ēgressa.

1. Translate: Ego sequor. Puella ēgressa erat. Vōs eum secūtī erātis.

2. Choose and explain: Nōs ___ nunc (ēgredimur / ēgressī erāmus). Tū ___ anteā (secūtus erās / sequeris).

3. Write one original sentence with secūta erat and one with ēgressī erant.

4. Give the pluperfect, third person singular, of sequor and of ēgredior, masculine and feminine.

5. Change ēgrederis and sequimur into pluperfect sentences, still in the same persons.

6. Why is secūtus erat a miss with Puella as the subject?

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
