Remember

Volume is the space a solid occupies, in cubic units. For a right rectangular prism, V = ℓ × w × h, the number of unit cubes that fill the box. This is not face area, and not density.

Key terms: volume of a solid, volume of a right rectangular prism.

1. A classroom prism has ℓ = 8, w = 2, and h = 5. What is its volume?

2. A classroom prism has volume 60 cubic units, length 5, and width 4. What is its height?

3. Why is “6 × 3 = 18, so the volume is 18” a miss for the prism in today’s diagram?

4. For each of today's key terms (volume of a solid, volume of a right rectangular prism), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
