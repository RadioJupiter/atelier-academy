Remember

Grisaille is monochrome value painting, in gray or in one hue, following the value map. Paint may be begun if it is ready; otherwise a rehearsal in drawing is enough and is named as a rehearsal. Full color is reserved. One light, held.

Key terms: grisaille.

1. Why is a gray head not a full-color portrait, even if the values are complete?

2. Why must the grisaille follow yesterday’s map rather than invent a new lamp?

3. For each of today's key terms (grisaille), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. What limited range is enough, if you do begin in paint?
