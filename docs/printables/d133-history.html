Remember

East of the 370-league line, lands found or to be found by Portugal pertain forever to the King of Portugal and his successors. West of the line, lands found or to be found by Castile pertain forever to the King and Queen of Castile, Leon, etc., and their successors.

1. What lands pertain to Portugal, and what lands pertain to Castile, according to the treaty?

2. What happens if Castile’s ships discover land east of the line?

3. How does this still differ from last week’s May 4 bull, and what official conversion must you not invent?

4. Quote, or closely paraphrase, the treaty’s “forever” wording for Portugal, and for Castile.

5. Why is “Leon,” in the treaty’s English, left as Davenport writes it?

6. What remains reserved?
