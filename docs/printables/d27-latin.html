Remember

A relative agrees in gender and number with its antecedent, but takes the case of its own clause. quī / quae / quod; quem / quam. Not the question-word quis?

Key terms: relative pronoun (quī, quae, quod).

1. Write: ‘The boy who is in the garden is Marcus.’

2. Correct: *Liber quī Mārcus habet magnus est. (Marcus has the book.)

3. For each of today's key terms (relative pronoun (quī, quae, quod)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

4. Choose quī or quem: Puer ___ in vīllā labōrat Quīntus est.
