Remember

P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B), when P(B) is not 0. On an invented fair cube, P(even | greater than 3) = 2/3.

1. What is P(A|B), and when is the formula used?

2. On an invented fair cube, find P(even | greater than 3), naming the faces in each part.

3. Why is last week’s P(A or B) not a substitute for P(A|B), even if the two numbers happen to agree on this cube?

4. For each of today's key terms (conditional probability), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
