Remember

ayer (yesterday), anoche (last night), and el lunes pasado (last Monday) place a completed action in the past and are used with the preterite. They do not replace the verb ending. With nosotros, they make hablamos / cantamos / compramos clearly past.

Key terms: ayer, anoche, el lunes pasado.

1. Give the English for ayer, anoche, and el lunes pasado.

2. Write “Yesterday you (tú) sang” and “Last night we spoke.”

3. Why is El lunes pasado nosotros compramos pan clearly preterite, even though compramos has the same spelling in the present?

4. Correct: Hoy yo hablé anoche and Ayer ellos compró pan.
