A definition you can only recognize on the card is not yet yours. Give the meaning, a fresh example, and a near-miss.

1. In a classroom 3-4-5 right triangle, A is opposite 3. Give sin A, cos A, and sin(90° − A). Why is a 6-8-10 triangle with the same angle A not a new sine?

2. Give the six perfect forms of loquor, and say why puerō locūtō is not the same construction.

3. Give the six imperfect forms of ir, of ser, and of ver. How do yo iba, yo era, and yo veía differ, and what preterite do ir and ser share?

4. What is resonance, in OpenStax 16.8, and how does less damping change the peak? Why must you not invent an official wind speed?

5. Name the three methods of heat transfer from OpenStax 14.4, and say which method is responsible for most of the heat into the room in OpenStax’s fireplace.

6. In Longfellow, Inferno V, what is the set wording for the fault of those in the hurricane, and how does the canto end?
