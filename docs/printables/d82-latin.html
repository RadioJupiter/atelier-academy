Remember

Sum, es, est, sumus, estis, sunt. Posse is pot- plus esse, with t becoming s before an s: possum, potes, potest, possumus, potestis, possunt. An infinitive follows posse.

Key terms: possum, the full present.

1. Give the six present forms of esse.

2. How is possumus formed from sumus, and how is potestis formed from estis?

3. Translate: Audīre nōn potes.

4. For each of today's key terms (possum, the full present), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
