Remember

The gate’s inscription runs from “Through me the way is to the city dolent” to “All hope abandon, ye who enter in!” The words are “in sombre color” on the summit of a gate. The guide leads the speaker in among the secret things. Typography as in Longfellow. Lines 22–69 wait until Thursday.

Key terms: the gate of Inferno.

1. Write out, or closely and exactly recall, the last line of the inscription, and the phrase that names the color of the words.

2. What three “through me” ways open the inscription?

3. What does the guide say they have come to behold, and how does he lead the speaker in?

4. For each of today's key terms (the gate of Inferno), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
