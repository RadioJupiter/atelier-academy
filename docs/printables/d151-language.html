Remember

The present perfect is he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han, plus a regular participle such as hablado, comido, or vivido. He hablado is I have spoken. It is not hay, and not haya. Irregular participles remain reserved.

1. What are the six present helping forms of haber, and how is a regular present perfect built?

2. Translate the original sentence “Nosotros hemos hablado,” and say why “hay hablado” would be a miss.

3. Why is dicho a miss for today’s first look?

4. For each of today's key terms (present perfect with haber, a first look), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).

Work in writing. Then mark from the answer key and rework every miss the next day.
