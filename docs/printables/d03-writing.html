Remember

An appositive sits next to the noun it renames, usually between commas. A subordinate clause needs a main clause. Do not leave a subordinate clause standing alone.

1. What is an appositive?

2. Why is Because the light was good. not a complete sentence?

3. Add an accurate appositive to each: (a) Kiselev wrote geometry textbooks used in Russian schools for decades. (b) Erasmus wrote De Copia. (c) The Iliad begins with the anger of Achilles.

4. Begin each with a subordinating conjunction of your choice and complete it accurately: (a) ___ the candle goes out, … (b) ___ vertical angles are equal, … (c) ___ the plate is placed beside the drawing at the same size, …

5. Combine into one sentence each: (a) The stele was set up in Babylonia. It was taken to Susa as plunder. It was excavated there in 1901–02. (b) The runner finished a lap. The runner's displacement was zero. The runner's distance was 400 m.
