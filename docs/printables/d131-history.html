Remember

The Treaty of Tordesillas, June 7, 1494, draws a pole-to-pole line “at a distance of three hundred and seventy leagues west of the Cape Verde Islands.” That is not last week’s hundred leagues, and it is not an official modern longitude.

1. What is the Treaty of Tordesillas, in the Davenport source, and what line does it draw?

2. How does that line differ from last week’s Inter Caetera of May 4, 1493?

3. What official conversion must you not invent, and what remains reserved?

4. For each of today's key terms (Treaty of Tordesillas (June 7, 1494)), write one sentence in your own words and one near-miss you must not confuse it with. Then apply one of the terms to a fresh case of your own (not the example on the card).
