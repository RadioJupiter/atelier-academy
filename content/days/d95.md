<div><dt>Definition</dt><dd>Gray, 1918, writes: “The skull is supported on the summit of the vertebral column, and is of an oval shape, wider behind than in front. It is composed of a series of flattened or irregular bones which, with one exception (the mandible), are immovably jointed together. It is divisible into two parts: (1) the cranium, which lodges and protects the brain, consists of eight bones, and (2) the skeleton of the face, of fourteen.” The eight cranial bones he then names are the occipital, the two parietals, the frontal, the two temporals, the sphenoidal, and the ethmoidal.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Example</dt><dd>The twenty-two bones are eight plus fourteen. The mandible is the one exception that is not immovably jointed. The hyoid, which Gray mentions in this section, is not one of the twenty-two.</dd></div>

Not a required recitation of every facial name on this first day, beyond the count of fourteen and the mandible as the movable exception; the fourteen as Gray lists them may be read from the table.</dd></div>

<p>Gray, 1918, places the skull on the summit of the vertebral column, oval, wider behind than in front, of bones “which, with one exception (the mandible), are immovably jointed together,” and divisible into a cranium of eight bones and a face of fourteen.</p>
<p>The eight cranial bones, in Gray’s list: occipital; two parietals; frontal; two temporals; sphenoidal; ethmoidal.</p>
<p>The fourteen facial bones, in Gray’s table, may be read: two nasals, two maxillae, two lacrimals, two zygomatics, two palatines, two inferior nasal conchae, vomer, and mandible. Today’s required work is the division 8 + 14 = 22, the eight cranial names, and the mandible as the movable exception. The full fourteen need not be recited from memory on this first day, but they should be recognized from Gray’s list. The hyoid is not a twenty-third skull bone in that count. A Basle regrouping that Gray mentions is a gloss only.</p>

### Answer key

**Check.**
1. Pyramid: V = (1/3) × 36 × 9 = 108 cubic units. Sphere: V = 36π cubic units.
2. A loosely attached circumstance phrase: a noun plus a present participle, both ablative. A perfect-participle absolute such as urbe captā has not been taught and is reserved.
