<p>OpenStax 16.10 closes this week with beats. Two waves of similar but not identical frequencies superpose and fluctuate in amplitude, alternately constructive and destructive. The beat frequency is f_B = |f1 − f2|. The resulting wave has the average frequency f_ave of the two. The book’s official example: a tuning fork of 256 Hz, with two beats per second, means the other frequency is either 254 or 258 Hz. An invented pair used for practice: 440 Hz and 444 Hz, so f_B = 4 Hz and f_ave = 442 Hz. Invent no official living pitch standard. Standing waves, already taught, return in the mix.</p>

<li>The pluperfect is the perfect participle plus the imperfect of sum: locūtus eram, I had spoken.</li>
<li>Locūtus sum is the perfect, I spoke or I have spoken, and does not mark the action as already completed before another past moment.</li>

**Check.**
