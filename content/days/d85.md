**Check.**

2. T = 2π√(0.32/50) = 2π√(0.0064) = 2π × 0.08 = 0.16π s.
3. T is multiplied by 1/2, because T is proportional to 1/√k, and √4 = 2.
4. The spring period depends on mass and on k, not on a length L. The small-swing pendulum period depends on L and g, not on mass.
5. T (and f) do not depend on amplitude.

<p>Gray’s further placements, used here: the cuboid is “on the lateral side of the foot, in front of the calcaneus, and behind the fourth and fifth metatarsal bones.” The navicular is “at the medial side of the tarsus, between the talus behind and the cuneiform bones in front.” The first cuneiform is the largest of the three, “at the medial side of the foot, between the navicular behind and the base of the first metatarsal in front.” Gray also calls it the internal cuneiform.
