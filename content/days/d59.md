Two invented-shop jobs have already been taught. Today you gather them. A **budget** (week 7) remains a third, already-taught job; it is not today’s work.

**Unit price:** total price ÷ number of same-kind units. **Profit:** a leftover after costs. They are different jobs. All shop numbers in this lesson are **labeled invented** classroom numbers, not a real ticket and not a historical price.

**A worked invented pair.** A pack of 4 identical notebooks is marked $6 (invented). The unit price is $6 ÷ 4 = **$1.50** per notebook. If the shop’s invented cost for that pack is $4, the leftover profit is $6 − $4 = **$2**. The unit price can be known even when a profit is not being asked for.

We will not invent a NASA or a Roman price.
