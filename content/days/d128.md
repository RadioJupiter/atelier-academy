<p>A 30°-60°-90° triangle comes from half an equilateral triangle of side 2, already used in this form: the side opposite 30° is 1, the hypotenuse is 2, and the side opposite 60° is √3. <dl class="frayer">
<div><dt>Definition</dt><dd>In OpenStax Example 16.10, intensity is proportional to amplitude squared. If two identical waves interfere perfectly constructively, the amplitude doubles, so the intensity becomes four times as great, not two times. For two waves each of intensity 1.00 W/m², the resulting intensity at that location is 4.00 W/m². Energy is not created: the area of 4.00 W/m² is smaller than the area the two waves covered before, and there are other places where the intensity is zero.</dd></div>
<div><dt>Example</dt><dd>The book’s official pair: 1.00 W/m² + 1.00 W/m², perfectly constructive, gives 4.00 W/m² at that location, not 2.00 W/m².</dd></div>

<div class="wide"><dt>Connects to</dt><dd>Monday’s I = P/A, last week’s pure constructive interference (amplitude 2X), and the already-taught idea that most superpositions are mixed.</dd></div>
</dl></article>
