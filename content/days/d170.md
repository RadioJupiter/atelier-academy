### Answer key

**Check.**
1. f is continuous at a when f is defined at a and the limit equals f(a). Invented f(x) = 2x has f(3) = 6 = the limit, so it is continuous at 3. Invented (x² − 4)/(x − 2) is not defined at 2, so it is not continuous at 2, even though the limit is 4.
2. A result clause marks what happens as a result, often with tam, ita, or sic. She comes so quickly that she follows him. A purpose clause, Puella venit ut eum sequātur, marks an intended purpose. A negative result uses ut nōn, not nē.
3. I have put, or I have placed; he has put, or she has put. Poner does not form *ponido. Visto is the participle of ver, not of poner.
4. Official θc = 42.2°, from official n₁ = 1.49 and n₂ = 1.00, because 1.00/1.49 = 0.671. A ray inside the plastic is totally reflected when it strikes the surface at an angle greater than 42.2°.
5. Official V₀ = 1.414 × 120 V = 170 V, and official P₀ = 2 × 60.0 W = 120 W. The official relation is V₀ = √2 V_rms, not the HTML’s dropped radical V_rms = V₀/2.
6. Twenty-five leagues on the side where they arrived. After proceeding two leagues, a port late in the evening. That night he resolved that some of the men should land at break of day in order to confer. The letter’s leagues are not converted.
7. He is writing a letter. “My good opinion once lost is lost for ever.” They are 1813 forms and stay inside quotations.
8. E = 7/2. The cube is invented. The shrine of St. Priscus and St. Nicholas. Usually four; the number may be five or three. Official lengths remain reserved, and the lesson is not a diagnosis.
