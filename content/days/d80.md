- Five **metatarsals** run forward from the tarsus toward the toes, the counterpart of the metacarpals.
- The **phalanges** of the toes follow the same count already used in the hand: **two** in the **hallux** (the great toe), **three** in each of the other toes.

A sole-down, toes-forward view is enough; this lesson does not invent official lengths or a measured arch. No living, named person’s foot is required or described. The site holds no identifying personal information.

The reserved seven names will be taught later, from a named anatomy source, not guessed today. ### Answer key

**Check.**
1. A = b × h, with h perpendicular to the base. Trapezoid: ½(6 + 10) × 4 = 32.
2. The apothem is the perpendicular from the center of a regular polygon to the middle of a side. Area = ½ × 48 × 7.2 = 172.8, invented measurements.
3. Uterque: each of the two (often with a singular verb). The present participle names a same-time action and agrees with a noun; it is not the main verb.
4. Tuve, tuviste, tuvo, tuvimos, tuvisteis, tuvieron. Estuve is the preterite of estar; fui is the preterite of ser or ir.
5. T = 2π√(L/g), for amplitudes less than about 15°. T depends on length and on g, not on mass.
6. A saturated solution has the same concentration as one in equilibrium with undissolved solute at a specified temperature and pressure (Gold Book S05471). Solubility is the composition of that saturated solution (S05740), not the leftover solid itself.
7. Greyhound: “wisdom, and on love and virtue,” not earth or pelf; this course does not identify the Greyhound with a particular historical person. Or Dewing: the structure seems to float with no firm basis, yet actually it is braced with exceptional firmness and security.
8. Grisaille is monochrome value painting, in gray or in one hue. If paint is not ready, a grisaille rehearsal in drawing, named as a rehearsal, is enough.
