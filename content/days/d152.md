<p>Ēgredior already has a present indicative, a future ēgrediar, ēgrediēris, ēgrediētur, and the perfect tenses already taught. Today the present subjunctive: ēgrediar, ēgrediāris, ēgrediātur, ēgrediāmur, ēgrediāminī, ēgrediantur. As with sequor, the first-person singular is spelled the same as the future; the other persons split, ā against ē. It is not sequātur, yesterday’s present subjunctive of sequor; the two verbs must not be swapped. The sentences are original, not copied from Ørberg. Identity is handled with Puer and Puella, and with he and she in the gloss. A purpose clause with ut remains reserved.</p>

Table 20.1 gives representative official values; the official resistivity of tungsten in that table is 5.6 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m. That table value is official, not invented.
