## Hours per week

| Subject | Minutes per week | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Atelier (drawing and painting) | 450 | The largest block, every day: the arts are the centre of this school. |
| Mathematics | 300 | Daily. |
| Writing & Rhetoric | 225 | Daily. |
| Latin | 150 | Daily, short and frequent, which suits language learning. |
| Modern language | 125 | Daily. |
| Physics | 135 | Monday, Wednesday, Friday. |
| History | 135 | Monday, Wednesday, Friday. |
| Science (phenomenon modules) | 90 | Tuesday, Thursday. |
| Literature | 90 | Tuesday, Thursday, plus reading at lunch and in the evening. |
| Seminar rotation | 150 | Logic, music theory, art history, economics and personal finance, elective. |
| **Total** | **1,850** | About 6 hours 10 minutes of instruction a day. |

Literature reading (whole books) is done in free-reading time at lunch and in the evening: about 30 to 45 minutes a day.

## Rules of the timetable

- Keep the order. Mathematics and Latin come early, when attention is freshest; the long Atelier block comes after lunch, when sustained, quiet work suits.
- Start on time; stop on time. Unfinished exercises are finished the next morning before new work.
- Weekends have no lessons. Reading for pleasure and visits to museums are encouraged.
