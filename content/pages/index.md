# Atelier Academy

A complete, structured course of study for an advanced student with a serious commitment to the visual arts. Every subject is taught by the strongest method we could find for it, from wherever in the world it comes. There is direct instruction, worked examples, exercises and separate answer keys, and nothing is decorative.

## How the school day works

The language of instruction is English. Latin and Spanish are daily subjects.

1. **Retrieval first.** Each day opens with five minutes of writing from memory what was learned before. Recalling is what makes knowledge stick.
2. **Follow the timetable in order.** Each block lists its time, its method and its source text.
3. **Work in writing.** Every exercise is answered by hand in the subject notebook before the answer key is opened. Keys are on separate pages for this reason.
4. **Correct everything.** Mark your work against the key in a second color. Rework every miss from scratch the next day.
5. **Tick Done** only when the corrections are finished. Fridays end with cumulative checks; anything under 80% is re-taught before new material.

## Before Day 1

Complete the **[placement diagnostics](placement.html)** over two days. They decide where each subject starts. The first two weeks of lessons are written for the standard entry point; the placement page says what to change if you test above or below it.

## What is here now

Weeks 1–35 are written in full for every subject: one hundred and seventy-five school days, 1400 subject lessons plus a Friday vocabulary review in weeks 1–35, each lesson with exercises and a key. Week 12 is the Term 1 consolidation and term examinations; week 13 opens Term 2, weeks 14–23 continue it, week 24 is the Term 2 consolidation and term examinations, and weeks 25–35 continue Term 3. The rest of the year is laid out in the [scope and sequence](scope.html), and new weeks will be added in the same format. Sources for every method and fact are listed on the [sources page](sources.html).
