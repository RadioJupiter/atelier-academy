## Writing

1. Model: *The painting, damaged in a flood in 1966, was restored over many years.*
2. (a) Fragment: *The colors shifted because the light changed.* (b) Run-on (comma splice risk): *The cast was white; the shadows were blue.* (c) Dangling modifier: *As I was running to the studio, the easel fell over.*
3. **Rubric (1–4 each):** clear, arguable thesis · specific examples · other side addressed · paragraph structure · sentence control · mechanics.

**Routing.** Total 18–24: standard entry, but move faster through the sentence work (Days 1–3) and spend the saved time on the Franklin exercises. Total below 15 or errors in exercise 2: do every sentence activity in full and add one extra *because/but/so* set daily for two weeks.

## Atelier

Assess the four pieces together:
- **Advanced:** proportions within about 3%, an evenly stepped value scale, and an egg that reads as a solid with a distinct shadow family. Copy Bargue plates at a faster pace (one every two or three days), and start cast drawing in week 2.
- **Standard:** some proportional errors, a value scale with uneven steps, flatness in the egg. Follow the lessons as written.
- **Foundation:** difficulty with straight lines or with seeing shapes. Add 20 minutes of line and angle drills (Day 1 exercises) at the start of every Atelier block for the first four weeks.
The written paragraph guides electives and later projects (for example, whether to start oil or acrylic for the grisaille in week 3).
