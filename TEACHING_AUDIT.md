# Teaching audit

Audit of every written lesson in weeks 1–2 (ten days, 80 lessons), made before the explicit-instruction rewrite. A lesson **teaches** if it explains the idea, states the fact, rule or method, *and* gives a fully worked example. **Partial** means it explains or states a rule but has no fully worked example, or (for Latin) mainly sends the student to Ørberg’s book. **Assignment only** means it mainly sets exercises, a check, or a studio task.

## American standardised style in the mathematics

Weeks 1–2 mathematics is already in the Singapore / Kiselev / Gelfand spirit (bar models to equations, proof, identities), not elementary American “word form / expanded form / value of the digit” drills. The only “write in words” exercise in these two weeks is in the **Spanish** lesson on numbers (Day 4), where writing *siete, once, quince* is vocabulary, not a mathematics test item. No change of method is needed on that point; mathematics lessons still need explicit worked examples and a check before independent practice.

## After the rewrite (intended standard)

Every lesson in weeks 1–2 (all 80, including history, literature, science, and seminar) now follows Learn → Remember → Watch me → Together → Check → practice. Latin grammar and vocabulary are taught here with original examples (the book is extra reading, not the only teacher). Physics keeps Peer Instruction but places a short explanation and a worked example before the ConcepTests. Atelier lessons include a numbered demonstration. Listen-aloud is in English; only Spanish or Latin vocabulary clips use those languages. Each lesson has a Key terms card (Frayer model, plus a verified origin when one can be checked; origins are omitted rather than guessed). Together and Check require the student to explain or apply those terms, or to distinguish them from a near-miss. A glossary lists every term by subject, with a link back to the lesson; Friday of each week has a short spaced vocabulary review. Where a short review sheet is added, it is one page per lesson and only practices what that lesson taught.
